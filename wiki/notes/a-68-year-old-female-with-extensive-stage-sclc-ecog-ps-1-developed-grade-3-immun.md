Attempting to re-challenge at a lower dose is inappropriate and contraindicated for two primary reasons:

**1. Dose reductions are contraindicated for immunotherapy:** 
Unlike cytotoxic chemotherapy, immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) are not managed with dose reductions [[an-82-year-old-frail-patient-with-metastatic-clear-cell-rcc-and-an-ecog-ps-of-2-]]. Immune-related adverse events (irAEs) are driven by off-target T-cell activation, meaning that resuming the drug at a lower dose does not mitigate the underlying immune response and is completely ineffective for toxicity management [[an-82-year-old-frail-patient-with-metastatic-clear-cell-rcc-and-an-ecog-ps-of-2-]]. The FDA prescribing information for atezolizumab explicitly states that no dose reductions are recommended [search-sourced] [tecentriq-hcp.com](https://www.tecentriq-hcp.com/sclc/dosing-and-administration/dose-modifications.html).

**2. Grade 3 pneumonitis mandates permanent discontinuation:** 
Under ASCO and ESMO clinical guidelines, as well as the atezolizumab drug label, any Grade 3 or 4 immune-related pneumonitis is a severe, potentially life-threatening toxicity that requires immediate and permanent discontinuation of the offending immunotherapy [search-sourced] [fda.gov](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/761034s043lbl.pdf) [jadproce.com](https://jadproce.com/media/1266/managing-iraes.pdf) [mdcalc.com](https://www.mdcalc.com/calc/10383/immune-related-adverse-events-lung-toxicity-pneumonitis). Re-challenge after Grade 3 pneumonitis is not recommended [search-sourced] [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8523897/). 

**3. High risk of recurrence:** 
Even if her symptoms resolve to Grade 1 or less after a steroid taper, re-challenging a patient with a prior severe irAE carries significant risk. Real-world data across tumor types demonstrate an overall irAE recurrence rate of 28.8% upon re-challenge, with approximately 23% recurrence specifically for pneumonitis and colitis [[pembrolizumab-rechallenge-after-severe-irae]] [[tnbc-keynote-522-irae-rechallenge-2026]]. Furthermore, recurrent irAEs are often more severe than the initial event [[pembrolizumab-rechallenge-after-severe-irae]].
