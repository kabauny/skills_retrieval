# Which biomarkers should be tested in metastatic colorectal cancer to guide therapy?

> ✅ **Verified by jim.chen on 2026-06-07.** Auto-ingested from an internet search; reviewed and confirmed.

In metastatic colorectal cancer (mCRC), major clinical organizations—including the NCCN, ESMO, and ASCO—recommend comprehensive molecular profiling to personalize and guide treatment decisions [search-sourced]. Br [mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6694/18/6/1007)oad next-generation sequencing (NGS) panels of tumor tissue are preferred, though blood-based assays (liquid biopsies/ctDNA) are acceptable if tumor tissue is unavailable or difficult to biopsy [search-sourced]. 

 [mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6694/18/6/1007)At a minimum, standard biomarker testing for mCRC should evaluate the following [search-sourced]:

* [jnccn.org](https://jnccn.org/view/journals/jnccn/21/5.5/article-p567.xml)   **Microsatellite Instability (MSI) / Mismatch Repair (MMR) Status:** Testing for MSI-high (MSI-H) or mismatch repair-deficient (dMMR) status is critical [[what-is-the-first-line-treatment-for-metastatic-msi-high-colorectal-cancer]]. Because these tumors have a high probability of neoantigen formation, they are highly responsive to immunotherapy, which has replaced traditional chemotherapy as the optimal upfront approach for this subgroup [[what-is-the-first-line-treatment-for-metastatic-msi-high-colorectal-cancer]]. MSI-H/dMMR status guides the use of first-line immune checkpoint inhibitors such as pembrolizumab monotherapy or a combination of nivolumab and ipilimumab [[what-is-the-first-line-treatment-for-metastatic-msi-high-colorectal-cancer]]. Emerging data also shows that adding atezolizumab to standard FOLFOX and bevacizumab yields superior outcomes for these patients [[what-is-the-first-line-treatment-for-metastatic-msi-high-colorectal-cancer]].
*   **RAS Mutations (KRAS and NRAS):** Expanded mutational testing of KRAS and NRAS (specifically exons 2, 3, and 4) is a mandatory minimum standard of care [search-sourced]. RA [bluecrossnc.com](https://www.bluecrossnc.com/providers/policies-guidelines-codes/commercial/laboratory/updates/testing-for-colorectal-cancer-management) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9310231/)S mutations are negative predictive biomarkers; patients with these mutations do not benefit from anti-EGFR therapies [search-sourced]. On [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9310231/)ly patients with RAS wild-type tumors are considered candidates for anti-EGFR monoclonal antibodies like cetuximab or panitumumab [search-sourced].
*  [emjreviews.com](https://www.emjreviews.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Unmet-Medical-Need-in-Patients-with-Metastatic-Colorectal-Cancer-with-BRAF-V600E-Mutations-A-Review.pdf)  **BRAF V600E Mutation:** BRAF mutation testing is recommended alongside RAS testing [search-sourced]. Th [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9310231/)e V600E mutation acts as an important prognostic biomarker indicating poorer overall survival, and it is a predictive biomarker used to select patients who may benefit from BRAF-targeted inhibitor regimens [search-sourced].
*  [emjreviews.com](https://www.emjreviews.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Unmet-Medical-Need-in-Patients-with-Metastatic-Colorectal-Cancer-with-BRAF-V600E-Mutations-A-Review.pdf)  **HER2 (ERBB2) Expression/Amplification:** Testing for HER2 helps identify candidates who will benefit from HER2-targeted agents [search-sourced]. Fo [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12154287/) [jnccn.org](https://jnccn.org/view/journals/jnccn/21/5.5/article-p567.xml)r patients with HER2-amplified tumors, guideline-approved options include trastuzumab-deruxtecan or combinations of trastuzumab with tucatinib or pertuzumab [search-sourced].
*  [jnccn.org](https://jnccn.org/view/journals/jnccn/21/5.5/article-p567.xml)  **NTRK Fusions and RET Mutations:** Though rare in colorectal cancer, NTRK and RET alterations are highly actionable, and identifying them can qualify patients for specific tissue-agnostic targeted therapies [search-sourced].
