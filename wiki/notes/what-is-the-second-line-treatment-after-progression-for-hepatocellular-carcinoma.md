# What is the second-line treatment after progression for hepatocellular carcinoma?

> ✅ **Verified by jim.chen on 2026-06-08.** Auto-ingested from an internet search; reviewed and confirmed.

The choice of second-line treatment for advanced hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) after progression depends primarily on which systemic therapy was administered in the first-line setting [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8475251/) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8464222/). Recent updates to oncology guidelines (such as those from NCCN, ASCO, and ESMO) have expanded the available regimens [esmo.org](https://www.esmo.org/guidelines/living-guidelines/esmo-living-guideline-hepatocellular-carcinoma/management-of-advanced-hcc/management-of-advanced-hcc-second-line-treatment) [oncologynewscentral.com](https://www.oncologynewscentral.com/article/fast-pace-of-drug-development-prompts-new-liver-cancer-guidelines). 

**Following First-Line Immunotherapy**
The current preferred first-line regimens for most patients with advanced HCC are immunotherapy combinations (atezolizumab plus bevacizumab, or durvalumab plus tremelimumab) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8475251/) [oncologynewscentral.com](https://www.oncologynewscentral.com/article/fast-pace-of-drug-development-prompts-new-liver-cancer-guidelines). If a patient experiences disease progression on these therapies, the recommended second-line options generally consist of tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs) [vjoncology.com](https://www.vjoncology.com/video/0qlub3_gjts-second-line-treatment-patterns-after-first-line-immunotherapy-in-hcc/) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8475251/), including:
* Sorafenib [esmo.org](https://www.esmo.org/guidelines/living-guidelines/esmo-living-guideline-hepatocellular-carcinoma/management-of-advanced-hcc/management-of-advanced-hcc-second-line-treatment)
* Lenvatinib [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12100525/) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8475251/)
* Cabozantinib [nccn.org](https://www.nccn.org/patients/guidelines/content/PDF/liver-hp-patient.pdf)
* Regorafenib [nccn.org](https://www.nccn.org/patients/guidelines/content/PDF/liver-hp-patient.pdf)

**Following First-Line Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors (TKIs)**
For patients who cannot receive immunotherapy in the first line (e.g., due to autoimmune contraindications, specific bleeding risks, or severe varices) and instead receive sorafenib or lenvatinib, the following second-line options are recommended [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8475251/) [oncologynewscentral.com](https://www.oncologynewscentral.com/article/fast-pace-of-drug-development-prompts-new-liver-cancer-guidelines) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8464222/):
* **Regorafenib:** For patients who previously tolerated sorafenib [gastroenterologyandhepatology.net](https://www.gastroenterologyandhepatology.net/archives/may-2025/looking-toward-the-future-emerging-therapies-for-hepatocellular-carcinoma/) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8464222/).
* **Cabozantinib:** Broadly approved for patients who have previously been treated with sorafenib [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8464222/).
* **Ramucirumab:** Specifically indicated for patients who have an alpha-fetoprotein (AFP) level of ≥400 ng/mL.
 [ascopubs.org](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.2_suppl.544) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8464222/)* **Immunotherapy (Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors):** Nivolumab (with or without ipilimumab) or pembrolizumab may be used in the second line for patients who progressed on a TKI, particularly if they have not yet received an immune checkpoint inhibitor.  [sitcancer.org](https://www.sitcancer.org/publications/cpg/liver) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12100525/) [oncologynewscentral.com](https://www.oncologynewscentral.com/article/fast-pace-of-drug-development-prompts-new-liver-cancer-guidelines)Pembrolizumab is also explicitly indicated for patients with MSI-H/dMMR tumors.
 [cabometyxhcp.com](https://www.cabometyxhcp.com/assets/pdfs/CABOMETYX-HCC_FlashCard.pdf)
**Patient Selection**
In all cases, moving to a second-line therapy requires that the patient maintains adequate liver function (typically Child-Pugh Class A or well-preserved function) and a good performance status (ECOG 0-1).
 [esmo.org](https://www.esmo.org/guidelines/living-guidelines/esmo-living-guideline-hepatocellular-carcinoma/management-of-advanced-hcc/management-of-advanced-hcc-second-line-treatment) [oncologynewscentral.com](https://www.oncologynewscentral.com/article/fast-pace-of-drug-development-prompts-new-liver-cancer-guidelines)
