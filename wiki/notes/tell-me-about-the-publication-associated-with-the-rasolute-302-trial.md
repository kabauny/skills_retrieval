# Tell me about the publication associated with the RASolute 302 trial

> ✅ **Verified by jim.chen on 2026-06-07.** Auto-ingested from an internet search; reviewed and confirmed.

The **RASolute 302** trial (ClinicalTrials.gov identifier NCT06625320) is a global, Phase 3, multicenter, open-label randomized study [clinicaltrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06625320) [ascopubs.org](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2025.43.16_suppl.TPS4230). 

**Trial Overview**
The trial was designed to evaluate the safety and efficacy of **daraxonrasib** (also known as RMC-6236, developed by Revolution Medicines), an investigational oral RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor, compared to the investigator's choice of standard-of-care chemotherapy [clinicaltrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06625320) [ascopubs.org](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2025.43.16_suppl.TPS4230). The study focuses on patients with previously treated metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (mPDAC) [clinicaltrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06625320) [nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42223072/). 

**Publication and Presentation Details**
The results of the trial were presented by lead investigator Dr. Brian Wolpin of the Dana-Farber Cancer Institute during the Plenary Session at the **2026 American Society of Clinical Oncology (ASCO) Annual Meeting** [dana-farber.org](https://www.dana-farber.org/newsroom/news-releases/2026/rason-inhibitor-doubles-median-overall-survival-in-results-of-phase-3-trial-for-patients-with-metastatic-pancreatic-cancer) [asco.org](https://www.asco.org/about-asco/press-center/multi-selective-ras-inhibitor-nearly-doubles-survival-pancreatic-cancer) [ajmc.com](https://www.ajmc.com/view/rasolute-302-brings-a-transformative-moment-on-pancreatic-cancer-a-60-improvement-in-overall-survival). Simultaneously, the landmark findings were published in the ***New England Journal of Medicine* (NEJM)** [ajmc.com](https://www.ajmc.com/view/rasolute-302-brings-a-transformative-moment-on-pancreatic-cancer-a-60-improvement-in-overall-survival) [nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42223072/). 

**Key Findings Published**
The publications highlighted "landscape-changing" results for this difficult-to-treat cancer, meeting all primary and key secondary endpoints [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/rasolute-302-new-treatment-paradigm-metastatic-pdac-oncogenic-ras-targeting) [asco.org](https://www.asco.org/practice-patients/patient-resources/research-summaries/RASolute-302-pancreatic-cancer):
*   **Overall Survival (OS):** Daraxonrasib effectively doubled the median overall survival compared to standard chemotherapy [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/rasolute-302-new-treatment-paradigm-metastatic-pdac-oncogenic-ras-targeting) [ajmc.com](https://www.ajmc.com/view/rasolute-302-brings-a-transformative-moment-on-pancreatic-cancer-a-60-improvement-in-overall-survival). For the patient population harboring a RAS G12 mutation, median OS reached 13.2 months with daraxonrasib versus 6.6–6.7 months with chemotherapy (Hazard Ratio [HR] = 0.40).  [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/rasolute-302-new-treatment-paradigm-metastatic-pdac-oncogenic-ras-targeting) [nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42223072/)
*   **Progression-Free Survival (PFS):** The trial also saw PFS more than double, reaching 7.3 months for the targeted agent compared to 3.5 months for the standard chemotherapy arm.
 [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/rasolute-302-new-treatment-paradigm-metastatic-pdac-oncogenic-ras-targeting)*   **Safety & Quality of Life:** The publication noted that daraxonrasib offered a more favorable safety profile with fewer side effects than current chemotherapies and significantly delayed the deterioration of patient-reported outcomes.
