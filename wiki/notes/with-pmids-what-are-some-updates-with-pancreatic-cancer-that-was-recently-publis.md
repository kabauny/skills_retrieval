*   **Targeting KRAS Alterations (PMID: 41701177):** Following presentations at the ASCO Gastrointestinal Cancers Symposium, a recent 2026 publication evaluated MEK inhibitor-based genomically matched combinatorial targeted therapies. The study assessed the efficacy of these targeted combinations in patients with metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma harboring specific *KRAS* alterations (G12R, G12D, and G12V) [search-sourced].
*   **RAS(ON) Inhibitors:** At the ASCO 2026 Annual Meeting, the Phase 3 RASolute 302 trial received high profile attention (and was simultaneously published in the *New England Journal of Medicine*) for the drug daraxonrasib. *   **Tumor Treating Fields (TTFields):** Highlighted recently around ASCO, the Phase 3 PANOVA-3 study demonstrated a statistically significant overall survival benefit when TTFields (electric fields that disrupt cancer cell division) were added to first-line gemcitabine and nab-paclitaxel for patients with locally advanced pancreatic cancer [search-sourced].
*   **Immunotherapy and Lymphopenia:** At ASCO 2025, data from the QUILT-88 trial showed that using ANKTIVA (an IL-15 superagonist) alongside CAR-NK therapy provided a landmark overall survival benefit for 3rd-to-6th line metastatic pancreatic cancer patients. The therapy successfully reversed treatment-induced lymphopenia, correlating improved lymphocyte counts directly with improved survival [search-sourced].

*   **Metastatic Pancreatic Cancer (PMID: 32755482):** ASCO updated its guidelines for metastatic pancreatic cancer to integrate routine germline and somatic testing. The update strongly recommends testing for microsatellite instability (MSI-H/dMMR), *BRCA* mutations, and *NTRK* gene fusions to identify candidates for targeted therapies such as pembrolizumab, olaparib, and TRK inhibitors [search-sourced].
