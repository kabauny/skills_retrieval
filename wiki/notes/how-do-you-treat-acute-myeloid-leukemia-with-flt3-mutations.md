# How do you treat acute myeloid leukemia with FLT3 mutations?

> ✅ **Verified by jim.chen on 2026-06-07.** Auto-ingested from an internet search; reviewed and confirmed.

The treatment of acute myeloid leukemia (AML) with FLT3 mutations has evolved significantly with the introduction of targeted therapies [search-sourced] [jons-online.com](https://www.jons-online.com/special-issues-and-supplements/2019/2019-fourth-annual-oncology-guide-to-new-fda-approvals/xospata-gilteritinib-first-drug-approved-as-monotherapy-for-adults-with-relapsed-or-refractory-acute-myeloid-leukemia-with-flt3-mutation). FLT3 mutations primarily consist of internal tandem duplications (FLT3-ITD) or tyrosine kinase domain mutations (FLT3-TKD) [search-sourced] [cancernetwork.com](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/treatment-of-patients-with-flt3-itd-acute-myeloid-leukemia-in-three-phases). Because mutation status can evolve or emerge over time, clinical guidelines strongly recommend molecular testing for FLT3 mutations at initial diagnosis and at each disease relapse or progression [search-sourced] [xospatahcp.com](https://www.xospatahcp.com/about/flt-mutations-in-aml) [vjhemonc.com](https://www.vjhemonc.com/video/ia60g9awimg-the-2021-treatment-algorithm-for-flt3-mutated-aml/).

Depending on patient fitness, exact mutation subtype, and disease stage, treatment incorporates specific FDA-approved FLT3 inhibitors [search-sourced] [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12640675/) [cancernetwork.com](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/treatment-of-patients-with-flt3-itd-acute-myeloid-leukemia-in-three-phases). 

### 1. Frontline Therapy for Fit / Newly Diagnosed Patients
For younger patients or those fit for intensive therapy, standard intensive induction chemotherapy (e.g., "7+3" cytarabine and an anthracycline) and consolidation are combined with a targeted FLT3 inhibitor [search-sourced] [vjhemonc.com](https://www.vjhemonc.com/video/xyy2bmuktie-the-role-of-flt3-and-idh-inhibitors-in-newly-diagnosed-older-adults-with-aml-based-on-ash-guidelines/):
*   **Midostaurin:** FDA-approved for use alongside standard induction and consolidation chemotherapy in newly diagnosed patients with any FLT3 mutation (ITD or TKD) [search-sourced] [ashpublications.org](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/129/26/3403/36251/Midostaurin-approved-for-FLT3-mutated-AML).
*   **Quizartinib:** FDA-approved in 2023 specifically for newly diagnosed patients with the FLT3-ITD mutation. It is combined with standard induction and consolidation chemotherapy, and can subsequently be used as maintenance monotherapy [search-sourced] [fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/fda-approves-quizartinib-newly-diagnosed-acute-myeloid-leukemia) [cancer.gov](https://www.cancer.gov/news-events/cancer-currents-blog/2023/fda-vanflyta-aml-flt3).
*   **Stem Cell Transplant:** Following remission, eligible patients frequently proceed to an allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (alloSCT) to achieve the best long-term outcomes [search-sourced] [vjhemonc.com](https://www.vjhemonc.com/video/ia60g9awimg-the-2021-treatment-algorithm-for-flt3-mutated-aml/) [vjhemonc.com](https://www.vjhemonc.com/video/pwy6el6dqwi-approaches-to-managing-flt3-mutant-aml/).

### 2. Frontline Therapy for Older or Unfit Patients
Older patients or those with comorbidities who are ineligible for intensive induction chemotherapy typically receive lower-intensity regimens [search-sourced] [vjhemonc.com](https://www.vjhemonc.com/video/xyy2bmuktie-the-role-of-flt3-and-idh-inhibitors-in-newly-diagnosed-older-adults-with-aml-based-on-ash-guidelines/).
*   The baseline standard of care is generally a hypomethylating agent (HMA) combined with venetoclax [search-sourced] [ashpublications.org](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/146/Supplement%201/7955/555027/FLT3-testing-and-guideline-concordance-in-Acute). 
*   However, emerging data and retrospective analyses show that a "triplet" therapy regimen—combining an HMA, venetoclax, and a FLT3 inhibitor—yields excellent long-term overall survival outcomes and is heavily utilized in current practice [search-sourced].
