# What is the efficacy profile of sunvozertinib, including objective response rate, progression-free survival, and overall

> ✅ **Verified by jim.chen on 2026-06-08.** Auto-ingested from an internet search; reviewed and confirmed.

Based on the latest clinical data, sunvozertinib (an oral EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor) has demonstrated significant efficacy in patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) harboring epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) exon 20 insertion mutations [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/first-line-sunvozertinib-superior-efficacy-over-chemo-advanced-nsclc-egfr-exon-20) [nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40618881/). 

Its efficacy profile—comprising Objective Response Rate (ORR), Progression-Free Survival (PFS), and Overall Survival (OS)—varies slightly depending on whether it is used in the first-line (treatment-naïve) or previously treated settings.

**First-Line Setting (Treatment-Naïve)**
Recent results from the randomized phase 3 WU-KONG28 trial (presented at ASCO 2026) demonstrated that first-line sunvozertinib was significantly superior to the standard of care (platinum-based chemotherapy):
*    [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/first-line-sunvozertinib-superior-efficacy-over-chemo-advanced-nsclc-egfr-exon-20) [asco.org](https://www.asco.org/abstracts-presentations/259326) [mdanderson.org](https://www.mdanderson.org/newsroom/research-newsroom/targeted-drug-outperforms-chemotherapy-for-patients-with-hard-to-treat-lung-cancer.h00-159855345.html)**PFS:** Sunvozertinib achieved a median PFS of 10.3 months, compared to 7.5 months for chemotherapy (Hazard Ratio: 0.65).
*    [asco.org](https://www.asco.org/abstracts-presentations/259326) [oncoprescribe.com](https://www.oncoprescribe.com/late-breaking-abstract-asco-2026-sunvozertinib-demonstrates-superior-first-line-efficacy-in-egfr-exon-20-insertion-positive-advanced-nsclc/)**ORR:** The objective response rate was 58.9% with sunvozertinib versus 31.1% with chemotherapy.
*    [mdanderson.org](https://www.mdanderson.org/newsroom/research-newsroom/targeted-drug-outperforms-chemotherapy-for-patients-with-hard-to-treat-lung-cancer.h00-159855345.html)**OS:** Overall survival data for the phase 3 trial are still immature/pending. 

In [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/first-line-sunvozertinib-superior-efficacy-over-chemo-advanced-nsclc-egfr-exon-20) [mdanderson.org](https://www.mdanderson.org/newsroom/research-newsroom/targeted-drug-outperforms-chemotherapy-for-patients-with-hard-to-treat-lung-cancer.h00-159855345.html) an earlier phase 2 study for first-line patients (WU-KONG15), sunvozertinib exhibited a confirmed ORR of 73.1%, a median PFS of 10.1 months, and an estimated median OS of 23.1 months.

**P [nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40618881/)reviously Treated Setting (Post-Chemotherapy)**
Sunvozertinib received FDA accelerated approval in July 2025 for adult patients with advanced or metastatic EGFR exon 20 insertion-mutated NSCLC whose disease progressed after platinum-based chemotherapy. This [fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-sunvozertinib-metastatic-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-egfr-exon-20) was based primarily on the pivotal phase 2 WU-KONG1B trial:
*   **ORR:** At the recommended 200 mg daily dose, the confirmed ORR was roughly 46%.
*    [cancernetwork.com](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/sunvozertinib-exhibits-favorable-responses-in-egfr-mutated-nsclc) [fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-sunvozertinib-metastatic-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-egfr-exon-20)**PFS:** The estimated median progression-free survival was 8.4 months.
*    [cancernetwork.com](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/sunvozertinib-exhibits-favorable-responses-in-egfr-mutated-nsclc)**OS:** The 12-month overall survival rate was reported at 62.1%.
