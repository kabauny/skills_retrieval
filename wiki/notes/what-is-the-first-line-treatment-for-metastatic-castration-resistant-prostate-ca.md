# What is the first-line treatment for metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer?

> ✅ **Verified by jim.chen on 2026-06-07.** Auto-ingested from an internet search; reviewed and confirmed.

For patients with metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC), the foundational step in first-line treatment is the continuation of **androgen deprivation therapy (ADT)** to maintain serum testosterone at castrate levels (< 50 ng/dL) [urotoday.com](https://www.urotoday.com/library-resources/mcrpc-treatment/114581-first-line-treatment-for-metastatic-castrate-resistant-prostate-cancer.html) [jnccn.org](https://jnccn.org/abstract/journals/jnccn/23/11/article-p469.xml). 

Beyond continuing ADT, the choice of first-line systemic therapy depends on the patient's symptoms, sites of metastasis, prior treatments (such as whether they received certain therapies in the castration-sensitive setting), and specific biomarkers [jnccn.org](https://jnccn.org/abstract/journals/jnccn/23/11/article-p469.xml). The primary first-line additions to ADT include:

**1. Androgen Receptor Pathway Inhibitors (ARPIs)**
Novel hormonal therapies are frequently favored as first-line options due to their favorable safety and side-effect profiles compared to chemotherapy [amegroups.org](https://tau.amegroups.org/article/view/66843/html). Standard choices include:
*   **Abiraterone acetate**, administered with oral prednisone [tri-kobe.org](https://www2.tri-kobe.org/nccn/guideline/archive/urological2016-2017/english/prostate.pdf).
*   **Enzalutamide** [frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/oncology/articles/10.3389/fonc.2024.1378993/full) [tri-kobe.org](https://www2.tri-kobe.org/nccn/guideline/archive/urological2016-2017/english/prostate.pdf). 

**2. Chemotherapy**
Taxane-based chemotherapy is a mainstay for first-line treatment, particularly for patients with rapidly progressing or highly symptomatic disease:
*   **Docetaxel** (given with prednisone) is a preferred first-line treatment [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5785604/) [frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/oncology/articles/10.3389/fonc.2024.1378993/full) [amegroups.org](https://tau.amegroups.org/article/view/66843/html). 
*   **Cabazitaxel** is also utilized and supported by network meta-analyses as a highly effective first-line or subsequent option [frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/oncology/articles/10.3389/fonc.2024.1378993/full).

**3. Immunotherapy**
*   **Sipuleucel-T**, an autologous cellular immunotherapy, is recommended for first-line treatment in patients who are asymptomatic or minimally symptomatic, have no liver metastases, and have an estimated life expectancy of greater than 6 months [onclive.com](https://www.onclive.com/view/nccn-guidelines-for-advanced-prostate-cancer).

**4. Radiopharmaceuticals**
*   **Radium-223** may be offered as a first-line option for patients who have symptomatic bone metastases and no known visceral metastases [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5785604/) [frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/oncology/articles/10.3389/fonc.2024.1378993/full). 

**5. Targeted Therapies (PARP Inhibitors)**
*   Newer guidelines have incorporated the use of **PARP inhibitors** (such as talazoparib combined with enzalutamide) in the first-line setting for mCRPC patients, particularly those harboring DNA repair pathway alterations (e.g., BRCA1/BRCA2 mutations) [ascopubs.org](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO-25-02693) [ascopost.com](https://ascopost.com/issues/november-10-2022/asco-treatment-guidance-for-metastatic-castration-resistant-prostate-cancer-updated-to-include-lupsma/). 

*(Note: The targeted radioligand Lutetium-177-PSMA-617 is typically reserved for patients who have already been treated with at least one prior ARPI and one prior line of taxane chemotherapy, rather than as a first-line treatment [ascopost.com](https://ascopost.com/issues/november-10-2022/asco-treatment-guidance-for-metastatic-castration-resistant-prostate-cancer-updated-to-include-lupsma/) [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/asco-treatment-guidance-mcrpc-updated-include-177-lutetium-psma-617).)*

Optimal treatment selection is guided by shared decision-making, weighing the complementary mechanisms of action, patient comorbidities, and potential toxicities [urotoday.com](https://www.urotoday.com/library-resources/mcrpc-treatment.html) [jnccn.org](https://jnccn.org/abstract/journals/jnccn/23/11/article-p469.xml).
