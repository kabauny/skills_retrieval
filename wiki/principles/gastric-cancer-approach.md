# Approach to Gastric cancer

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Gastric cancer
Stage and assess resectability (perioperative chemo for resectable; palliative systemic for metastatic). Test HER2, PD-L1 CPS, MSI, CLDN18.2 up front — each opens a distinct first-line combination.
