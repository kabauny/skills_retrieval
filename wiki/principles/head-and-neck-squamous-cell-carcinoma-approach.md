# Approach to Head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC)

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC)
Stage and define HPV status. Curative-intent surgery or chemoradiation for localized disease; recurrent/metastatic disease uses immunotherapy by PD-L1 CPS +/- chemo.
