# Approach to Endometrial cancer

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Endometrial cancer
Stage surgically. Use molecular classification (MMR/MSI, p53, POLE) for prognosis and to select immunotherapy; advanced disease increasingly uses chemo + IO.
