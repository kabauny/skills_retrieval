# Approach to Follicular lymphoma (FL)

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Follicular lymphoma (FL)
Confirm grade and rule out transformation. Asymptomatic low-burden disease often warrants observation; treat by symptoms/burden, then sequence anti-CD20 combinations, lenalidomide, and bispecifics on relapse.
