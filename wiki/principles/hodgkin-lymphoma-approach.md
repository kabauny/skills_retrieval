# Approach to Hodgkin lymphoma

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Hodgkin lymphoma
Stage and risk-stratify; use PET-adapted therapy to balance cure against late toxicity. Brentuximab vedotin and checkpoint inhibitors anchor advanced and relapsed disease.
