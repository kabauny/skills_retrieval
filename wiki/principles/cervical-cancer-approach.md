# Approach to Cervical cancer

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Cervical cancer
Stage to choose curative chemoradiation vs surgery for localized disease. For advanced/recurrent disease, chemo + bevacizumab + immunotherapy by PD-L1 status.
