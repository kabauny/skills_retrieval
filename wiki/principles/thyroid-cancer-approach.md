# Approach to Thyroid cancer

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Thyroid cancer
Surgery +/- radioactive iodine for differentiated disease. In RAI-refractory or medullary/anaplastic disease, NGS drives RET/BRAF/NTRK-targeted therapy or multikinase inhibitors.
