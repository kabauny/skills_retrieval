# Approach to Esophageal cancer

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Esophageal cancer
Stage and assess resectability; trimodality (neoadjuvant chemoradiation + surgery) for locally advanced. Histology and HER2/PD-L1 status guide systemic and adjuvant immunotherapy choices.
