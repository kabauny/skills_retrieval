# Approach to Acute myeloid leukemia (AML)

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Acute myeloid leukemia (AML)
Stratify by cytogenetics/molecular markers and fitness urgently. Fit patients: intensive induction +/- targeted add-ons (FLT3, IDH); unfit: hypomethylating agent + venetoclax. Plan transplant by risk.
