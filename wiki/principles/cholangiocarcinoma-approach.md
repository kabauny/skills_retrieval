# Approach to Cholangiocarcinoma

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Cholangiocarcinoma
Stage and assess resectability. Obtain NGS early — FGFR2 fusions, IDH1, HER2 each unlock targeted therapy. Gemcitabine/cisplatin + immunotherapy is the systemic backbone.
