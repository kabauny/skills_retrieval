# Approach to Chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL)

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL)
Confirm diagnosis and test del(17p)/TP53 and IGHV before treating. Observe asymptomatic disease; at indication, choose BTK inhibitors or venetoclax-based therapy guided by TP53 status and patient factors.
