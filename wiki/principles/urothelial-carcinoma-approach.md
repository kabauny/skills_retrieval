# Approach to Urothelial carcinoma

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Urothelial carcinoma
Distinguish non-muscle-invasive, muscle-invasive (curative-intent: neoadjuvant chemo + cystectomy), and metastatic. Assess cisplatin eligibility; test FGFR and HER2 for later-line targeted/ADC therapy.
