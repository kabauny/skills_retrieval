# Approach to Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC)

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC)
Stage with BCLC integrating tumor burden, liver function, and performance status. Curative options (resection, ablation, transplant) for early disease; systemic IO-based therapy for advanced — gated by adequate liver function.
