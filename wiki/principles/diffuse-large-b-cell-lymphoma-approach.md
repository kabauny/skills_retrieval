# Approach to Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL)

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL)
Stage and assess fitness. Frontline anti-CD20 immunochemotherapy with curative intent; relapsed/refractory disease is risk-stratified toward CAR-T and bispecifics. Manage CRS/ICANS toxicity actively.
