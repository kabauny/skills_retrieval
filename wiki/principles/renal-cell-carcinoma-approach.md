# Approach to Renal cell carcinoma (RCC)

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Renal cell carcinoma (RCC)
Stage; localized disease is often cured surgically. For advanced disease, stratify by IMDC risk and histology, then select IO-IO or IO-TKI combinations; weigh toxicity against benefit.
