# Approach to Melanoma

> **Principle node (disease framework).** How to approach this disease before choosing therapy — the order of operations, pointing to the lenses.

## How to approach Melanoma
Stage; resect localized disease with adjuvant therapy for high risk. For advanced disease, choose immunotherapy vs BRAF/MEK-targeted therapy by BRAF status, weighing toxicity and pace of disease.
