# Disease-free survival


## Brief

A common clinical endpoint in cancer trials, measuring the length of time after treatment during which a patient stays free of disease, including recurrence, progression, or death from the disease.

## Why this matters

DFS is a critical endpoint in oncology trials used to evaluate the efficacy of new treatments in preventing disease recurrence or progression.
