# Stupp protocol


## Brief

A standard treatment protocol for newly diagnosed glioblastoma, involving initial concurrent chemoradiotherapy followed by adjuvant/maintenance temozolomide.

## Why this matters

It defines the standard of care for glioblastoma treatment.
