# Gefitinib

## Overview
Gefitinib (Iressa) is an oral, reversible epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI). It is approved for first-line treatment of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) bearing sensitizing EGFR mutations (exon 19 deletions or exon 21 L858R). It was one of the first targeted agents to validate the EGFR-mutation paradigm in lung cancer.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: reversibly inhibits the EGFR tyrosine kinase, blocking downstream proliferation/survival signaling driven by activating EGFR mutations such as exon 19 deletions and exon 21 L858R [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/206995s003lbl.pdf).
- Principal FDA indication: first-line treatment of metastatic NSCLC whose tumors harbor EGFR exon 19 deletions or exon 21 (L858R) substitution mutations as detected by an FDA-approved test; approved July 2015 at 250 mg orally once daily [FDA approval review](https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/22/6/1307/121642/FDA-Approval-of-Gefitinib-for-the-Treatment-of).
- US approval was supported by a single-arm cohort of 106 treatment-naive EGFR-mutation-positive patients with a blinded independent central review objective response rate of 50% and median duration of response of 6.0 months [FDA approval review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26980062/).
- Pivotal IPASS trial: in the EGFR-mutation-positive subgroup (n=261), gefitinib roughly doubled progression-free survival vs carboplatin-paclitaxel (PFS HR 0.48, 95% CI 0.36-0.64, P<0.001) [IPASS](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa0810699).
- IPASS also showed harm in EGFR-mutation-negative patients (PFS HR 2.85 favoring chemotherapy), establishing mutation testing as a prerequisite for benefit [IPASS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20573926/).
- Serious adverse events: interstitial lung disease (1.3% of 2462 patients; 0.7% Grade ≥3, with fatal cases), hepatotoxicity (Grade ≥3 ALT 5.1%, fatal hepatotoxicity 0.04%), and gastrointestinal perforation [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/206995s003lbl.pdf).
- Common adverse reactions include diarrhea and skin reactions (rash, acne, dry skin, pruritus); dose is withheld for Grade ≥3 diarrhea [Iressa prescribing information](https://www.drugs.com/pro/iressa.html).
