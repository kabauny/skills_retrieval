# osimertinib

## Overview

Osimertinib (Tagrisso) is a third-generation, irreversible oral EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI) that selectively targets both sensitizing EGFR mutations and the T790M resistance mutation while largely sparing wild-type EGFR. It is a standard-of-care therapy across the spectrum of EGFR-mutated non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), spanning adjuvant treatment of resected disease through first-line metastatic disease. Its CNS penetration and durable disease control have made it the preferred first-line EGFR-TKI in this population.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: irreversible third-generation EGFR-TKI that binds the cysteine-797 residue in the ATP-binding site, inhibiting both common sensitizing mutations (exon 19 deletion, L858R) and the T790M resistance mutation with relative sparing of wild-type EGFR [FDA/LiverTox](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK548495/).
- Indications: first-line treatment of metastatic NSCLC with EGFR exon 19 deletions or L858R mutations; adjuvant treatment after resection of EGFR-mutated NSCLC; treatment of EGFR T790M-positive NSCLC after progression on a prior EGFR-TKI; and, in combination with platinum-based chemotherapy, first-line locally advanced/metastatic EGFR-mutated NSCLC [FDA approval (Cancer Network)](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/fda-approves-osimertinib-chemo-in-egfr-mutated-nsclc).
- FLAURA (first-line, vs older EGFR-TKI): improved progression-free survival (median 18.9 vs 10.2 mo, HR 0.46) and overall survival (HR 0.799, 95.05% CI 0.641–0.997; 3-yr OS 54% vs 44%) [FLAURA OS, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1913662).
- ADAURA (adjuvant, resected stage IB–IIIA, vs placebo): disease-free survival HR 0.20 (stage IB–IIIA) and 0.17 (stage II–IIIA) in the primary analysis, with overall survival HR 0.49 (5-yr OS 85% vs 73% in stage II–IIIA) [ADAURA OS, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2304594).
- FLAURA2 (osimertinib + platinum/pemetrexed chemotherapy first-line): improved PFS and overall survival versus osimertinib monotherapy, supporting the chemotherapy-combination approval [FLAURA2, IASLC](https://www.iaslc.org/iaslc-news/press-release/flaura2-trial-shows-osimertinib-plus-chemotherapy-improves-overall).
- Common adverse events (≥20%): diarrhea (41%), rash (34%), dry skin (23%), nail toxicity (22%), and fatigue (22%) [FDA label / RxList](https://www.rxlist.com/tagrisso-side-effects-drug-center.htm).
- Serious adverse events: interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis (3.5%, fatal in 0.6%), cardiomyopathy/reduced LVEF (1.9%, rarely fatal), QTc prolongation, and embryo-fetal toxicity; ECG/electrolyte and LVEF monitoring is recommended in at-risk patients [FDA label / RxList](https://www.rxlist.com/tagrisso-side-effects-drug-center.htm).
