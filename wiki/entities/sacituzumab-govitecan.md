# Sacituzumab govitecan

## Overview
Sacituzumab govitecan (Trodelvy) is a Trop-2-directed antibody-drug conjugate in which a humanized anti-Trop-2 monoclonal antibody is linked via a hydrolysable linker to SN-38, the active topoisomerase I-inhibitor metabolite of irinotecan. It is FDA-approved for metastatic triple-negative breast cancer, pretreated HR+/HER2- metastatic breast cancer, and (accelerated approval) previously treated locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma. Its development was driven by the phase 3 ASCENT and TROPiCS-02 trials and the phase 2 TROPHY-U-01 study.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: anti-Trop-2 monoclonal antibody conjugated to the topoisomerase I inhibitor SN-38; binds Trop-2 (highly expressed on >90% of breast and many epithelial tumors), internalizes, and releases SN-38 to cause DNA strand breaks and apoptosis [ASCENT/NEJM](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8504932/).
- Indication 1 — metastatic triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) after ≥2 prior systemic therapies (≥1 for metastatic disease), the original April 2020 FDA approval [ASCENT review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8504932/).
- Pivotal trial ASCENT (phase 3, n=468, relapsed/refractory TNBC): median OS 12.1 vs 6.7 mo (HR 0.48, p<0.001), median PFS 5.6 vs 1.7 mo (HR 0.41), ORR 35% vs 5% versus single-agent chemotherapy [ASCENT review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8504932/).
- Indication 2 — pretreated HR+/HER2- metastatic breast cancer (after endocrine therapy, a CDK4/6 inhibitor, and 2-4 prior chemotherapy lines), supported by TROPiCS-02 (phase 3, n=543): median OS 14.4 vs 11.2 mo and a 34% reduction in risk of progression or death versus chemotherapy [Dana-Farber TROPiCS-02](https://www.dana-farber.org/newsroom/news-releases/2023/final-overall-study-analysis-continues-to-show-benefit-of-sacituzumab-govitecan-in-advanced-hr-breast-cancer).
- Indication 3 — locally advanced/metastatic urothelial carcinoma after platinum-based chemotherapy and a checkpoint inhibitor (accelerated approval), based on TROPHY-U-01 cohort 1 (phase 2, n=113): ORR 23% (5.3% complete responses) [TROPHY-U-01](https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(24)00009-7/fulltext).
- Serious adverse events: BOXED WARNING for severe neutropenia (life-threatening neutropenia ~9%) and severe diarrhea; grade ≥3 neutropenia (~46% pooled) and diarrhea (~11%) are the principal toxicities, with nausea, fatigue, anemia, and alopecia also common [Trodelvy safety information](https://www.trodelvyhcp.com/important-safety-information).
