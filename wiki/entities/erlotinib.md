# erlotinib

## Overview

Erlotinib (Tarceva) is an oral, reversible small-molecule tyrosine kinase inhibitor of the epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR). It is FDA-approved as first-line, maintenance, or later-line therapy for metastatic NSCLC harboring EGFR exon 19 deletions or exon 21 L858R substitutions, and in combination with gemcitabine for locally advanced, unresectable, or metastatic pancreatic cancer. In EGFR-mutant NSCLC it markedly outperforms chemotherapy, while its pancreatic benefit is statistically significant but modest.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: reversibly inhibits intracellular tyrosine kinase phosphorylation of EGFR (HER1/ErbB1), blocking downstream proliferation, angiogenesis, and survival signaling [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2016/021743s025lbl.pdf).
- NSCLC indication: metastatic NSCLC with EGFR exon 19 deletions or exon 21 L858R mutations (first-line, maintenance, or after progression on chemotherapy), dosed 150 mg/day [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2016/021743s025lbl.pdf).
- Pancreatic indication: first-line locally advanced/unresectable or metastatic pancreatic cancer in combination with gemcitabine, dosed 100 mg/day [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2016/021743s025lbl.pdf).
- Pivotal NSCLC trial — EURTAC (phase 3, EGFR-mutant, first-line): median PFS 9.7 vs 5.2 months for chemotherapy, HR 0.37 (95% CI 0.25–0.54; p<0.0001) [EURTAC](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS1470-2045(11)70393-X/abstract).
- Pivotal pancreatic trial — NCIC CTG PA.3 (phase 3, gemcitabine ± erlotinib): OS HR 0.82 (95% CI 0.69–0.99; p=0.038), median OS 6.24 vs 5.91 months; 1-year survival 23% vs 17% [PA.3](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2006.07.9525).
- Most common adverse reactions: rash (~70%) and diarrhea (~42%), typically onset in the first month of treatment [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2016/021743s025lbl.pdf).
- Serious adverse reactions: interstitial lung disease (~1.1%, can be fatal), hepatotoxicity, renal failure, GI perforation, bullous/exfoliative skin disorders, and hemorrhage in patients on warfarin [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2016/021743s025lbl.pdf).
