# High-Risk Non-Muscle Invasive Bladder Cancer


## Brief

A subtype of NMIBC characterized by features such as T1 tumors, high-grade/grade 3 tumors, carcinoma in situ (CIS), or large/recurrent/multiple tumors, which carries a higher risk of recurrence and progression.

## Why this matters

This specific patient population is the focus of the new durvalumab approval, highlighting a critical need for effective therapies to prevent disease progression.
