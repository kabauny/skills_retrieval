# Letrozole

## Overview
Letrozole (Femara) is an oral nonsteroidal third-generation aromatase inhibitor used as endocrine therapy for hormone receptor-positive breast cancer in postmenopausal women. By suppressing peripheral estrogen biosynthesis, it deprives estrogen-dependent tumors of their growth signal. It is a cornerstone of adjuvant, extended adjuvant, and advanced/metastatic treatment, often combined with CDK4/6 inhibitors in the metastatic setting.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: nonsteroidal competitive inhibitor of the aromatase enzyme (CYP19), binding the heme of its cytochrome P450 subunit to block conversion of androgens to estrogens, suppressing plasma estradiol/estrone by 75-95% within 2-3 days [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2014/020726s027lbl.pdf).
- FDA-approved indications (postmenopausal women): adjuvant treatment of HR-positive early breast cancer; extended adjuvant treatment after prior standard tamoxifen; and first- and second-line treatment of HR-positive or unknown advanced breast cancer [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2014/020726s027lbl.pdf).
- Pivotal adjuvant trial BIG 1-98: upfront letrozole vs tamoxifen reduced disease-free survival events by 19% (HR 0.81; P=0.003), driven especially by fewer distant metastases (HR 0.73; P=0.001) [BIG 1-98](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17912636/).
- Extended adjuvant trial MA.17: letrozole vs placebo after ~5 years of tamoxifen improved disease-free survival with HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.45-0.76; P<0.001) at median 30-month follow-up [MA.17](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17912635/).
- MA.17R: continuing letrozole for a further 5 years after initial AI therapy raised 5-year DFS to 95% vs 91% (HR 0.66; 95% CI 0.48-0.91; P=0.01), mainly via fewer second primary breast cancers [MA.17R](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1604700).
- Notable adverse events: hot flashes, arthralgia/myalgia, and estrogen-deprivation effects on bone — reduced bone mineral density (median lumbar spine decrease ~4.1% at 2 years vs +0.3% with tamoxifen), with higher rates of osteoporosis and fracture; baseline and periodic DEXA monitoring is recommended [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2014/020726s027lbl.pdf).
