# Enzalutamide

## Overview
Enzalutamide (Xtandi) is an oral second-generation androgen receptor (AR) signaling inhibitor used across the spectrum of advanced prostate cancer. It blocks androgen binding to the AR, inhibits AR nuclear translocation, and prevents AR-DNA interaction, suppressing the androgen-driven transcription that fuels prostate cancer growth. First approved in 2012 for metastatic castration-resistant disease, its indications now span castration-resistant and castration-sensitive settings, both metastatic and nonmetastatic.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: competitively inhibits androgen binding to the AR with higher affinity than bicalutamide, blocks AR nuclear translocation, and inhibits AR binding to DNA, making it a pure AR antagonist [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/203415s018,213674s005lbl.pdf).
- FDA-approved indications: metastatic and nonmetastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (CRPC), metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer (mCSPC), and nonmetastatic castration-sensitive disease with high-risk biochemical recurrence [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-enzalutamide-non-metastatic-castration-sensitive-prostate-cancer-biochemical-recurrence).
- AFFIRM (post-docetaxel mCRPC): improved overall survival vs placebo, OS HR 0.63 (median OS 18.4 vs 13.6 mo) [Enzalutamide review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30848157/).
- PREVAIL (chemotherapy-naive mCRPC): OS HR 0.71 (median OS 32.4 vs 30.2 mo); 5-year follow-up HR 0.83 [PREVAIL 5-yr](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0302283820303298).
- ARCHES (mCSPC, with ADT): reduced risk of death by 34%, OS HR 0.66 (95% CI 0.53-0.81) [ARCHES](https://www.pfizer.com/news/press-release/press-release-detail/astellas-and-pfizers-xtandir-enzalutamide-reduced-risk).
- EMBARK (high-risk biochemically recurrent nmCSPC): enzalutamide + leuprolide improved metastasis-free survival vs leuprolide alone, MFS HR 0.42 (95% CI 0.30-0.61) [EMBARK NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2303974).
- Notable adverse events: fatigue, hypertension, falls and fractures, and rare but serious seizures and posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome (PRES); monotherapy also causes gynecomastia and breast tenderness [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/203415s018,213674s005lbl.pdf).
