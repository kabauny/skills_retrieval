# Ovarian cancer

## Overview
Ovarian cancer is the leading cause of gynecologic cancer death in the United States, and most patients present with advanced (FIGO stage III–IV) disease. Over 95% of cases are epithelial, with high-grade serous carcinoma the dominant and most lethal subtype. The therapeutic spine is maximal cytoreductive surgery plus platinum–taxane chemotherapy, followed by biomarker-directed maintenance (PARP inhibitor and/or bevacizumab).

## Key facts
- Epithelial tumors account for >95% of ovarian cancers; histologic subtypes include high-grade serous (most common and most lethal), low-grade serous, endometrioid, clear cell, and mucinous, with ~5% being non-epithelial (germ cell, sex-cord stromal) tumors [Epithelial Ovarian Cancer StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK567760/).
- FIGO staging is surgical; stage gates intent and approach — early-stage disease can be cured by complete surgical staging plus chemotherapy, whereas advanced stage III–IV disease is managed for prolonged control, with stage IV median survival roughly 15–29 months and ~20% 5-year survival [FIGO stage IV cytoreduction analysis](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/oncology/articles/10.3389/fonc.2023.1103357/full).
- Resectability sets the surgical pathway: primary debulking surgery followed by platinum chemotherapy versus neoadjuvant chemotherapy with interval debulking for bulky stage III–IV disease; meta-analysis shows comparable OS/PFS but higher complete resection and lower morbidity with the neoadjuvant route [NACT vs PDS meta-analysis](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9756845/).
- BRCA1/2 (germline or somatic) and homologous recombination deficiency (HRD) are the key biomarkers that gate maintenance therapy — they predict platinum sensitivity and PARP-inhibitor benefit [NCCN Ovarian Cancer Guidelines](https://jnccn.org/view/journals/jnccn/19/2/article-p191.xml?print=).
- In BRCA-mutated newly diagnosed advanced disease, maintenance olaparib after platinum response markedly prolonged PFS in the phase 3 SOLO1 trial (HR 0.30, 95% CI 0.23–0.41; median PFS 56 vs 14 months at long-term follow-up) [SOLO1 NEJM 2018](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1810858).
- Adding olaparib to bevacizumab maintenance benefited the HRD-positive population in the phase 3 PAOLA-1 trial (overall HR 0.59; HRD-positive HR 0.33, median PFS 37.2 vs 17.7 months), supporting HRD testing to direct combination maintenance [PAOLA-1 NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1911361).
- The recurrent-disease landscape layers platinum re-challenge, anti-angiogenic therapy (bevacizumab), PARP inhibitors, and immunotherapy onto the front-line backbone, with regimen choice driven by platinum-free interval and biomarker status [NCCN Ovarian Cancer Guidelines](https://jnccn.org/view/journals/jnccn/19/2/article-p191.xml?print=).
