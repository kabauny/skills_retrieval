# Ciltacabtagene autoleucel

## Overview

Ciltacabtagene autoleucel (Carvykti, cilta-cel) is an autologous, B-cell maturation antigen (BCMA)-directed chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T-cell therapy for relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma. First FDA-approved in 2022 for heavily pretreated disease (≥4 prior lines), it was expanded in April 2024 to earlier use after at least one prior line including a proteasome inhibitor (PI) and an immunomodulatory agent (IMiD), in patients refractory to lenalidomide. It produces deep, durable, single-infusion responses but carries a boxed warning for serious immune and neurologic toxicities.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: autologous CAR T-cells with two BCMA-binding single-domain antibodies, redirecting T-cells to kill BCMA-expressing malignant plasma cells; given as a one-time infusion after lymphodepletion ([FDA label](https://www.fda.gov/media/156560/download)).
- Pivotal phase 3 CARTITUDE-4 (N=419, randomized 1:1 vs. DPd or PVd standard of care) met its primary endpoint, reducing risk of progression or death by 74% (PFS HR 0.26; P<0.0001) in lenalidomide-refractory MM after 1–3 prior lines ([CARTITUDE-4, NEJM/J&J](https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/carvykti-ciltacabtagene-autoleucel-reduces-risk-of-disease-progression-or-death-by-74-percent-in-earlier-line-multiple-myeloma-treatment-in-the-landmark-phase-3-cartitude-4-study)).
- CARTITUDE-4 updated analysis (median follow-up 33.6 mo) showed a significant overall survival benefit: OS HR 0.55 (95% CI 0.39–0.79; P=0.0009), 30-month OS 76.4% vs 63.8%, with PFS still not reached vs 11.8 mo ([CARTITUDE-4 OS, Lancet Oncology](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanonc/article/PIIS1470-2045(25)00653-9/fulltext)).
- Phase 1b/2 CARTITUDE-1 (heavily pretreated R/R MM) reported overall response rate 97%, stringent complete response 67%, with deep and durable remissions on long-term follow-up ([CARTITUDE-1, Lancet/PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34175021/)).
- Boxed warning: cytokine release syndrome (CRS), immune effector cell–associated neurotoxicity syndrome (ICANS), Parkinsonism, Guillain-Barré syndrome, HLH/MAS, prolonged cytopenias, and secondary malignancies ([FDA label](https://www.fda.gov/media/156560/download)).
- Neurotoxicity is notable: across CARTITUDE-1 and -4, ICANS occurred in 13% (Grade ≥3 in 2%); delayed movement/neurocognitive toxicity including parkinsonism is also described ([FDA Carvykti label](https://www.fda.gov/media/156560/download)).
- Initial 2022 FDA approval was for adults with R/R MM after ≥4 prior lines (PI, IMiD, anti-CD38 mAb); the April 2024 label expansion (post-ODAC) moved it to second-line and beyond ([FDA D.I.S.C.O.](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-disco-burst-edition-fda-approval-carvykti-ciltacabtagene-autoleucel-treatment-adult-patients)).
