# RASolute 302 trial

## Overview

RASolute 302 (NCT06625320) is a global, randomized, open-label phase 3 trial of daraxonrasib (RMC-6236), an oral RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor, in patients with previously treated metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC). Roughly 500 patients who had received one prior line of chemotherapy were randomized 1:1 to daraxonrasib versus investigator's choice of standard cytotoxic chemotherapy. The trial met its primary and key secondary endpoints, nearly doubling median overall survival, and was presented in the 2026 ASCO Plenary and published in NEJM.

## Key facts

- Design: global, randomized, open-label phase 3 trial; ~500 patients (NCT06625320) randomized 1:1 to daraxonrasib 300 mg orally once daily vs investigator's choice chemotherapy [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06625320](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06625320).
- Population: adults with histologically/cytologically confirmed metastatic PDAC after one prior line of chemotherapy; enrolled regardless of RAS mutation status (RAS-mutant and RAS wild-type) [Revolution Medicines](https://ir.revmed.com/news-releases/news-release-details/daraxonrasib-demonstrates-unprecedented-overall-survival-benefit).
- Mechanism: daraxonrasib is an oral RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor targeting the active (GTP-bound) state of multiple RAS variants [Dana-Farber](https://www.dana-farber.org/newsroom/news-releases/2026/rason-inhibitor-doubles-median-overall-survival-in-results-of-phase-3-trial-for-patients-with-metastatic-pancreatic-cancer).
- Primary result (OS, ITT): median OS 13.2 vs 6.7 months, HR 0.40 (P<.0001) — a ~60% reduction in risk of death, nearly doubling survival [CancerNetwork](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/daraxonrasib-shows-unprecedented-survival-in-pretreated-pancreatic-cancer).
- PFS (ITT): median 7.2 vs 3.6 months, HR 0.49 (95% CI 0.38–0.64; P<.0001) [Cleveland Clinic Journal of Medicine](https://www.ccjm.org/page/asco-2026/daraxonrasib-pancreatic).
- RAS G12 subgroup: median PFS 7.3 vs 3.5 months, HR 0.45 (95% CI 0.34–0.59; P<.0001) [Cleveland Clinic Journal of Medicine](https://www.ccjm.org/page/asco-2026/daraxonrasib-pancreatic).
- Safety: grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs in 43.6% (daraxonrasib) vs 57.5% (chemotherapy); most common grade ≥3 events were rash and stomatitis; patients reported delayed deterioration in pain and quality of life [Cleveland Clinic Journal of Medicine](https://www.ccjm.org/page/asco-2026/daraxonrasib-pancreatic).
- Significance: results presented in the 2026 ASCO Plenary and published in NEJM; FDA allowed expanded access, supporting a transformative role in pretreated metastatic PDAC [NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2605555).
