# Sorafenib

## Overview
Sorafenib (Nexavar) is an oral small-molecule multikinase inhibitor that blocks RAF serine/threonine kinases and multiple receptor tyrosine kinases driving tumor proliferation and angiogenesis. It was the first systemic agent to demonstrate a survival benefit in advanced hepatocellular carcinoma and is also approved for advanced renal cell carcinoma and radioactive-iodine-refractory differentiated thyroid cancer. Its characteristic toxicity is dermatologic, dominated by hand-foot skin reaction.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: multikinase inhibitor targeting RAF-1/B-RAF (including mutant) plus the receptor tyrosine kinases VEGFR-1/2/3, PDGFR-β, KIT, FLT3, and RET, inhibiting both tumor-cell proliferation and angiogenesis [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/021923s023lbl.pdf)
- Principal FDA-approved indications: unresectable hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC), and locally recurrent/metastatic, progressive, differentiated thyroid carcinoma refractory to radioactive iodine; dosed 400 mg orally twice daily [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/021923s023lbl.pdf)
- Pivotal HCC trial SHARP (phase 3, n=602): median overall survival 10.7 vs 7.9 months for sorafenib vs placebo (HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.55–0.87, P<0.001), a 31% reduction in risk of death [SHARP](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa0708857)
- Pivotal RCC trial TARGET (phase 3, n=903, prior systemic therapy): median progression-free survival 5.5 vs 2.8 months (HR 0.44, 95% CI 0.35–0.55, P<0.01) [TARGET](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa060655)
- Pivotal thyroid trial DECISION (phase 3, n=417, RAI-refractory DTC): median progression-free survival 10.8 vs 5.8 months (HR 0.59, 95% CI 0.45–0.76, P<0.0001), a 41% reduction in risk of progression/death [DECISION](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(14)60421-9/abstract)
- Most common toxicity is hand-foot skin reaction (any-grade ~30-60%); other notable/serious events include diarrhea, fatigue, hypertension, rash/alopecia, and boxed/warning-level risks of cardiac ischemia/infarction, hemorrhage, GI perforation, and QT prolongation [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/021923s023lbl.pdf)
