# Sunitinib

## Overview
Sunitinib (SUTENT) is an oral, small-molecule multitargeted receptor tyrosine kinase inhibitor that blocks VEGFRs, PDGFRs, KIT, FLT3, and RET, exerting both antiangiogenic and antitumor effects. It is FDA-approved across three distinct tumor types: advanced renal cell carcinoma, imatinib-resistant or -intolerant gastrointestinal stromal tumor (GIST), and progressive well-differentiated pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors (pNET). It carries a boxed warning for severe, potentially fatal hepatotoxicity.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: multitargeted RTK inhibitor of VEGFR1-3, PDGFRα/β, KIT, FLT3, CSF-1R, and RET, producing antiangiogenic and direct antitumor activity [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2011/021938s13s17s18lbl.pdf).
- FDA-approved indications: advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC), gastrointestinal stromal tumor (GIST) after disease progression on or intolerance to imatinib, and progressive well-differentiated unresectable/metastatic pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors (pNET) [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2011/021938s13s17s18lbl.pdf).
- Pivotal RCC trial (Motzer, NEJM): in 750 untreated metastatic RCC patients, sunitinib vs interferon-alfa improved median PFS from 5 to 11 months (HR 0.42) with ORR 31% vs 6% [Motzer NEJM 2007](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa065044).
- Pivotal pNET trial (Raymond, NEJM): median PFS 11.4 vs 5.5 months for sunitinib vs placebo (HR 0.42, 95% CI 0.26-0.66, P<0.001) [Raymond NEJM 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3756637/).
- Pivotal GIST trial (Demetri, Lancet): in imatinib-resistant GIST, median time to progression 27.3 vs 6.4 weeks vs placebo (HR 0.33, P<0.0001) with roughly halved risk of death [Demetri Lancet 2006](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/sunitinib-effective-imatinib-resistant-gist-patients).
- Boxed warning: severe and sometimes fatal hepatotoxicity (liver failure 0.3% in trials); monitor LFTs at baseline and each cycle [FDA Warnings](https://www.pfizermedical.com/sutent/warnings).
- Other serious adverse events: heart failure/cardiomyopathy and myocardial ischemia, hypertension, QT prolongation/Torsade de Pointes, and severe cutaneous reactions (SJS/TEN); common toxicities include hand-foot syndrome, fatigue, diarrhea, and hypothyroidism [FDA Warnings](https://www.pfizermedical.com/sutent/warnings).
