# Mosunetuzumab

## Overview
Mosunetuzumab (Lunsumio, mosunetuzumab-axgb) is a humanized CD20×CD3 T-cell-engaging bispecific monoclonal antibody that redirects endogenous T cells to kill malignant B cells. It received FDA accelerated approval in December 2022 for adult patients with relapsed or refractory follicular lymphoma after two or more prior lines of systemic therapy, and is given as a fixed-duration, off-the-shelf regimen with step-up dosing to mitigate cytokine release syndrome.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: bispecific antibody binding CD20 on B cells and CD3 on T cells, forming an immunologic synapse that triggers T-cell activation and lysis of malignant B cells [FDA D.I.S.C.O.](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-disco-burst-edition-fda-approval-lunsumio-mosunetuzumab-axgb-adult-patients-relapsed-or).
- Indication: accelerated approval for relapsed/refractory follicular lymphoma after >=2 prior lines of systemic therapy; a subcutaneous formulation (Lunsumio VELO) was approved December 2025 [ASCO](https://www.asco.org/news-initiatives/policy-news-analysis/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-mosunetuzumab-axgb-relapsed-or).
- Pivotal trial GO29781 (phase 1/2, single-arm, n=90 FL patients) showed objective response rate 78% and complete response rate 60% by independent review [ASCO Post](https://ascopost.com/issues/february-10-2023/mosunetuzumab-axgb-for-relapsed-or-refractory-follicular-lymphoma/).
- Responses were durable: 24-month duration-of-response rate 53%, 2-year PFS 48% and 2-year OS 87% at extended follow-up [OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/mosunetuzumab-elicits-long-term-responses-in-relapsed-refractory-follicular-lymphoma).
- Boxed warning for serious/life-threatening cytokine release syndrome; step-up dosing in Cycle 1 is required to reduce CRS risk [FDA D.I.S.C.O.](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-disco-burst-edition-fda-approval-lunsumio-mosunetuzumab-axgb-adult-patients-relapsed-or).
- Across 218 treated patients, CRS occurred in 39% (mostly low grade), neurologic toxicity in 39%, serious infections in 17%, and tumor flare in 4% [FDA D.I.S.C.O.](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-disco-burst-edition-fda-approval-lunsumio-mosunetuzumab-axgb-adult-patients-relapsed-or).
