# Pola-R-CHP

## Overview

Pola-R-CHP is a frontline combination chemoimmunotherapy regimen for diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) in which polatuzumab vedotin (Polivy), a CD79b-directed antibody-drug conjugate carrying the antimicrotubule agent MMAE, replaces vincristine in standard R-CHOP. The regimen pairs polatuzumab vedotin with rituximab, cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, and prednisone. It was tested head-to-head against R-CHOP in the pivotal phase 3 POLARIX trial and gained FDA approval in 2023 for previously untreated DLBCL/high-grade B-cell lymphoma with an intermediate-to-high IPI score.

## Key facts

- Polatuzumab vedotin (Polivy) is a CD79b-directed antibody-drug conjugate delivering monomethyl auristatin E (MMAE), substituted for the vincristine of R-CHOP to form the R-CHP backbone [FDA approval, OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/fda-approves-polatuzumab-vedotin-plus-r-chp-for-previously-untreated-dlbcl).
- FDA approved Pola-R-CHP on April 19, 2023 for adults with previously untreated DLBCL, not otherwise specified (NOS), or high-grade B-cell lymphoma (HGBL) and an International Prognostic Index (IPI) score of 2 or greater [FDA, OncPracticeManagement](https://www.oncpracticemanagement.com/issues/2023/may-2023-vol-13-no-5/fda-approves-polivy-plus-r-chp-for-previously-untreated-dlbcl).
- Approval was based on POLARIX (NCT03274492), a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 trial of 879 previously untreated DLBCL patients with IPI 2-5, randomized to Pola-R-CHP (n=440) vs R-CHOP (n=439) [POLARIX, NEJM 2022](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2115304).
- Primary endpoint was investigator-assessed progression-free survival; at median 28.2 months, 2-year PFS was 76.7% with Pola-R-CHP vs 70.2% with R-CHOP (stratified HR 0.73, 95% CI 0.57-0.95; p=0.02) [POLARIX, NEJM 2022](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2115304).
- The PFS benefit persisted at 5-year follow-up (HR 0.77), though no overall-survival advantage was demonstrated [5-year POLARIX, JCO 2025](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO-25-00925).
- The most common adverse reactions (≥20%) include peripheral neuropathy, nausea, fatigue, diarrhea, constipation, alopecia, and mucositis [FDA, CancerNetwork](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/polatuzumab-vedotin-r-chp-meets-primary-end-point-of-progression-free-survival-for-dlbcl-in-polarix-phase-3-trial).
- Safety and overall response rates were broadly comparable to R-CHOP, with no increase in grade 3-4 toxicity from the substitution [POLARIX, NEJM 2022](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2115304).
