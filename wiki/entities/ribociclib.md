# Ribociclib

## Overview
Ribociclib (Kisqali) is an oral selective cyclin-dependent kinase 4/6 (CDK4/6) inhibitor used in hormone receptor (HR)-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer. It is approved in combination with endocrine therapy across the advanced/metastatic setting and, more recently, the adjuvant (early breast cancer) setting. By blocking CDK4/6 it restores Rb-mediated cell-cycle control and arrests proliferation of estrogen-driven tumor cells.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: selectively inhibits CDK4 and CDK6, preventing phosphorylation of the retinoblastoma (Rb) protein and causing G1 cell-cycle arrest in HR+ breast cancer cells [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/209092s013,209935s021lbl.pdf).
- Indicated with an aromatase inhibitor as initial endocrine-based therapy, or with fulvestrant as initial therapy or after progression, in HR+/HER2- advanced or metastatic breast cancer (postmenopausal women or men) [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/209092s013,209935s021lbl.pdf).
- MONALEESA-2 (1L + letrozole, postmenopausal advanced): final median OS 63.9 vs 51.4 months, HR 0.76 (95% CI 0.63-0.93; P=0.008) [MONALEESA-2 NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2114663).
- MONALEESA-7 (pre/perimenopausal + AI/tamoxifen + goserelin): median PFS 27.5 vs 13.8 months, a key trial establishing benefit in younger women [FDA approval summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10840597/).
- MONALEESA-3 (+ fulvestrant): in first-line patients median OS 67.6 vs 51.8 months, HR 0.67 (95% CI 0.50-0.90) at the updated analysis [MONALEESA-3 OS](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0923753421015532).
- NATALEE (adjuvant + nonsteroidal AI in early breast cancer): invasive disease-free survival HR 0.749 (95% CI 0.628-0.892), 3-year iDFS 90.7% vs 87.6% [NATALEE iDFS](https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(24)04064-X/fulltext).
- Notable/serious adverse events: neutropenia (the most common; ~59% grade 3/4 in MONALEESA-2), QTc-interval prolongation, and hepatotoxicity (grade 3/4 ALT/AST elevations up to ~9%); ECG and LFT/electrolyte monitoring are required [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/209092s013,209935s021lbl.pdf).
