# Non-Muscle Invasive Bladder Cancer


## Brief

A type of bladder cancer where the tumor has not grown into the muscle layer of the bladder wall, often treated with surgery and intravesical therapy.

## Why this matters

NMIBC is a common form of bladder cancer, and new treatment options like durvalumab aim to prevent recurrence and progression in high-risk patients.
