# Alectinib

## Overview
Alectinib (Alecensa) is an oral, second-generation small-molecule tyrosine kinase inhibitor of anaplastic lymphoma kinase (ALK). It is used in ALK-positive non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), both as first-line therapy for advanced/metastatic disease and as adjuvant therapy after resection of early-stage disease. It has strong CNS penetration, giving notable activity against brain metastases.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: selective second-generation ALK tyrosine kinase inhibitor that blocks ALK and the oncogenic signaling of EML4-ALK fusions; it retains activity against several crizotinib-resistant ALK mutations and crosses the blood-brain barrier [FDA/NCI](https://www.cancer.gov/news-events/cancer-currents-blog/2024/fda-alectinib-lung-cancer-alk-positive).
- Approved for first-line treatment of advanced/metastatic ALK-positive NSCLC, and (since April 18, 2024) as adjuvant therapy after resection of ALK-positive NSCLC with tumors ≥4 cm or node-positive [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-alectinib-adjuvant-treatment-alk-positive-non-small-cell-lung-cancer).
- Pivotal first-line trial ALEX vs crizotinib: alectinib reduced risk of progression or death by 53% (PFS HR 0.47, 95% CI 0.34–0.65; p<0.0001) [ALEX/NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1704795).
- ALEX CNS benefit: CNS progression occurred in 12% (alectinib) vs 45% (crizotinib) (cause-specific HR 0.16, 95% CI 0.10–0.28; p<0.001), supporting use with brain metastases [ALEX CNS analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6290889/).
- Pivotal adjuvant trial ALINA vs platinum chemotherapy: disease recurrence or death reduced by 76% (DFS HR 0.24, 95% CI 0.13–0.43; p<0.0001); 3-year DFS 88.7% vs 54.0% [ALINA/OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/fda-approves-adjuvant-alectinib-for-alk-early-stage-nsclc).
- Safety: grade 3–5 adverse events were less frequent than with crizotinib (41% vs 50%); notable toxicities include hepatotoxicity (transaminase/bilirubin elevations), myalgia and CPK elevation, anemia, bradycardia, edema, constipation, and photosensitivity [ALEX/NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1704795).
