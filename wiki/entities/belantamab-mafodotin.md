# Belantamab mafodotin

## Overview

Belantamab mafodotin (Blenrep) is a B-cell maturation antigen (BCMA)-directed antibody-drug conjugate carrying the microtubule inhibitor monomethyl auristatin F (MMAF). After being withdrawn as monotherapy following the negative confirmatory DREAMM-3 trial, it was reapproved by the FDA on October 23, 2025 in combination regimens for relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma based on the DREAMM-7 and DREAMM-8 phase 3 trials. It offers an off-the-shelf, non-CAR-T BCMA-targeted option, but carries a boxed warning for ocular toxicity.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: BCMA-directed antibody-drug conjugate; the afucosylated humanized IgG1 antibody delivers the cytotoxic MMAF payload to myeloma cells, driving apoptosis plus antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity/phagocytosis [[FDA label](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-belantamab-mafodotin-blmf-relapsed-or-refractory-multiple-myeloma)].
- FDA approved Oct 23, 2025 with bortezomib + dexamethasone for relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma after ≥1 prior line, and with pomalidomide + dexamethasone after ≥1 prior line including lenalidomide [[FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-belantamab-mafodotin-blmf-relapsed-or-refractory-multiple-myeloma)].
- DREAMM-7 (phase 3, open-label, randomized, N=494): belamaf + bortezomib + dexamethasone (BVd) vs daratumumab + bortezomib + dexamethasone (DVd); median PFS 36.6 vs 13.4 months, HR 0.41 (95% CI 0.31–0.53; p<0.00001) [[DREAMM-7, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa2405090)].
- DREAMM-7 updated overall survival (median follow-up 39.4 mo): median OS not reached for BVd vs 41.0 months for DVd, HR 0.58, a statistically significant survival benefit [[DREAMM-7 OS, PubMed/Lancet](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40680754/)].
- DREAMM-8 (phase 3, randomized): belamaf + pomalidomide + dexamethasone (BPd) vs pomalidomide + bortezomib + dexamethasone (PVd); BPd significantly improved PFS, HR 0.52 (95% CI 0.37–0.73; p<0.001) [[DREAMM-8, JCO/ASCO](https://www.asco.org/abstracts-presentations/234910)].
- Boxed warning: ocular toxicity (keratopathy/corneal epithelium changes, blurred vision, dry eye); in DREAMM-7 ocular toxicity occurred in 92% of belamaf patients (Grade 3/4 in 77%), with 83% requiring dose modification [[FDA label](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-belantamab-mafodotin-blmf-relapsed-or-refractory-multiple-myeloma)].
- Other notable adverse events include thrombocytopenia and infusion-related reactions; in DREAMM-8 grade 3/4 AEs occurred in 91% of BPd patients with treatment discontinuation in 22% [[DREAMM-8 LancetHaem](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanhae/article/PIIS2352-3026(25)00256-X/abstract)].
- Risk mitigation requires ophthalmic exams at baseline and before each dose, with dose holds/reductions guided by corneal exam findings and visual acuity [[ASCO Post](https://ascopost.com/news/october-2025/fda-approves-belantamab-mafodotin-for-relapsed-or-refractory-multiple-myeloma/)].
