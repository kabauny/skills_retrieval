# Renal cell carcinoma (RCC)

## Overview
Renal cell carcinoma is the most common form of kidney cancer, accounting for roughly 85% of kidney tumors, with clear cell histology (ccRCC) comprising about 70%. Localized disease is managed surgically with curative intent, while metastatic disease is treated palliatively with immunotherapy-based combinations. The IMDC risk model (favorable vs intermediate/poor) is the dominant tool for stratifying prognosis and selecting first-line systemic therapy.

## Key facts
- Kidney cancer is common: the American Cancer Society estimated about 80,450 new US cases and 15,160 deaths for 2026; RCC makes up ~85% of kidney tumors, with clear cell, papillary, and chromophobe the principal histologic subtypes ([ACS statistics](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/kidney-cancer/about/key-statistics.html), [NCCN Kidney Cancer 1.2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10191771/)).
- Staging/resectability sets intent: localized (stage I–III) RCC is managed with curative partial or radical nephrectomy (or ablation/active surveillance for small masses), whereas stage IV/metastatic disease is treated with palliative systemic therapy, often with cytoreductive nephrectomy in selected patients ([NCCN Kidney Cancer 1.2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10191771/)).
- Histology and IMDC risk gate therapy: clear-cell vs non-clear-cell histology and the IMDC favorable/intermediate/poor risk classification determine first-line regimen choice, with VHL loss driving HIF-mediated angiogenesis that underlies VEGF-targeted (TKI) therapy ([NCCN Kidney Cancer 1.2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10191771/)).
- CheckMate 214 established the ipilimumab + nivolumab doublet for intermediate/poor-risk advanced ccRCC, with sustained OS benefit vs sunitinib (median 46.7 vs 26.0 mo; HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.59–0.81) at extended follow-up ([CheckMate 214 8-yr, Ann Oncol](https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(24)01516-3/fulltext)).
- KEYNOTE-426 supports pembrolizumab + axitinib across all IMDC risk groups, with improved OS vs sunitinib (HR 0.84, 95% CI 0.71–0.99 at ≥5 yr), PFS HR 0.69, and ORR 60.6% vs 39.6% ([KEYNOTE-426 5-yr, Nat Med](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03867-5)).
- CLEAR established lenvatinib + pembrolizumab as a first-line option with the longest reported PFS (median 23.9 vs 9.2 mo; HR 0.39, 95% CI 0.32–0.49), OS HR 0.66 (95% CI 0.49–0.88), and ORR 71% vs 36% vs sunitinib ([CLEAR, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2035716)).
- Treatment landscape by line: first line uses an IO/IO (ipilimumab+nivolumab) or IO/TKI combination by IMDC risk; later lines rely on VEGFR TKIs (cabozantinib, axitinib, tivozanib), mTOR inhibitors (everolimus), and the HIF-2α inhibitor belzutifan ([NCCN Kidney Cancer 1.2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10191771/)).
