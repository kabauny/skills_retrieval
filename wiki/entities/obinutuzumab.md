# Obinutuzumab

## Overview
Obinutuzumab (Gazyva) is a glycoengineered, humanized type II anti-CD20 IgG1 monoclonal antibody. Compared with rituximab, its Fc glycoengineering enhances antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) and direct cell death while reducing complement-dependent cytotoxicity. It is approved, in combination with chemotherapy, for previously untreated chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) and for follicular lymphoma, and more recently for lupus nephritis.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: type II glycoengineered anti-CD20 IgG1 antibody binding a CD20 epitope on B cells; afucosylation increases FcγRIII affinity, boosting ADCC and antibody-dependent phagocytosis and inducing strong direct cell death, with comparatively low complement-dependent cytotoxicity vs rituximab [Review/PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6166403/).
- Principal FDA-approved oncology indications: previously untreated CLL (with chlorambucil) and follicular lymphoma (first-line with chemotherapy, then maintenance; and relapsed/refractory with bendamustine then maintenance) [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/125486s037s038lbl.pdf).
- Pivotal CLL trial — CLL11 (untreated CLL with comorbidities): obinutuzumab+chlorambucil vs chlorambucil alone gave median PFS 26.7 vs 11.1 months, HR for progression/death 0.18 (95% CI 0.13–0.24), and improved OS (HR 0.41) [CLL11/NEJM](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24401022/).
- CLL11 head-to-head vs rituximab: obinutuzumab+chlorambucil vs rituximab+chlorambucil prolonged PFS to a median 28.7 vs 15.7 months (HR 0.46, 95% CI 0.38–0.55) [CLL11/NEJM](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24401022/).
- Pivotal FL trial — GALLIUM (untreated follicular lymphoma): obinutuzumab- vs rituximab-based chemo improved 3-year PFS to 80.0% vs 73.3%, HR for progression/relapse/death 0.66 (95% CI 0.51–0.85; p=0.001) [GALLIUM/NEJM 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28976863/).
- Boxed warning: hepatitis B virus reactivation (screen all patients before treatment) and progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy (PML), both potentially fatal [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/125486s037s038lbl.pdf).
- Notable adverse events: severe, life-threatening infusion-related reactions (mitigated with premedication/step-up dosing), neutropenia (including Grade 4) and other cytopenias (lymphopenia, thrombocytopenia), tumor lysis syndrome, and infections [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/125486s037s038lbl.pdf).
