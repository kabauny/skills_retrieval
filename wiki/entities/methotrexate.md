# Methotrexate

## Overview
Methotrexate is an antifolate antimetabolite that inhibits dihydrofolate reductase, blocking the folate-dependent synthesis of thymidylate and purine nucleotides and thereby halting DNA synthesis and cell replication. It is one of the oldest and most versatile oncology agents, used across a wide dose range from low oral doses to high-dose intravenous regimens (with leucovorin rescue). In hematologic oncology it is a backbone of CNS-directed therapy, including high-dose methotrexate (HD-MTX) for CNS prophylaxis in high-risk diffuse large B-cell lymphoma.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: competitively inhibits dihydrofolate reductase; its intracellular polyglutamate forms also inhibit thymidylate synthase and purine synthesis, interrupting DNA/RNA synthesis and replication [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/214121s001lbl.pdf).
- FDA-approved oncology indications include acute lymphoblastic leukemia, meningeal leukemia, non-Hodgkin lymphoma, breast cancer, head and neck squamous cell carcinoma, osteosarcoma, cutaneous T-cell lymphoma (mycosis fungoides), and gestational trophoblastic neoplasia [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK556114/).
- Dosing spans 10 to 5000 mg/m2 IV; high-dose regimens (e.g., osteosarcoma) require leucovorin rescue and accumulate the 7-hydroxymethotrexate metabolite, mandating drug-level and renal monitoring [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/011719s126lbl.pdf).
- In high-risk DLBCL, prophylactic HD-MTX lowered the 3-year isolated CNS relapse rate to 3.1% vs 14.6% without prophylaxis (P=0.032), an 84% relative risk reduction (HR 0.16) on multivariable analysis [Blood Cancer Journal 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41408-021-00535-y).
- HD-MTX CNS prophylaxis reduced isolated CNS relapse but showed no significant improvement in systemic relapse, PFS, or OS, and its overall role remains controversial absent randomized trials [Blood 2022](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/139/16/2499/483371/Timing-of-high-dose-methotrexate-CNS-prophylaxis).
- Carries boxed warnings for embryo-fetal toxicity (contraindicated in pregnancy for non-neoplastic disease), severe hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis, and life-threatening toxicities of the bone marrow, GI tract, liver, lung, kidney, and skin [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/214121s001lbl.pdf).
- Common serious adverse events include myelosuppression, mucositis/stomatitis, hepatotoxicity, nephrotoxicity, and pulmonary toxicity; immunosuppression raises infection risk [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK556114/).
