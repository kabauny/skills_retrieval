# Isatuximab

## Overview
Isatuximab (Sarclisa, isatuximab-irfc) is an IgG1 monoclonal antibody targeting a specific epitope of CD38, used to treat multiple myeloma. It kills myeloma cells through antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity, complement-dependent cytotoxicity, antibody-dependent cellular phagocytosis, direct apoptosis, and inhibition of CD38 ectoenzyme activity. It is given intravenously in combination regimens across both relapsed/refractory and newly diagnosed settings.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: binds a distinct CD38 epitope and induces tumor cell death via ADCC, CDC, ADCP, direct pro-apoptotic signaling, and inhibition of CD38 ADP-ribosyl cyclase enzymatic activity [FDA prescribing information](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/761113s000lbl.pdf).
- FDA-approved indications: with pomalidomide + dexamethasone (Isa-Pd) for relapsed/refractory MM after ≥2 prior therapies (including lenalidomide and a PI), with carfilzomib + dexamethasone (Isa-Kd) for R/R MM after 1–3 prior lines, and with bortezomib/lenalidomide/dexamethasone (Isa-VRd) for newly diagnosed transplant-ineligible MM [FDA approval (Isa-VRd)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-isatuximab-irfc-bortezomib-lenalidomide-and-dexamethasone-newly-diagnosed-multiple).
- ICARIA-MM (phase 3): Isa-Pd vs Pd in R/R MM improved median PFS to 11.5 vs 6.5 months (HR 0.60, 95% CI 0.44–0.81, p=0.001) [ICARIA-MM, Lancet](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31735560/).
- IKEMA (phase 3): Isa-Kd vs Kd in relapsed MM prolonged median PFS to 35.7 vs 19.2 months (HR 0.58, 95.4% CI 0.42–0.79) [IKEMA, Lancet](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34097854/).
- IMROZ (phase 3): Isa-VRd vs VRd in newly diagnosed transplant-ineligible MM improved PFS (median not reached vs 54.3 months; HR 0.60, 98.5% CI 0.406–0.876, p=0.0005; 60-month PFS 83.2% vs 45.2%) [FDA approval (Isa-VRd)](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-isatuximab-irfc-bortezomib-lenalidomide-and-dexamethasone-newly-diagnosed-multiple).
- Infusion-related reactions occurred in ~35% of patients (mostly first infusion; rare anaphylaxis), warranting premedication and infusion monitoring [FDA prescribing information](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/761113s000lbl.pdf).
- Serious/notable adverse events: neutropenia (grade 3–4 ~52%) with febrile neutropenia and neutropenic infections, plus serious and fatal infections such as pneumonia; CD38 binding can interfere with blood compatibility (indirect Coombs) and serum protein electrophoresis [FDA prescribing information](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/761113s000lbl.pdf).
