# Selpercatinib

## Overview
Selpercatinib (Retevmo) is a highly selective, CNS-active small-molecule inhibitor of the RET (REarranged during Transfection) receptor tyrosine kinase. It was the first targeted therapy developed specifically for RET-driven cancers and is approved across RET fusion-positive non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), RET-mutant and RET fusion-positive thyroid cancers, and—tumor-agnostically—any RET fusion-positive solid tumor. It is given orally and produces high, durable response rates with notable intracranial activity.

## Key facts
- Selective RET kinase inhibitor that blocks oncogenic signaling from RET fusions and activating RET point mutations, with activity against solitary brain/CNS disease [[FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/213246s011s013lbl.pdf)].
- FDA-approved for RET fusion-positive NSCLC, advanced RET-mutant medullary thyroid cancer (MTC), RET fusion-positive thyroid cancer requiring systemic therapy/radioactive-iodine-refractory, and tumor-agnostically for any locally advanced/metastatic RET fusion-positive solid tumor progressing after prior therapy [[FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-selpercatinib-ret-fusion-positive-thyroid-cancer)].
- Pivotal phase 1/2 LIBRETTO-001 trial: in RET fusion-positive NSCLC, ORR was 64% in platinum-pretreated patients and 85% in treatment-naive patients; in RET fusion-positive thyroid cancer, ORR 85% (pretreated) and 96% (naive) [[LIBRETTO-001 / ASCO Post](https://ascopost.com/issues/october-25-2022/selpercatinib-approved-for-locally-advanced-or-metastatic-ret-fusion-positive-solid-tumors/)].
- Pivotal phase 3 LIBRETTO-431 (first-line RET fusion-positive NSCLC vs platinum chemo ± pembrolizumab): median PFS 24.8 vs 11.2 months, HR 0.465 (95% CI 0.309–0.699; p<0.001) in the pembrolizumab-eligible ITT population [[LIBRETTO-431 / NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/pdf/10.1056/NEJMoa2309457)].
- LIBRETTO-431 also showed reduced CNS progression (cause-specific HR ~0.28), supporting selpercatinib's CNS-protective activity [[LIBRETTO-431 / PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38807655/)].
- Boxed/serious warnings include hepatotoxicity (serious in ~3%; AST elevation 59% / ALT 55%, Grade 3–4 ~11–12%), hypertension (35%, Grade 3 in 17%), QT prolongation (Grade 3–4 in ~5%), interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis, hemorrhagic events, hypersensitivity, tumor lysis syndrome, impaired wound healing, and embryo-fetal toxicity [[FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/213246s011s013lbl.pdf)].
- Common adverse reactions include edema, diarrhea, fatigue, dry mouth, hypertension, abdominal pain, constipation, rash, nausea, and headache; ALT/AST monitoring is recommended every 2 weeks for the first 3 months [[FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/213246s011s013lbl.pdf)].
