# Cemiplimab

## Overview
Cemiplimab (Libtayo, cemiplimab-rwlc) is a fully human IgG4 monoclonal antibody that blocks the PD-1 immune checkpoint, restoring antitumor T-cell activity. It was the first immunotherapy approved for advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (CSCC) and is also approved across non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), basal cell carcinoma, and cervical cancer. It is given intravenously at a fixed dose of 350 mg every 3 weeks.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: binds the PD-1 receptor on T cells and blocks its interaction with the ligands PD-L1 and PD-L2, releasing checkpoint-mediated suppression of antitumor immunity [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2021/761097s007lbl.pdf).
- First approved September 2018 for metastatic or locally advanced CSCC not curable by surgery or radiation — the first immunotherapy in this disease; in October 2025 it gained approval as adjuvant therapy for high-risk CSCC after surgery and radiation [Regeneron](https://investor.regeneron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/libtayor-cemiplimab-rwlc-approved-us-first-and-only).
- In advanced NSCLC, approved as first-line monotherapy for tumors with PD-L1 tumor proportion score ≥50% (2021) and in combination with platinum chemotherapy regardless of PD-L1, for tumors without EGFR/ALK/ROS1 aberrations (2022) [Regeneron](https://investor.regeneron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/libtayor-cemiplimab-rwlc-combination-chemotherapy-approved-fda/).
- Pivotal NSCLC trial EMPOWER-Lung 1 (phase 3, PD-L1 ≥50%): median OS 26.1 vs 13.3 months for cemiplimab vs chemotherapy (HR 0.59, 95% CI 0.48–0.72); median PFS 8.1 vs 5.3 months (HR 0.50) [EMPOWER-Lung 1](https://ascopost.com/news/august-2023/first-line-cemiplimab-vs-chemotherapy-in-advanced-nsclc-35-month-follow-up-of-empower-lung-1/).
- Pivotal CSCC trial EMPOWER-CSCC-1 (Study 1540, phase 2, plus phase 1 cohorts): objective response rate ~46% (95% CI 38.9–53.4) by independent central review, with durable responses on long-term follow-up [NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1805131).
- Serious immune-mediated adverse reactions can affect any organ: immune-mediated pneumonitis in 2.6% (Grade 3–4 ~0.9%) and immune-mediated colitis in 2% of pooled patients (n=1281), plus hepatitis, endocrinopathies, nephritis, and dermatologic reactions [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2021/761097s007lbl.pdf).
