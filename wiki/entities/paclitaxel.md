# paclitaxel

## Overview

Paclitaxel (originally branded Taxol) is a taxane microtubule-stabilizing chemotherapy agent first approved by the FDA in 1992. It is a backbone cytotoxic across many solid tumors, given alone or with platinum agents, and remains foundational in ovarian, breast, and non-small cell lung cancer regimens. It carries a boxed warning for severe hypersensitivity reactions and bone marrow suppression, mandating corticosteroid/antihistamine premedication.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: binds the β-tubulin subunit and stabilizes microtubules, blocking their disassembly, disrupting mitotic spindle dynamics and arresting cells at the G2/M phase, leading to apoptosis [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK536917/).
- Principal FDA-approved indications: ovarian cancer (refractory and first-line with cisplatin/carboplatin), breast cancer (adjuvant node-positive and metastatic), non-small cell lung cancer (with cisplatin), and AIDS-related Kaposi sarcoma [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/021660s047lbl.pdf).
- Pivotal trial GOG-111 (advanced ovarian cancer): cisplatin-paclitaxel vs cisplatin-cyclophosphamide improved PFS (18 vs 13 mo) and median OS (38 vs 24 mo), OS HR ~0.61 [GOG-111](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2376171/).
- Confirmatory European/Canadian intergroup trial reproduced the benefit: paclitaxel-cisplatin median OS 35.6 vs 25.8 mo and PFS 15.5 vs 11.5 mo (P=0.0016) [Intergroup trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10793106/).
- Adjuvant breast cancer trial CALGB 9344: adding paclitaxel after AC reduced recurrence (DFS HR 0.83, P=0.0023) and death (OS HR 0.82, P=0.0064), 5-year OS 80% vs 77% [CALGB 9344](https://ascopost.com/News/17378).
- Boxed warning: severe/fatal hypersensitivity (anaphylaxis in 2-4% despite premedication) and bone marrow suppression; all patients require corticosteroid, diphenhydramine, and H2-antagonist premedication [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/021660s047lbl.pdf).
- Notable adverse events: dose-limiting neutropenia, cumulative peripheral sensory neuropathy, arthralgia/myalgia, alopecia, mucositis, hypotension, and bradycardia [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK536917/).
