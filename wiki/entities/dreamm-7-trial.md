# DREAMM-7 trial

## Overview

DREAMM-7 (NCT04246047) is a global, randomized, open-label, phase 3 head-to-head trial in relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma after at least one prior line of therapy. It compared belantamab mafodotin plus bortezomib and dexamethasone (BVd) against the daratumumab-based standard-of-care regimen (DVd). The trial met its primary endpoint with a near-tripling of progression-free survival and later demonstrated a statistically significant overall survival benefit, supporting the 2025 FDA approval of the Blenrep combination.

## Key facts

- Design: global, 1:1 randomized, open-label, phase 3 trial; 494 patients (BVd n=243, DVd n=251) with multiple myeloma progressing after ≥1 prior line ([DREAMM-7, NEJM 2024](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMc2410841)).
- Intervention BVd (belantamab mafodotin, bortezomib, dexamethasone) vs comparator DVd (daratumumab, bortezomib, dexamethasone) ([DREAMM-7, Blood ASH 2024](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/144/Supplement%201/772/530972/Belantamab-Mafodotin-Bortezomib-and-Dexamethasone)).
- Primary endpoint met: independent-review median PFS 36.6 months (BVd) vs 13.4 months (DVd), hazard ratio 0.41 ([GSK press release](https://www.gsk.com/en-gb/media/press-releases/dreamm-7-phase-iii-trial-shows-pfs-improvement-and-strong-os-trend-for-blenrep-combo-versus-soc-combo-in-multiple-myeloma/)).
- Updated overall survival analysis: median OS not reached with BVd vs 41.0 months with DVd, hazard ratio 0.58 (95% CI 0.43-0.79; p=0.0002) ([DREAMM-7 OS, Lancet Oncology 2025](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanonc/article/PIIS1470-2045(25)00330-4/abstract)).
- Median duration of response 35.6 months (BVd) vs 17.8 months (DVd); first head-to-head phase 3 OS advantage for a BCMA-targeting therapy vs a daratumumab regimen ([DREAMM-7 OS analysis, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40680754/)).
- Notable toxicity: ocular toxicity in 92% of BVd patients (grade 3/4 in 77%), with frequent reduction in visual acuity, blurred vision, and dry eye, commonly requiring dose modification ([FDA approval, FDA.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-belantamab-mafodotin-blmf-relapsed-or-refractory-multiple-myeloma)).
- Status/significance: supported FDA approval (October 2025) of belantamab mafodotin-blmf plus bortezomib and dexamethasone for relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma ([FDA approval, FDA.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-belantamab-mafodotin-blmf-relapsed-or-refractory-multiple-myeloma)).
