# Esophageal cancer

## Overview
Esophageal cancer comprises two dominant histologies with divergent biology: squamous cell carcinoma (SCC), which arises in the upper/mid esophagus and predominates worldwide, and adenocarcinoma, which arises near the gastroesophageal junction (GEJ) and has risen sharply in Western countries on the back of GERD, obesity, and Barrett's esophagus. Staging (separate AJCC/TNM systems for SCC vs adenocarcinoma) and resectability define curative (resection ± perioperative or definitive chemoradiation) versus palliative intent. Biomarkers — histology, PD-L1, and HER2 (adenocarcinoma) — increasingly gate the choice of immunotherapy and targeted agents.

## Key facts
- Two main histologic subtypes: squamous cell carcinoma (the most common type globally, ~37% in the US, upper/mid esophagus) and adenocarcinoma (now predominant in developed nations, distal esophagus/GEJ), with prognosis and treatment differing enough that AJCC and NCCN use separate stage groupings for each [stage review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4021327/).
- Staging/resectability sets intent: localized resectable disease is treated with curative-intent surgery, typically with neoadjuvant chemoradiation or perioperative chemotherapy, whereas advanced/metastatic disease is managed with palliative systemic therapy [staging guidelines](https://jtd.amegroups.org/article/view/2262/html).
- Key biomarkers gating therapy are histology (SCC vs adenocarcinoma), PD-L1 expression (TPS/CPS, predicting checkpoint-inhibitor benefit), and HER2 in adenocarcinoma (directing anti-HER2 therapy) [epidemiology/pathology review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4021327/).
- First-line advanced disease: CheckMate 648 showed nivolumab plus chemotherapy or nivolumab plus ipilimumab improved overall survival vs chemotherapy in advanced ESCC (overall median OS 13.2 vs 10.7 mo, HR 0.74; in PD-L1 ≥1%, nivo+chemo HR 0.54) [CheckMate 648](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2111380).
- KEYNOTE-590 established pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy first-line across esophageal/GEJ cancer, improving median OS 12.4 vs 9.8 mo (HR 0.73), with the largest benefit in PD-L1 CPS ≥10 (HR 0.62) [KEYNOTE-590](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34454674/).
- Adjuvant immunotherapy: CheckMate 577 showed adjuvant nivolumab doubled disease-free survival vs placebo (median DFS 22.4 vs 11.0 mo, HR 0.69) in patients with residual pathologic disease after neoadjuvant chemoradiation and resection, an FDA-approved indication [CheckMate 577](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2032125).
- Notable serious adverse events of the checkpoint-inhibitor regimens include immune-mediated toxicities (pneumonitis, colitis, hepatitis, endocrinopathies) plus chemotherapy-related cytopenias; CheckMate 648 reported no new safety signals beyond known class effects [CheckMate 648](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2111380).
