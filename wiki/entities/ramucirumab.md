# ramucirumab

## Overview

Ramucirumab (Cyramza) is a fully human IgG1 monoclonal antibody that targets the extracellular domain of VEGF receptor-2 (VEGFR-2), blocking angiogenesis. It is approved across multiple gastrointestinal and thoracic malignancies, generally in the second-line setting and usually combined with chemotherapy, and carries a boxed warning for hemorrhage, GI perforation, and impaired wound healing.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: a recombinant human IgG1 antibody that binds VEGFR-2, blocking the VEGF-A, VEGF-C, and VEGF-D ligands and inhibiting ligand-stimulated receptor activation and tumor angiogenesis [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/125477s034lbl.pdf).
- Principal FDA indications: advanced gastric/GEJ adenocarcinoma (single-agent or with paclitaxel, after fluoropyrimidine/platinum), metastatic NSCLC (with docetaxel, after platinum), metastatic colorectal cancer (with FOLFIRI, after bevacizumab/oxaliplatin/fluoropyrimidine), and HCC with AFP >=400 ng/mL after sorafenib [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/125477s034lbl.pdf).
- Gastric cancer: REGARD (single agent) improved median OS 5.2 vs 3.8 mo and RAINBOW (with paclitaxel) improved median OS 9.6 vs 7.4 mo versus placebo [Cyramza approval review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4665044/).
- NSCLC (REVEL): ramucirumab plus docetaxel improved median OS to 10.5 vs 9.1 mo versus placebo plus docetaxel after platinum-based chemotherapy [Cyramza approval review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4665044/).
- HCC (REACH-2): in AFP >=400 ng/mL patients after sorafenib, ramucirumab improved median OS 8.5 vs 7.3 mo (HR 0.71, 95% CI 0.53-0.95, P=0.0199) [REACH-2 analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6791995/).
- Colorectal (RAISE): ramucirumab plus FOLFIRI significantly prolonged OS and PFS versus placebo plus FOLFIRI in the second line [colorectal indication](https://www.ahdbonline.com/articles/cyramza-ramucirumab-receives-new-fda-indication-for-second-line-treatment-of-patients-with-metastatic-colorectal-cancer).
- Safety: boxed warning for hemorrhage (including fatal GI hemorrhage), GI perforation, and impaired wound healing; other notable toxicities include hypertension, infusion-related reactions, and proteinuria [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/125477s034lbl.pdf).
