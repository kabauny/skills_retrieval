# Olaparib

## Overview
Olaparib (Lynparza) is an oral poly(ADP-ribose) polymerase (PARP) inhibitor that exploits synthetic lethality in tumors with homologous recombination repair (HRR) deficiency, most notably BRCA1/2 mutations. It is approved across four tumor types — ovarian, breast, pancreatic, and prostate cancer — primarily in the maintenance or BRCA/HRR-mutated settings. It is given orally at 300 mg twice daily.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: inhibits and traps PARP1, PARP2, and PARP3 on DNA single-strand breaks, blocking their repair; persistent breaks convert to double-strand breaks that are lethal in cells lacking functional homologous-recombination repair (e.g., BRCA-mutated tumors) — synthetic lethality. [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/208558s014lbl.pdf)
- Principal FDA-approved indications: first-line and recurrent maintenance of BRCA-mutated advanced ovarian cancer; gBRCAm HER2-negative metastatic breast cancer; gBRCAm metastatic pancreatic cancer maintenance after platinum; and BRCA/HRR-mutated metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (including with abiraterone). [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/208558s014lbl.pdf)
- Ovarian (SOLO-1/GOG 3004): first-line maintenance in newly diagnosed BRCA-mutated advanced ovarian cancer; PFS HR 0.30 (95% CI 0.23–0.41) and OS HR 0.55 (95% CI 0.40–0.76; P=.0004) at 7-year follow-up. [SOLO-1 (JCO)](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.22.01549)
- Breast (OlympiAD): vs chemotherapy of physician's choice in gBRCAm HER2-negative metastatic breast cancer; overall median OS 19.3 vs 17.1 mo (HR 0.89, not significant), with greater benefit in first-line patients (22.6 vs 14.7 mo, HR 0.55). [OlympiAD (Ann Oncol)](https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(19)31111-1/fulltext)
- Prostate (PROfound): in mCRPC with HRR alterations after a new hormonal agent; in the BRCA subgroup rPFS HR ~0.08 (germline) and OS HR ~0.55 (germline). [PROfound (NEJM)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1911440)
- Pancreatic (POLO): maintenance in gBRCAm metastatic pancreatic cancer after platinum; median PFS 7.4 vs 3.8 mo (HR 0.53, 95% CI 0.35–0.82; P=.004), but no significant OS benefit (19.0 vs 19.2 mo, HR 0.83; P=.35). [POLO (JCO)](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.21.01604)
- Notable/serious adverse events: anemia, neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, nausea, fatigue, and decreased appetite; serious risks include myelodysplastic syndrome/acute myeloid leukemia (MDS/AML), pneumonitis, and venous thromboembolism. [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/208558s014lbl.pdf)
