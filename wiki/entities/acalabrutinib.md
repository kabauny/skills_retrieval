# Acalabrutinib

## Overview
Acalabrutinib (Calquence) is a second-generation, highly selective covalent Bruton tyrosine kinase (BTK) inhibitor used to treat B-cell malignancies. It is FDA-approved for chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL)/small lymphocytic lymphoma (SLL) and for previously treated mantle cell lymphoma (MCL). Its selectivity for BTK aims to reduce off-target toxicities (notably cardiac) seen with first-generation agents.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: forms a covalent bond with BTK, irreversibly inhibiting B-cell receptor signaling required for malignant B-cell proliferation, trafficking, chemotaxis, and adhesion [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/216387Orig2s000Correctedlbl.pdf).
- Principal FDA-approved indications: CLL/SLL (treatment-naive and relapsed/refractory) and adult MCL after at least one prior therapy [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/216387Orig2s000Correctedlbl.pdf).
- ELEVATE-TN (phase 3, treatment-naive CLL): acalabrutinib + obinutuzumab vs obinutuzumab-chlorambucil reduced progression/death risk with PFS HR 0.10 (95% CI 0.06–0.17); acalabrutinib monotherapy HR 0.20 (95% CI 0.13–0.30); median PFS not reached vs 22.6 months [ELEVATE-TN](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32305093/).
- ASCEND (phase 3, relapsed/refractory CLL): single-agent acalabrutinib vs investigator choice (rituximab + idelalisib or bendamustine) improved PFS, with later follow-up showing median PFS not reached vs ~16.8 months [ASCEND](https://www.onclive.com/view/acalabrutinib-in-cll-insights-from-the-elevate-tn-and-ascend-trials).
- Serious adverse events: hemorrhage (Grade ≥3 in ~2%, including CNS, GI, intracranial; some fatal), cytopenias, serious/opportunistic infections, second primary malignancies, and hepatotoxicity [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/216387Orig2s000Correctedlbl.pdf).
- Cardiac arrhythmia: in a pooled analysis of 1764 patients, Grade 3/4 atrial fibrillation/flutter occurred in 2.6% (median onset 17.1 months) [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/216387Orig2s000Correctedlbl.pdf).
- Most common adverse reactions (≥30%): upper respiratory tract infection, diarrhea, headache, and musculoskeletal pain [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/216387Orig2s000Correctedlbl.pdf).
