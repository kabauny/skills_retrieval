# RASolute 302

## Overview

RASolute 302 (NCT06625320) is a global, randomized, open-label phase 3 registrational trial of daraxonrasib (RMC-6236), an oral multi-selective RAS(ON) inhibitor, versus investigator's choice of standard chemotherapy in previously treated metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC). The trial enrolled ~459 patients and met its primary overall-survival endpoint, with daraxonrasib roughly doubling median OS (13.2 vs 6.7 months). Results were presented at the 2026 ASCO Plenary and published simultaneously in the New England Journal of Medicine, and were characterized as a potentially transformative advance in a disease with historically poor outcomes.

## Key facts

- Design: global, randomized, open-label phase 3; patients with metastatic PDAC after 1 prior line of therapy were randomized 1:1 to daraxonrasib 300 mg orally once daily (n=228) vs investigator's choice of standard chemotherapy (n=231) [[RASolute 302, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2605555)].
- Comparator was investigator's choice of one of four regimens: gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel, mFOLFIRINOX, nanoliposomal irinotecan + 5-FU, or FOLFOX [Cancer Therapy Advisor](https://www.cancertherapyadvisor.com/reports/rasolute-302-daraxonrasib-survival-orr-metastatic-pdac/).
- Primary endpoint met: median overall survival 13.2 months with daraxonrasib vs 6.7 months with chemotherapy (HR 0.40; P < .0001), a 60% reduction in risk of death [CancerNetwork](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/daraxonrasib-shows-unprecedented-survival-in-pretreated-pancreatic-cancer).
- Progression-free survival: 7.3 vs 3.5 months in the RAS G12 population (HR 0.45) and 7.2 vs 3.6 months overall (HR 0.49) [Cancer Therapy Advisor](https://www.cancertherapyadvisor.com/reports/rasolute-302-daraxonrasib-survival-orr-metastatic-pdac/).
- Objective response rate: 33.2% vs 11.8% (RAS G12) and 31.6% vs 11.2% (overall), favoring daraxonrasib [Cancer Therapy Advisor](https://www.cancertherapyadvisor.com/reports/rasolute-302-daraxonrasib-survival-orr-metastatic-pdac/).
- Benefit was seen across the intent-to-treat population, including various RAS mutations and RAS wild-type disease, and patients reported delayed deterioration in cancer-related pain and quality of life [AJMC](https://www.ajmc.com/view/rasolute-302-brings-a-transformative-moment-on-pancreatic-cancer-a-60-improvement-in-overall-survival).
- Most common daraxonrasib treatment-related adverse events were rash, diarrhea, stomatitis, nausea, vomiting, fatigue, anemia, decreased appetite, and paronychia; treatment-related discontinuation was 1.2% (daraxonrasib) vs 11.2% (chemotherapy) [Cancer Therapy Advisor](https://www.cancertherapyadvisor.com/reports/rasolute-302-daraxonrasib-survival-orr-metastatic-pdac/).
