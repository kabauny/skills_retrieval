# Head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC)

## Overview
HNSCC comprises squamous cancers of the upper aerodigestive tract — chiefly the oral cavity, oropharynx, larynx, and hypopharynx — and is among the most common cancers worldwide. Two distinct etiologies dominate: tobacco/alcohol-driven disease and human papillomavirus (HPV)-associated oropharyngeal cancer, the latter rising in incidence and carrying a markedly better prognosis. HPV/p16 status and PD-L1 expression are the central biomarkers shaping staging, treatment intensity, and systemic therapy selection.

## Key facts
- HNSCC is roughly the seventh most common cancer globally with about 600,000 new cases per year, arising most often in the larynx, pharynx, and oral cavity; squamous histology predominates across these sites [Epidemiology review (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31011837/).
- Approximately 70% of oropharyngeal cancers are HPV-related; HPV/p16-positive disease occurs in younger, often non-smoking patients and has significantly better locoregional control and survival than HPV-negative disease [HPV molecular landscape (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7487583/).
- The AJCC 8th edition (2016) created a separate staging system for p16-positive oropharyngeal cancer to reflect its favorable prognosis, downstaging many tumors relative to HPV-negative disease [AJCC 8th edition staging analysis (Oncologist)](https://academic.oup.com/oncolo/article/27/1/48/6516308).
- Treatment intent is set by stage and resectability: early-stage disease is cured with single-modality surgery or radiation, while locally advanced (stage III–IV) unresected disease standardly receives concurrent cisplatin chemoradiation (level IA evidence) [Staging and approach (NCBI Bookshelf)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK608597/).
- In first-line recurrent/metastatic HNSCC, KEYNOTE-048 established pembrolizumab: pembrolizumab monotherapy improved OS in PD-L1 CPS ≥20 (HR 0.61) and CPS ≥1 (HR 0.74) populations and was noninferior overall (HR 0.81) vs the EXTREME regimen [KEYNOTE-048 CPS subgroup (JCO)](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.21.02198).
- Pembrolizumab plus platinum chemotherapy prolonged OS across CPS ≥20 (HR 0.63), CPS ≥1 (HR 0.65), and the total population (HR 0.72) at 5-year follow-up; in CPS ≥20, median OS was 14.7 vs 11.0 months (HR 0.60) vs EXTREME [KEYNOTE-048 5-year update (JCO)](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.21.02508).
- PD-L1 CPS thus gates first-line systemic choice — pembrolizumab monotherapy for biomarker-enriched disease versus pembrolizumab-chemotherapy for higher disease burden — making PD-L1 testing a routine treatment-selection step in recurrent/metastatic disease [KEYNOTE-048 CPS subgroup (JCO)](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.21.02198).
