# Melanoma

## Overview
Melanoma is an aggressive malignancy arising from melanocytes, most commonly in the skin. Cutaneous melanoma is the dominant form, with subtypes including superficial spreading, nodular (the deadliest), lentigo maligna, and acral lentiginous. Modern management is defined by surgical staging with sentinel lymph node assessment, BRAF mutation testing, and the central role of checkpoint immunotherapy and BRAF/MEK-targeted therapy in resectable and advanced disease.

## Key facts
- Cutaneous melanoma is the most lethal common skin cancer; histologic subtypes include superficial spreading, nodular (most aggressive), lentigo maligna, and acral lentiginous, each with distinct mutation patterns (BRAF most frequent in nodular/lentigo maligna) [Melanoma subtypes review](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12044287/).
- Staging (AJCC) by Breslow thickness, ulceration, and sentinel node status sets intent: thin localized lesions are cured by wide excision, while nodal (stage III) and metastatic (stage IV) disease drive adjuvant or systemic therapy decisions [NCCN Cutaneous Melanoma Guidelines](https://jnccn.org/abstract/journals/jnccn/22/5/article-p290.xml).
- BRAF V600 mutation status is the pivotal biomarker gating targeted therapy; LDH and stage/ulceration further stratify prognosis and treatment intensity [NCCN Cutaneous Melanoma Guidelines](https://jnccn.org/abstract/journals/jnccn/22/5/article-p290.xml).
- First-line advanced disease relies on PD-1 checkpoint blockade: in CheckMate 067, nivolumab plus ipilimumab gave a 10-year OS HR of 0.53 (95% CI 0.44-0.65) and nivolumab alone HR 0.63 vs ipilimumab [CheckMate 067 NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1709684).
- In KEYNOTE-006, pembrolizumab improved OS vs ipilimumab (median OS 32.7 vs 15.9 mo; HR 0.70, 95% CI 0.58-0.83) in unresectable stage III/IV melanoma [KEYNOTE-006 Annals of Oncology](https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(24)03910-3/fulltext).
- For BRAF V600-mutant disease, BRAF/MEK inhibitor combinations (dabrafenib/trametinib, vemurafenib/cobimetinib, encorafenib/binimetinib) are options; COMBI-d showed dabrafenib plus trametinib prolonged PFS vs dabrafenib alone (11.0 vs 8.8 mo, HR 0.67) [COMBI-d 5-year outcomes](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31166680/).
- Adjuvant therapy for resected stage III BRAF V600-mutant melanoma: COMBI-AD final analysis showed adjuvant dabrafenib plus trametinib markedly prolonged relapse-free survival (median 93.1 vs 16.6 mo) and reduced risk of death by ~20% vs placebo [COMBI-AD final results](https://ascopost.com/issues/september-25-2024/adjuvant-dabrafenib-plus-trametinib-for-stage-iii-braf-v600-mutated-melanoma-final-results-of-the-combi-ad-trial).
- Nivolumab plus relatlimab (anti-LAG-3) is an NCCN-listed neoadjuvant/advanced option, reflecting the expanding immunotherapy landscape across resectable stage III and metastatic disease [NCCN Cutaneous Melanoma Guidelines](https://jnccn.org/abstract/journals/jnccn/22/5/article-p290.xml).
