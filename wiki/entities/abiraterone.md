# Abiraterone

## Overview
Abiraterone (as abiraterone acetate, Zytiga) is an oral androgen-biosynthesis inhibitor that blocks CYP17, the enzyme required for testosterone synthesis in testicular, adrenal, and prostatic tumor tissue. Given with prednisone, it is a backbone agent in advanced prostate cancer, used in both metastatic castration-resistant (mCRPC) and metastatic high-risk castration-sensitive (mCSPC) disease.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: abiraterone acetate is converted in vivo to abiraterone, which inhibits 17α-hydroxylase/C17,20-lyase (CYP17), blocking androgen (DHEA, androstenedione, testosterone) biosynthesis; co-administered prednisone offsets the resulting mineralocorticoid excess [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2021/202379s035lbl.pdf).
- Approved (with prednisone) for metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC) and metastatic high-risk castration-sensitive prostate cancer (mCSPC); standard dose 1000 mg once daily with prednisone 5 mg twice daily [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2021/202379s035lbl.pdf).
- COU-AA-301 (post-docetaxel mCRPC): median OS 14.8 vs 10.9 mo, HR 0.65 (p<0.0001) for abiraterone+prednisone vs placebo+prednisone [COU-AA-301](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanonc/article/PIIS1470-2045(12)70379-0/abstract).
- COU-AA-302 (chemotherapy-naive mCRPC): final OS HR 0.81 (95% CI 0.70-0.93, p=0.0033) [COU-AA-302](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanonc/article/PIIS1470-2045(14)71205-7/abstract).
- LATITUDE (newly diagnosed high-risk mCSPC): median OS 53.3 vs 36.5 mo, HR 0.66 (p<0.0001) for abiraterone+prednisone added to ADT vs placebo+ADT [LATITUDE](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1470204519300828).
- Notable adverse events reflect mineralocorticoid excess and CYP17 blockade: hypokalemia, fluid retention/edema, hypertension, and hepatotoxicity (transaminase elevations requiring monitoring) [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2021/202379s035lbl.pdf).
- Also indicated with prednisone for deleterious or suspected deleterious BRCA-mutated mCRPC (e.g., in combination with niraparib as Akeega) [Akeega FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2023/216793s000lbl.pdf).
