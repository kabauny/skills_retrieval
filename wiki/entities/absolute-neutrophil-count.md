# Absolute Neutrophil Count


## Brief

A measure of the total number of neutrophils in the blood, which are a type of white blood cell essential for fighting infection.

## Why this matters

It is a critical blood parameter monitored during chemotherapy to assess bone marrow suppression, risk of infection, and guide treatment decisions.
