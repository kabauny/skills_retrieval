# Pemigatinib

## Overview
Pemigatinib (Pemazyre) is an oral, selective small-molecule inhibitor of fibroblast growth factor receptors FGFR1, FGFR2, and FGFR3. It received FDA accelerated approval in April 2020 as the first targeted therapy for previously treated, unresectable locally advanced or metastatic cholangiocarcinoma harboring an FGFR2 fusion or other rearrangement. Approval was based on the single-arm phase 2 FIGHT-202 trial.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: selective oral kinase inhibitor of FGFR1, 2, and 3, blocking constitutive FGFR2 signaling driven by oncogenic fusions/rearrangements [FDA approval summary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36206041/).
- Principal FDA indication: adults with previously treated, unresectable locally advanced or metastatic cholangiocarcinoma with an FGFR2 fusion or other rearrangement, detected by an FDA-approved test (accelerated approval, April 17, 2020) [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-pemigatinib-cholangiocarcinoma-fgfr2-rearrangement-or-fusion).
- Pivotal trial FIGHT-202 (phase 2, open-label, single-arm) in 107-108 FGFR2 fusion/rearrangement patients: objective response rate 37.0% (3 complete, 37 partial responses) [FIGHT-202 final results](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11190465/).
- FIGHT-202 cohort A durability and survival: median duration of response 9.1 months, median progression-free survival 7.0 months, median overall survival 17.5 months [FIGHT-202 final results](https://www.esmoopen.com/article/S2059-7029(24)01257-2/fulltext).
- Dosing: 13.5 mg orally once daily for 14 consecutive days followed by 7 days off in 21-day cycles [FDA approval summary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36206041/).
- Serious toxicity: hyperphosphatemia leading to soft-tissue mineralization/calcinosis, and retinal pigment epithelial detachment (RPED in 11% of patients, Grade 3-4 in 1.3%; median onset 56 days) requiring serial OCT monitoring [Pemazyre prescribing information](https://www.drugs.com/pro/pemazyre.html).
- Most common adverse reactions (≥20%): hyperphosphatemia, alopecia, diarrhea, nail toxicity, fatigue, dysgeusia, nausea, and dry eye [Pemazyre prescribing information](https://www.drugs.com/pro/pemazyre.html).
