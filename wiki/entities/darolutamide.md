# Darolutamide

## Overview
Darolutamide (NUBEQA) is a structurally distinct, highly potent oral androgen receptor (AR) inhibitor with low blood-brain barrier penetration and limited drug-drug interaction potential. It is FDA-approved for nonmetastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (nmCRPC) and, in combination with docetaxel, for metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer (mHSPC). Both indications are given alongside continued androgen-deprivation therapy (ADT).

## Key facts
- Mechanism: competitive AR antagonist that blocks androgen binding, AR nuclear translocation, and AR-mediated transcription; structurally distinct chemotype with low CNS penetration and reduced DDI potential ([FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-darolutamide-tablets-metastatic-hormone-sensitive-prostate-cancer)).
- Indication 1 — nmCRPC: approved in 2019 for nonmetastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer with continued ADT ([NEJM ARAMIS](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2001342)).
- Indication 2 — mHSPC: approved August 2022 in combination with docetaxel for metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer ([FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-darolutamide-tablets-metastatic-hormone-sensitive-prostate-cancer)).
- Pivotal trial ARAMIS (nmCRPC): metastasis-free survival 40.4 vs 18.4 mo, HR 0.41; final OS HR 0.69 (P=0.003), 3-year OS 83% vs 77% ([NEJM ARAMIS](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2001342)).
- Pivotal trial ARASENS (mHSPC): darolutamide + docetaxel + ADT reduced risk of death 32.5%, OS HR 0.68 (95% CI 0.57-0.80, P<0.001); median OS not reached vs 48.9 mo for placebo ([NEJM ARASENS](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2119115)).
- Safety: generally well tolerated with adverse-event rates similar to placebo; notable events include fatigue, rash, and (in the docetaxel combination) increased neutropenia and hematologic toxicity ([NEJM ARASENS](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2119115)).
