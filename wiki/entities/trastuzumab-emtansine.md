# Trastuzumab emtansine (T-DM1)

## Overview

Trastuzumab emtansine (T-DM1, Kadcyla) is an antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) that links the anti-HER2 antibody trastuzumab via a stable thioether linker to DM1, a maytansine-derived microtubule inhibitor. After HER2 binding and internalization, lysosomal degradation releases DM1 intracellularly, disrupting the microtubule network while retaining trastuzumab's HER2 signaling blockade and antibody-dependent cytotoxicity. It is FDA-approved for HER2-positive breast cancer in both the metastatic (previously treated) and adjuvant (residual invasive disease after neoadjuvant therapy) settings.

## Key facts

- **Mechanism:** HER2-directed ADC delivering the cytotoxic maytansinoid DM1 (tubulin inhibitor) selectively to HER2-overexpressing cells, combining targeted chemotherapy delivery with HER2 pathway inhibition [[FDA label]](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2013/125427lbl.pdf).
- **Metastatic indication established by EMILIA:** in patients previously treated with trastuzumab and a taxane, T-DM1 improved median OS to 29.9 vs 25.9 months (HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.64-0.88) and reduced grade 3+ adverse events (48% vs 60%) compared with capecitabine + lapatinib [EMILIA final OS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28526536/).
- **Adjuvant indication established by KATHERINE:** in 1,486 patients with residual invasive HER2+ disease after neoadjuvant taxane/trastuzumab, adjuvant T-DM1 (3.6 mg/kg IV q3w × 14) roughly halved the risk of invasive disease or death vs trastuzumab (3-year IDFS 88.3% vs 77.0%, HR 0.50) [[katherine-trial]] [NEJM KATHERINE](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1814017).
- **Long-term KATHERINE benefit:** at 8.4-year median follow-up the IDFS benefit was sustained (HR 0.54) with 7-year IDFS 80.8% vs 67.1% and improved OS (89.1% vs 84.4%), confirming a durable adjuvant survival advantage [ASCO Post KATHERINE long-term](https://ascopost.com/news/january-2025/adjuvant-t-dm1-in-patients-with-her2-positive-breast-cancer-long-term-results-of-the-katherine-trial/).
- **Boxed warning:** hepatotoxicity (including liver failure and death), cardiac toxicity (reduced LVEF), and embryo-fetal toxicity; monitor hepatic function and LVEF before initiation and dosing [[FDA boxed warning]](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2014/125427s033lbl.pdf).
- **Common adverse events:** thrombocytopenia, fatigue, elevated transaminases, nausea, musculoskeletal pain, headache, and peripheral neuropathy; management may require dose interruption, reduction, or discontinuation [[FDA label]](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2013/125427lbl.pdf).
- **Emerging adjuvant comparison:** [[destiny-breast05]] reported lower 3-year IDFS for T-DM1 (83.7%) vs [[trastuzumab-deruxtecan]] (92.4%, HR 0.47), though T-DM1 retains a much lower interstitial lung disease risk, favoring it in patients with baseline pulmonary risk [[mrd-her2-breast-cancer-residual-disease-2026]].
