# Atezolizumab

## Overview

Atezolizumab (TECENTRIQ) is a humanized IgG1 monoclonal antibody and immune checkpoint inhibitor that targets programmed death-ligand 1 (PD-L1). By blocking PD-L1 it restores anti-tumor T-cell activity, and it is approved across thoracic, hepatobiliary, urothelial, and breast malignancies, most often in combination regimens. It is a backbone of first-line chemo-immunotherapy in metastatic non-small cell lung cancer and extensive-stage small cell lung cancer.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: humanized anti-PD-L1 IgG1 monoclonal antibody that blocks PD-L1 binding to both PD-1 and B7.1, releasing inhibition of the anti-tumor T-cell response; it is Fc-engineered to avoid antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/761034s018lbl.pdf).
- Principal indications include first-line metastatic non-squamous NSCLC (with bevacizumab/chemotherapy), first-line extensive-stage SCLC (with carboplatin/etoposide), unresectable hepatocellular carcinoma (with bevacizumab), adjuvant stage II-IIIA NSCLC with PD-L1 >=1%, and PD-L1-high triple-negative breast cancer [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/761034s018lbl.pdf).
- IMpower150 (first-line metastatic non-squamous NSCLC): atezolizumab + bevacizumab + carboplatin/paclitaxel cut risk of death versus bevacizumab/chemotherapy alone (OS HR 0.78; median OS 19.2 vs 14.7 mo) [IMpower150 (NEJM)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1716948).
- IMpower133 (first-line extensive-stage SCLC): adding atezolizumab to carboplatin/etoposide improved survival (OS HR 0.70, 95% CI 0.54-0.91, p=0.007; median OS 12.3 vs 10.3 mo) [IMpower133 (JCO)](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.20.01055).
- IMbrave150 (unresectable HCC): atezolizumab + bevacizumab versus sorafenib reduced death by 42% (OS HR 0.58; median OS 19.2 vs 13.4 mo) with ORR 30% vs 11% [IMbrave150 (NEJM)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1915745).
- Notable toxicities are immune-mediated adverse reactions: pneumonitis, colitis, hepatitis, endocrinopathies (thyroid, adrenal insufficiency, type 1 diabetes, hypophysitis), nephritis, and severe infusion/skin reactions, requiring corticosteroids and treatment interruption [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/761034s018lbl.pdf).
