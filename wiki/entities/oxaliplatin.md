# Oxaliplatin

## Overview

Oxaliplatin is a third-generation platinum (DACH-platinum) cytotoxic agent used in combination with fluoropyrimidines (5-fluorouracil/leucovorin) to treat colorectal and other gastrointestinal cancers. It is a cornerstone of the FOLFOX and FOLFIRINOX regimens, given in both the curative adjuvant setting and the palliative metastatic setting. Its signature toxicity is a cumulative, cold-sensitive sensory peripheral neuropathy.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: oxaliplatin undergoes nonenzymatic conversion to reactive DACH-platinum species that form inter- and intrastrand Pt-DNA crosslinks (chiefly at the N7 of guanine), inhibiting DNA replication and transcription [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/022160s016lbl.pdf).
- Principal FDA-approved indications, always in combination with infusional 5-fluorouracil/leucovorin: adjuvant treatment of stage III colon cancer after complete resection of the primary tumor, and treatment of advanced (metastatic) colorectal cancer [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/022160s016lbl.pdf).
- Pivotal adjuvant trial MOSAIC: adding oxaliplatin to 5-FU/LV (FOLFOX4 vs LV5FU2) improved 5-year disease-free survival 73.3% vs 67.4% (HR 0.80; 95% CI 0.68–0.93; P=.003) [MOSAIC (NEJM)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa032709).
- MOSAIC overall-survival benefit was concentrated in stage III disease: 6-year OS 72.9% vs 68.7% (HR 0.80; P=.023), sustained at 10 years (stage III OS 67.1% vs 59.0%; HR 0.80; P=.016) [MOSAIC 10-yr (JCO)](https://ascopubs.org/doi/abs/10.1200/JCO.2015.63.4238).
- Signature toxicity is peripheral neuropathy: an acute, cold-triggered, reversible sensory neuropathy within hours/days of dosing, plus a cumulative dose-limiting persistent sensory neuropathy [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/022160s016lbl.pdf).
- Other serious/common adverse events: grade 3–4 hypersensitivity reactions including fatal anaphylaxis (2–3% in colon cancer), neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, anemia, nausea/vomiting, diarrhea, fatigue, stomatitis, and transaminase/alkaline phosphatase elevations [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/022160s016lbl.pdf).
