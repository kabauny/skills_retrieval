# TAILORx trial

## Overview

TAILORx was a practice-changing phase 3 trial (N=10,273 enrolled) that established the [[oncotype-dx-recurrence-score]] (21-gene assay) as the primary genomic decision tool in HR+/HER2−, **node-negative** early breast cancer. Its central randomized question — among women with intermediate Recurrence Score (RS) 11–25 — found that endocrine therapy alone was **non-inferior** to chemoendocrine therapy, sparing most women adjuvant chemotherapy. An exploratory subgroup analysis nonetheless identified a real, if modest, chemotherapy benefit in younger (≤50) women with higher intermediate scores.

## Key facts

- **Design / population:** prospective phase 3 trial enrolling 10,273 women with HR+, HER2−, **axillary node-negative** early breast cancer across 1,182 sites; treatment assigned by Oncotype DX RS — RS 0–10 endocrine alone, **RS 11–25 randomized** to endocrine vs chemoendocrine therapy, RS ≥26 chemoendocrine [[oncotype-dx-recurrence-score]] [NCI press release](https://www.cancer.gov/news-events/press-releases/2018/tailorx-breast-cancer-chemotherapy).
- **Randomized arm size:** the RS 11–25 randomization included 6,711 women (ages 18–75), the pivotal comparison of the trial [NEJM 2018, Sparano](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1804710).
- **Primary endpoint met (non-inferiority):** endocrine therapy alone was non-inferior to chemoendocrine therapy for invasive disease-free survival (IDFS), **HR 1.08 (95% CI 0.94–1.24; P=0.26)** [ASCO Post, TAILORx](https://ascopost.com/issues/june-10-2018/tailorx-endocrine-therapy-alone-in-intermediate-risk-breast-cancer/).
- **Absolute outcomes nearly identical:** 9-year IDFS was **83.3% (endocrine alone) vs 84.3% (chemoendocrine)**; 5-year IDFS 92.8% vs 93.1% [NCI press release](https://www.cancer.gov/news-events/press-releases/2018/tailorx-breast-cancer-chemotherapy).
- **Premenopausal exception (≤50 yr):** among women ≤50, chemotherapy benefit emerged at higher intermediate scores — roughly **9% fewer IDFS events for RS 16–20** and **6% fewer for RS 21–25**; the chemo benefit varied jointly with RS and age [ASCO Post, TAILORx](https://ascopost.com/issues/june-10-2018/tailorx-endocrine-therapy-alone-in-intermediate-risk-breast-cancer/).
- **Headline clinical implication:** the assay may spare adjuvant chemotherapy in up to ~85% of node-negative HR+/HER2− patients — especially women >50 with RS ≤25 and women ≤50 with RS ≤15 [NEJM 2018, Sparano](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1804710).
- **Significance / current status:** results (NEJM, June 2018) are reflected in NCCN and ASCO guidance and made Oncotype DX standard of care for chemo decisions in node-negative HR+/HER2− disease [AJMC summary](https://www.ajmc.com/view/phase-3-tailorx-results-confirm-chemotherapy-unnecessary-in-70-of-women-with-earlystage-breast-cancer).
- **Distinct from [[rxponder-trial]]:** RxPONDER addressed node-positive (1–3 nodes) disease; the two trials answer different questions for different populations. See [[node-negative-vs-node-positive-genomic-trial-scoping]].
- **Relevance to the Q4 case (39yo, RS 22, Grade 3, 2.5 cm, N0):** sits squarely in the ≤50, RS 21–25 intermediate subgroup with high-risk features where TAILORx showed a measurable chemotherapy benefit [[premenopausal-intermediate-oncotype-2026]].
