# Sonrotoclax

## Overview

Sonrotoclax (BGB-11417, brand name Beqalzi; BeiGene/BeOne Medicines) is a next-generation, potent and selective small-molecule BCL-2 inhibitor that induces apoptosis in malignant B cells by blocking the anti-apoptotic BCL-2 protein. In May 2026 the FDA granted it accelerated approval for relapsed/refractory mantle cell lymphoma (MCL) after at least two prior lines including a BTK inhibitor — the first new BCL-2 inhibitor approved in roughly a decade. It is also under broad development in CLL, where the CELESTIAL-TNCLL phase 3 program pairs it with zanubrutinib.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: selective BCL-2 inhibitor that restores apoptosis in BCL-2-dependent B-cell malignancies; positioned as a more potent next-generation successor to venetoclax [FDA approves sonrotoclax, OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/fda-approves-sonrotoclax-for-relapsed-refractory-mantle-cell-lymphoma).
- FDA accelerated approval (May 2026) for adults with relapsed/refractory MCL after ≥2 prior systemic therapies including a BTK inhibitor — the only BCL-2 inhibitor approved for this setting [Sonrotoclax accelerated approval, AJMC](https://www.ajmc.com/view/sonrotoclax-granted-accelerated-approval-for-r-r-mantle-cell-lymphoma).
- Pivotal trial BGB-11417-201 (phase 1/2, n=103 R/R MCL with prior anti-CD20 therapy and a BTK inhibitor) showed an overall response rate of 52% [FDA approves sonrotoclax, Targeted Oncology](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/fda-approves-next-gen-bcl2-inhibitor-sonrotoclax-for-r-r-mantle-cell-lymphoma).
- Responses were rapid (median 1.9 months) with a median duration of response of 15.8 months at 11.9-month median follow-up [FDA Approves Sonrotoclax for MCL, OncologyNewsCentral](https://www.oncologynewscentral.com/drugs/info/fda-approves-sonrotoclax-for-mantle-cell-lymphoma).
- Dosing uses a 4-week ramp-up to mitigate tumor lysis syndrome (TLS), reaching the recommended 320 mg once daily [Sonrotoclax approval, Pharmacy Times](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/sonrotoclax-approval-delivers-a-long-awaited-new-option-for-relapsed-or-refractory-mantle-cell-lymphoma).
- Serious adverse events occurred in 37% of patients; the most common serious event was pneumonia (10%), and the TLS risk drives the ramp-up schedule [FDA approves sonrotoclax, OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/fda-approves-sonrotoclax-for-relapsed-refractory-mantle-cell-lymphoma).
- CELESTIAL-TNCLL (BGB-11417-301; NCT06073821) is an ongoing randomized, open-label phase 3 trial of sonrotoclax + zanubrutinib vs venetoclax + obinutuzumab in treatment-naive CLL [CELESTIAL-TNCLL, JCO](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2024.42.16_suppl.TPS7087).
- In treatment-naive CLL phase 1/1b data (BGB-11417-101), sonrotoclax + zanubrutinib produced high rates of undetectable MRD with no observed TLS or cardiac toxicity; neutropenia was the most common grade ≥3 event [Sonrotoclax + zanubrutinib frontline CLL, Blood](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/144/Supplement%201/1012/531224/Sonrotoclax-and-Zanubrutinib-as-Frontline).
