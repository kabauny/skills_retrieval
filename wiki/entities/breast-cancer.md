# Breast cancer

## Overview
Breast cancer is the most common cancer in women and is not one disease but a family of biologically distinct subtypes defined by hormone receptor (ER/PR) and HER2 status. Treatment intent and regimen are set first by stage/resectability and then by these receptor-defined subtypes, with additional genomic biomarkers (recurrence scores, germline BRCA, PIK3CA) refining systemic therapy decisions. The three principal therapeutic groups are hormone receptor-positive/HER2-negative (most common), HER2-positive, and triple-negative breast cancer.

## Key facts
- Subtypes are grouped by immunohistochemistry into ER+/PR+, HER2+, and triple-negative (TNBC, lacking all three); gene-expression "intrinsic" subtypes are luminal A, luminal B, HER2-enriched, and basal-like, and HR+/HER2- is the most common subtype ([NCBI Bookshelf](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK583808/)).
- Staging and resectability frame intent: localized disease is treated with surgery +/- radiation and (neo)adjuvant systemic therapy aimed at cure, whereas metastatic disease is incurable and managed with sequential subtype-directed systemic lines ([NCCN Breast Cancer Guidelines](https://jnccn.org/view/journals/jnccn/22/5/article-p331.xml)).
- ER/PR by IHC gate endocrine therapy: positive tumors receive antiestrogens/aromatase inhibitors (and, in advanced disease, CDK4/6 inhibitors), while ER/PR define the luminal subtypes used for genomic risk stratification ([NCBI Bookshelf](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK583808/)).
- HER2 by IHC/ISH gates anti-HER2 therapy: ASCO-CAP scores IHC as 0/1+/2+/3+ with 3+ positive, 2+ equivocal (reflexed to ISH), and 0/1+ negative; IHC 3+ or ISH-amplified tumors receive trastuzumab-based therapy ([ASCO-CAP HER2 Guideline 2023](https://www.cap.org/protocols-and-guidelines/cap-guidelines/current-cap-guidelines/recommendations-for-human-epidermal-growth-factor-2-testing-in-breast-cancer)).
- "HER2-low" (IHC 1+ or 2+/ISH-negative) is now an actionable category: DESTINY-Breast04 showed trastuzumab deruxtecan improved median OS to ~22.9 vs 16.8 months over chemotherapy in HER2-low metastatic disease ([DESTINY-Breast04, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2203690)).
- Ki-67 (proliferation) and 21-gene recurrence scores guide adjuvant chemotherapy in HR+/HER2-, node-negative disease: TAILORx showed recurrence score 0-25 derives no chemotherapy benefit (endocrine therapy alone), while >25 benefits from added chemotherapy ([Oncotype DX clinical evidence](https://www.oncotypedxtest.com/healthcare-professionals/clinical-evidence); [RxPONDER/21-gene node-positive, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2108873)).
- Germline BRCA1/2 gates PARP inhibitors: OlympiA showed adjuvant olaparib improved invasive disease-free survival (HR 0.58) in high-risk, HER2-negative, gBRCA-mutated early breast cancer after chemotherapy ([OlympiA / FDA approval](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/fda-approves-olaparib-as-adjuvant-treatment-for-brca-mutated-her2-negative-breast-cancer)).
- PIK3CA mutations (~40% of HR+/HER2- tumors) gate PI3K-pathway inhibitors: alpelisib plus fulvestrant is FDA-approved for PIK3CA-mutated HR+/HER2- advanced disease after endocrine progression (PFS 11.0 vs 5.7 months in SOLAR-1) ([SOLAR-1, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1813904); [FDA Approval Summary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33168657/)).
