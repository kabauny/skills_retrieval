# Trametinib

## Overview
Trametinib (Mekinist) is an oral, reversible, highly selective allosteric inhibitor of MEK1 and MEK2, kinases downstream of BRAF in the MAPK/ERK signaling pathway. It is used almost exclusively in combination with the BRAF inhibitor dabrafenib to treat BRAF V600E/K-mutated cancers, where dual blockade overcomes paradoxical pathway reactivation seen with BRAF inhibition alone. Approved indications span metastatic and adjuvant melanoma, BRAF V600E NSCLC, anaplastic thyroid cancer, and tissue-agnostic BRAF V600E solid tumors.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: reversible, selective allosteric inhibitor of MEK1/MEK2, blocking constitutive MAPK pathway activation driven by upstream BRAF/RAS mutations; combined with dabrafenib to suppress feedback reactivation of ERK signaling [FDA label](https://www.novartis.com/us-en/sites/novartis_us/files/mekinist.pdf).
- Principal indications: BRAF V600E/K unresectable/metastatic melanoma and stage III adjuvant melanoma (with dabrafenib), BRAF V600E metastatic NSCLC, BRAF V600E anaplastic thyroid cancer, and tissue-agnostic BRAF V600E solid tumors [NCI](https://www.cancer.gov/news-events/cancer-currents-blog/2022/fda-dabrafenib-trametinib-braf-solid-tumors).
- Pivotal melanoma trial COMBI-d (phase 3, n=423): dabrafenib + trametinib vs dabrafenib alone improved median PFS to 11.0 vs 8.8 months and median OS to 25.1 vs 18.7 months [COMBI-d NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1412690).
- NSCLC: phase 2 study (NCT01336634) of dabrafenib + trametinib in BRAF V600E NSCLC showed ORR 63.2% with median PFS 9.7 months in pretreated patients and ORR 64% with median PFS 10.9 months in treatment-naive patients [JTO](https://www.jto.org/article/S1556-0864(21)02403-5/fulltext).
- Tissue-agnostic activity: phase 2 ROAR basket trial demonstrated durable responses across multiple BRAF V600E rare cancers (e.g., biliary tract, low/high-grade glioma), supporting the 2022 tumor-agnostic approval [ROAR Nature Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-023-02321-8).
- Serious adverse events: cardiomyopathy/LVEF decline (monitor LVEF at baseline, 1 month, then every 2-3 months), retinal vein occlusion and retinal pigment epithelial detachment, interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis, hemorrhage, and serious skin toxicities [FDA label](https://www.novartis.com/us-en/sites/novartis_us/files/mekinist.pdf).
- Common adverse reactions include rash, diarrhea, peripheral edema, hypertension, and pyrexia (the latter especially with the dabrafenib combination) [FDA label](https://www.novartis.com/us-en/sites/novartis_us/files/mekinist.pdf).
