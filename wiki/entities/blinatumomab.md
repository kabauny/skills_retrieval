# Blinatumomab

## Overview
Blinatumomab (BLINCYTO) is a CD19-directed CD3 T-cell engager (BiTE) bispecific antibody construct that redirects cytotoxic T cells to lyse CD19-positive B cells. It is approved for B-cell precursor acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL) across the relapsed/refractory, measurable residual disease (MRD)-positive, and consolidation settings. It carries a boxed warning for cytokine release syndrome and neurological toxicities.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: a bispecific T-cell engager that simultaneously binds CD19 on B cells and CD3 on T cells, forming a cytolytic synapse that drives T-cell activation and CD19-positive cell lysis independent of antigen specificity [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/125557s029lbl.pdf).
- Principal FDA indications: CD19-positive B-cell precursor ALL — relapsed/refractory disease (adults and children ≥1 month), MRD-positive disease (MRD ≥0.1% in first/second remission), and Philadelphia-chromosome-negative disease in the consolidation phase regardless of MRD status [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/125557s029lbl.pdf).
- Pivotal trial TOWER (NEJM 2017, N=405): in relapsed/refractory Ph-negative B-cell ALL, blinatumomab improved median overall survival to 7.7 vs 4.0 months versus standard chemotherapy (HR 0.71, p=0.012) [TOWER](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1609783).
- TOWER complete remission rates were higher with blinatumomab than chemotherapy (34% vs 16%) [FDA supplemental approval](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6291336/).
- Pivotal MRD trial BLAST (Blood 2018, single-arm phase 2): 78% of evaluable patients achieved a complete MRD response after one cycle, supporting approval in MRD-positive ALL [BLAST](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6027091/).
- BLAST long-term follow-up (median 59.8 mo): complete MRD responders had markedly longer overall survival (not reached vs 14.4 mo for non-responders, p=0.002), suggesting MRD clearance can be curative [BLAST long-term](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32619115/).
- Boxed warning: cytokine release syndrome (CRS) — which may be life-threatening or fatal, median onset ~2 days from infusion — and neurological toxicities including immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome (ICANS) [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/125557s029lbl.pdf).
