# POTOMAC Trial

## Overview

POTOMAC (NCT03528694) is a randomized, open-label, multicenter phase 3 trial evaluating the PD-L1 inhibitor durvalumab added to Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) in patients with BCG-naïve, high-risk non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC) after transurethral resection. It is the first immunotherapy combination to show a disease-free survival benefit in this setting and provided the pivotal evidence for the May 2026 FDA approval of durvalumab plus BCG.

## Key facts

- Design: randomized 1:1:1 (N=1,018) to durvalumab + BCG induction and maintenance (n=339), durvalumab + BCG induction only (n=339), or BCG induction and maintenance alone (n=340) [[POTOMAC, Lancet](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41115436/)].
- Population: adults with BCG-naïve, high-risk NMIBC (high-grade Ta, T1, and/or carcinoma in situ) following TURBT [[FDA approval](https://ascopost.com/news/may-2026/fda-approves-durvalumab-plus-bcg-for-high-risk-nmibc/)].
- Primary endpoint met: durvalumab + BCG induction/maintenance reduced risk of disease recurrence or death by 32% vs BCG alone (DFS HR 0.68, 95% CI 0.50–0.93; p=0.0154) [[POTOMAC, Lancet](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41115436/)].
- At median follow-up of 60.7 months, median DFS was not reached in either arm; DFS events occurred in 20% (67/339) of the combination arm vs 29% (98/340) of the control arm [[ASCO Post](https://ascopost.com/issues/december-10-2025/final-analysis-supports-use-of-durvalumab-plus-bcg-infusions-in-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer/)].
- Overall survival showed no detriment (descriptive HR 0.80, 95% CI 0.53–1.20) after >5 years follow-up [[AstraZeneca](https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2025/imfinzi-improved-dfs-in-early-bladder-cancer.html)].
- Safety: most common AEs were BCG-associated urinary toxicities (dysuria, hematuria, cystitis); immune-mediated AEs occurred in 27% of combination patients (most commonly hypothyroidism 11%), grade 3/4 imAEs in 8%, with no imAE deaths [[OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/durvalumab-plus-bcg-demonstrates-manageable-safety-profile-in-bcg-naive-high-risk-nmibc/)].
- Significance: basis for FDA approval (May 28, 2026) of durvalumab (Imfinzi) + BCG — the first and only immunotherapy combination for BCG-naïve high-risk NMIBC [[FDA approval](https://ascopost.com/news/may-2026/fda-approves-durvalumab-plus-bcg-for-high-risk-nmibc/)].
