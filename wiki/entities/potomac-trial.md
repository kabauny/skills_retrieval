# POTOMAC Trial


## Brief

A Phase III clinical trial that investigated the efficacy and safety of durvalumab in combination with BCG induction and maintenance therapy for adult patients with BCG-naïve, high-risk non-muscle invasive bladder cancer, showing significantly improved disease-free survival.

## Why this matters

The POTOMAC trial provided the pivotal evidence for the FDA approval of durvalumab in combination with BCG for high-risk NMIBC, demonstrating improved patient outcomes.
