# Lorlatinib

## Overview
Lorlatinib (LORBRENA) is a third-generation, brain-penetrant ALK/ROS1 tyrosine kinase inhibitor engineered to overcome resistance mutations that limit earlier-generation ALK inhibitors. It is used in ALK-positive metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), where its CNS penetration drives strong intracranial disease control. Its distinctive toxicity profile centers on hyperlipidemia and a broad spectrum of CNS effects.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: macrocyclic kinase inhibitor with in vitro activity against ALK and ROS1 (plus TRKA/B/C and others), designed for blood-brain-barrier penetration and activity against ALK resistance mutations [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2021/210868s004lbl.pdf).
- Principal FDA indication: adults with ALK-positive metastatic NSCLC as detected by an FDA-approved test [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/fda-approves-lorlatinib-second-or-third-line-treatment-alk-positive-metastatic-nsclc).
- Pivotal trial (CROWN, phase 3, first-line, n=296): at 5 years median PFS was not reached with lorlatinib vs 9.1 months with crizotinib, HR 0.19 (95% CI 0.13–0.27); 60% vs 8% progression-free at 5 years [CROWN 5-year JCO](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.24.00581).
- Intracranial efficacy (CROWN): median time to intracranial progression not reached vs 16.4 months for crizotinib (HR 0.06); 92% vs 21% free of intracranial progression at 5 years [CROWN 5-year](https://ascopost.com/issues/august-25-2024/lorlatinib-vs-crizotinib-in-advanced-alk-positive-nsclc-5-year-outcomes-from-phase-iii-crown-trial/).
- Original CROWN registration readout (12-month PFS 78% vs 39% for crizotinib) established first-line superiority [CROWN NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2027187).
- Hyperlipidemia is the most common adverse reaction: grade 3/4 elevations in cholesterol (17%) and triglycerides (17%), with ~80% of patients requiring lipid-lowering therapy [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2021/210868s004lbl.pdf).
- Serious CNS effects can occur, including seizures, hallucinations, and changes in cognition, mood (including suicidal ideation), speech, and sleep; common reactions (≥20%) also include edema, peripheral neuropathy, and weight gain [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2021/210868s004lbl.pdf).
