# Midostaurin

## Overview
Midostaurin (Rydapt, PKC412) is an oral multitargeted small-molecule kinase inhibitor that inhibits FLT3 (both internal tandem duplication and tyrosine kinase domain mutants), KIT, PDGFR, and VEGFR2. It is FDA-approved in combination with intensive chemotherapy for newly diagnosed FLT3-mutated acute myeloid leukemia, and as monotherapy for advanced systemic mastocytosis. Its pivotal AML approval rests on the RATIFY (CALGB 10603) phase 3 trial.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: oral multitargeted kinase inhibitor that blocks mutant FLT3 (ITD and TKD), as well as KIT, PDGFRα/β, and VEGFR2 signaling, suppressing proliferation of FLT3-mutant leukemic blasts [FDA label](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/midostaurin).
- Principal indication: newly diagnosed FLT3-mutation-positive AML (detected by an FDA-approved test), given with standard cytarabine/daunorubicin induction and high-dose cytarabine consolidation at 50 mg orally twice daily [Blood approval summary](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/129/26/3403/36251/Midostaurin-approved-for-FLT3-mutated-AML).
- Pivotal trial RATIFY (CALGB 10603): randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 in 717 FLT3-mutant AML patients aged 18-59; OS HR for death 0.78 (one-sided P=0.009), a 22% reduction in risk of death [RATIFY NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1614359).
- RATIFY also showed significantly longer median overall survival favoring midostaurin (74.7 vs 25.6 months) and improved event-free survival when added to chemotherapy [RATIFY NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1614359).
- Second indication: advanced systemic mastocytosis (aggressive SM, SM with associated hematological neoplasm, or mast cell leukemia) at 100 mg orally twice daily, approved on response rate and duration in a single-arm trial (ASM CR+ICR 38%; SM-AHN 16%) [FDA SM approval summary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30115735/).
- Notable adverse events in AML: febrile neutropenia, nausea, exfoliative dermatitis, vomiting, headache, petechiae, and pyrexia; in advanced SM, decreases in left ventricular ejection fraction and congestive heart failure were observed [FDA label](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/midostaurin).
