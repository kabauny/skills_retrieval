# Epcoritamab

## Overview
Epcoritamab (Epkinly, epcoritamab-bysp) is a subcutaneously administered bispecific antibody that engages CD3 on T cells and CD20 on malignant B cells, redirecting cytotoxic T cells to lyse CD20-positive lymphoma. It is approved for relapsed/refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) and follicular lymphoma (FL) after multiple prior lines of therapy. It carries boxed warnings for cytokine release syndrome (CRS) and immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome (ICANS), mitigated by step-up dosing and corticosteroid prophylaxis.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: T-cell-engaging bispecific antibody binding CD3 (T cells) and CD20 (B cells), directing cytotoxic T cells against CD20-positive malignant B cells; given subcutaneously with cycle 1 step-up dosing [FDA accelerated approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-epcoritamab-bysp-relapsed-or-refractory-diffuse-large-b-cell).
- First approved May 19, 2023 (accelerated) for R/R DLBCL not otherwise specified, including DLBCL arising from indolent lymphoma, and high-grade B-cell lymphoma after >= 2 prior lines of systemic therapy [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-epcoritamab-bysp-relapsed-or-refractory-diffuse-large-b-cell).
- Pivotal phase 1/2 EPCORE NHL-1 trial (NCT03625037) in R/R large B-cell lymphoma: ORR 61% with CR rate 39% in the DLBCL cohort; at median 37.1-month follow-up overall ORR was 59% / CR 41%, with median DOR 20.8 mo and median duration of CR 36.1 mo [EPCORE NHL-1 3-yr update](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/144/Supplement%201/4480/533511/3-Year-Update-from-the-Epcore-NHL-1-Trial).
- Approved for R/R follicular lymphoma: traditional approval as monotherapy after >= 2 prior lines and as a triplet with rituximab + lenalidomide (R2) on November 18, 2025 [AbbVie press release](https://news.abbvie.com/2025-11-18-AbbVie-Announces-U-S-FDA-Approval-of-EPKINLY-R-epcoritamab-bysp-in-Combination-with-Rituximab-and-Lenalidomide-for-Relapsed-or-Refractory-Follicular-Lymphoma).
- Pivotal phase 3 EPCORE FL-1 trial (epcoritamab + R2 vs R2): 79% reduction in risk of progression or death (PFS HR 0.21; 95% CI 0.13-0.33; P < .0001), ORR 89% vs 74%, and CR 74% vs 43% [AbbVie press release](https://news.abbvie.com/2025-11-18-AbbVie-Announces-U-S-FDA-Approval-of-EPKINLY-R-epcoritamab-bysp-in-Combination-with-Rituximab-and-Lenalidomide-for-Relapsed-or-Refractory-Follicular-Lymphoma).
- Notable adverse events: CRS is the most common (about 51% all-grade in LBCL monotherapy, mostly grade 1-2, ~3% grade 3) and ICANS; both prompt boxed warnings, plus infections, fatigue, rash, and cytopenias [EPCORE NHL-1 3-yr update](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/144/Supplement%201/4480/533511/3-Year-Update-from-the-Epcore-NHL-1-Trial).
