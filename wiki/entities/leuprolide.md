# Leuprolide

## Overview
Leuprolide is a synthetic nonapeptide gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) agonist used as androgen-deprivation therapy (ADT), most prominently for advanced prostate cancer. Given continuously via depot injection, it down-regulates pituitary GnRH receptors and produces a medical castration equivalent to surgical orchiectomy. It is delivered in depot formulations dosed monthly through every-6-month intervals (Lupron Depot, Eligard).

## Key facts
- Mechanism: synthetic GnRH agonist that, after an initial LH/FSH and testosterone surge, causes sustained pituitary GnRH-receptor down-regulation, suppressing LH/FSH and gonadal testosterone to castrate levels [FDA Lupron Depot label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2011/020517s025s030s032lbl.pdf).
- Principal indication: palliative treatment of advanced prostate cancer; also approved for endometriosis, uterine fibroids (with iron), and central precocious puberty depending on formulation [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK551662/).
- Pivotal efficacy: a phase 3 study of the leuprolide acetate 6-month depot met its primary endpoint, with 93.4% of patients (95% CI 89.2-97.6%) maintaining serum testosterone ≤50 ng/dL from week 4 through week 48; mean testosterone fell to ~15.9 ng/dL by week 4 [Crawford 6-month depot trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3278745/).
- Castration is rapid and reliable, achieving testosterone suppression comparable to surgical castration within weeks of initiation [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK551662/).
- Tumor-flare risk: the initial testosterone surge (days 5-12) can transiently worsen bone pain, urinary obstruction, or spinal cord compression in advanced disease, mitigated by concurrent antiandrogen therapy [FDA Lupron Depot label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2011/020517s025s030s032lbl.pdf).
- Most common adverse events: hot flushes (~58%), injection-site pain, fatigue, and constipation; ADT also carries risks of decreased bone mineral density, metabolic/cardiovascular effects, hyperglycemia, and QT prolongation [Crawford 6-month depot trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3278745/).
