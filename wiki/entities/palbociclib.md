# Palbociclib

## Overview
Palbociclib (IBRANCE) is an oral, selective inhibitor of cyclin-dependent kinases 4 and 6 (CDK4/6) used in hormone receptor-positive (HR+), HER2-negative advanced or metastatic breast cancer. By blocking CDK4/6, it prevents Rb phosphorylation and arrests tumor cells at the G1-to-S cell-cycle transition. It is given in combination with endocrine therapy (an aromatase inhibitor in the first-line setting or fulvestrant after prior endocrine therapy). It improves progression-free survival substantially, though pivotal trials did not demonstrate statistically significant overall survival benefit.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: selective small-molecule inhibitor of CDK4 and CDK6, blocking retinoblastoma (Rb) protein phosphorylation and halting cell-cycle progression from G1 to S phase [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/207103s010lbl.pdf).
- FDA-approved for HR+/HER2- advanced or metastatic breast cancer, with an aromatase inhibitor as initial endocrine-based therapy, or with fulvestrant after disease progression on prior endocrine therapy [FDA label](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/palbociclib-ibrance-capsules).
- PALOMA-2 (first-line, with letrozole): median PFS 24.8 vs 14.5 months, HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.46-0.72; P<0.001) [PALOMA-2](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1607303).
- PALOMA-2 overall survival was not statistically significant: median OS 53.9 vs 51.2 months, HR 0.956 (95% CI 0.777-1.177) [PALOMA-2 OS](https://www.pfizer.com/news/press-release/press-release-detail/pfizer-announces-overall-survival-results-phase-3-paloma-2).
- PALOMA-3 (with fulvestrant after prior endocrine therapy): median PFS 9.5 vs 4.6 months, HR 0.46 (95% CI 0.36-0.59) [PALOMA-3](https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/22/20/4968/124844/FDA-Approval-of-Palbociclib-in-Combination-with).
- PALOMA-3 overall survival (secondary endpoint, not statistically significant): median OS 34.8 vs 28.0 months, a 6.8-month numerical improvement [PALOMA-3 OS](https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/28/16/3433/707399/Overall-Survival-with-Palbociclib-and-Fulvestrant).
- Most common adverse reaction is neutropenia (80-83% any grade; ~66% Grade >=3), with febrile neutropenia in ~1.8%; CBC monitoring is required at baseline, each cycle, and day 15 of the first 2 cycles [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/207103s010lbl.pdf).
- Serious/notable toxicities include fatigue, infections, stomatitis, anemia, thrombocytopenia, and rare but potentially fatal interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis reported postmarketing [Pfizer warnings](https://www.pfizermedicalinformation.com/en-us/ibrance/warnings).
