# ALTAIR Trial

## Overview

ALTAIR (EPOC1905) was a randomized, double-blind, phase 3 trial within the CIRCULATE-Japan platform testing whether starting trifluridine/tipiracil (FTD/TPI, Lonsurf) versus placebo improves outcomes in patients with resected colorectal cancer who become circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) positive — i.e., molecular residual disease (MRD) — after standard therapy, with no radiologic evidence of disease. It was the first prospective randomized trial to intervene on a purely molecular recurrence in CRC. The trial did not meet its primary endpoint, showing a numerical but not statistically significant disease-free survival (DFS) improvement.

## Key facts

- Design: double-blind, placebo-controlled phase 3 RCT embedded in CIRCULATE-Japan; 243 patients randomized 1:1 to FTD/TPI (n=122) vs placebo (n=121), enrolled July 2020–June 2023 [[ALTAIR, Nat Med 2026|https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04428-0]] ([Nature Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04428-0)).
- Population: resected colorectal cancer (stage 0–IV) that became blood ctDNA-positive after standard adjuvant therapy with no radiologic evidence of disease; intervention was 6 months of FTD/TPI vs placebo ([ALTAIR, JCO 2025 LBA22](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2025.43.4_suppl.LBA22)).
- Primary endpoint (investigator-assessed DFS) was not met: median DFS 9.30 months (95% CI 7.92–10.84) with FTD/TPI vs 5.55 months (95% CI 4.17–7.33) with placebo; HR 0.79 (95% CI 0.60–1.05; P=.107) ([Nature Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04428-0)).
- Sustained ctDNA clearance was strongly prognostic: no clearance (HR 19.32; 95% CI 4.67–79.96; P<.0001) and transient clearance (HR 7.74; 95% CI 1.84–32.59; P=.0053) predicted worse DFS than sustained clearance ([Nature Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04428-0)).
- Toxicity was substantial relative to the modest signal: grade ≥3 neutropenia occurred in roughly 57% of FTD/TPI patients, the dominant hematologic adverse event ([Nature Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04428-0)).
- Results were first reported at the 2025 ASCO Gastrointestinal Cancers Symposium (LBA22) before full publication in Nature Medicine ([ASCO GI 2025, JCO LBA22](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2025.43.4_suppl.LBA22)).
- Significance: despite the negative primary result, ALTAIR established the feasibility of MRD-guided post-adjuvant treatment selection and informs ongoing ctDNA-directed adjuvant trial design ([Targeted Oncology](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/trifluridine-tipiracil-demonstrates-numerically-improved-dfs-after-resection-in-ctdna-crc); [CIRCULATE-Japan, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8253296/)).
