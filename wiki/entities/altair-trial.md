# ALTAIR Trial


## Brief

A trial investigating whether starting systemic therapy with trifluridine/tipiracil versus placebo improves outcomes in resected colorectal cancer patients who develop positive MRD during surveillance.

## Why this matters

Provides critical data suggesting that intervening with chemotherapy based solely on a molecular recurrence (positive MRD) during surveillance does not definitively improve overall disease-free survival over observation.
