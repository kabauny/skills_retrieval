# Dostarlimab

## Overview
Dostarlimab (Jemperli) is a humanized anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody (immune checkpoint inhibitor) developed by GSK. It is approved for primary advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer, where it is added to carboplatin/paclitaxel chemotherapy followed by single-agent maintenance, and as monotherapy for previously treated dMMR endometrial cancer. Benefit is greatest in mismatch-repair-deficient (dMMR)/microsatellite-instability-high (MSI-H) tumors, though the chemotherapy combination indication has been expanded to all comers.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: binds the PD-1 receptor and blocks its interaction with ligands PD-L1/PD-L2, restoring T-cell-mediated antitumor immunity [[mechanism]] [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/fda-approves-dostarlimab-gxly-chemotherapy-endometrial-cancer).
- FDA-approved with carboplatin and paclitaxel followed by single-agent dostarlimab for adults with primary advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer; the indication was expanded in August 2024 from dMMR/MSI-H only to all adult patients [FDA expanded indication](https://www.gsk.com/en-gb/media/press-releases/us-fda-expands-jemperli-dostarlimab-plus-chemotherapy-approval-to-all-adult-patients-with-primary-advanced-or-recurrent-endometrial-cancer/).
- Also approved as a single agent for dMMR recurrent/advanced endometrial cancer that has progressed on or after a platinum regimen in patients who are not candidates for curative surgery or radiation [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/fda-approves-dostarlimab-gxly-chemotherapy-endometrial-cancer).
- Pivotal phase 3 RUBY trial (NCT03981796, n=494): in the dMMR/MSI-H subgroup, dostarlimab + chemo cut risk of progression or death by 72% (PFS HR 0.28, 95% CI 0.16–0.50; 24-mo PFS 61.4% vs 15.7%) [RUBY (NEJM)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2216334).
- In the RUBY overall population, PFS HR was 0.64 (95% CI 0.51–0.80) and overall survival improved (24-mo OS 71.3% vs 56.0%; OS HR 0.64, 95% CI 0.46–0.87), the first immuno-oncology regimen to show an OS benefit in this setting [RUBY (NEJM)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2216334).
- Monotherapy efficacy supported by the phase 1 GARNET study: confirmed ORR 45.4% (95% CI 37.0–54.0) including 15.6% complete responses in dMMR endometrial cancer, with durable responses (median DOR not reached) [GARNET (Clin Cancer Res)](https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/29/22/4564/729963/Safety-Efficacy-and-Biomarker-Analyses-of).
- Notable adverse events: immune-mediated toxicities (pneumonitis, colitis, hepatitis, endocrinopathies including hypothyroidism, nephritis) and infusion reactions; common AEs include fatigue, anemia, nausea, diarrhea, and rash [GARNET (Clin Cancer Res)](https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/29/22/4564/729963/Safety-Efficacy-and-Biomarker-Analyses-of).
