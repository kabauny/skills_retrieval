# Tucatinib

## Overview

Tucatinib (Tukysa) is an oral, highly HER2-selective tyrosine kinase inhibitor that is roughly 1000-fold more selective for HER2 than for EGFR in vitro, minimizing the rash and diarrhea typical of less-selective HER2 TKIs. It is CNS-penetrant and has established efficacy in pretreated HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer, including patients with brain metastases, and in chemotherapy-refractory HER2-positive RAS wild-type metastatic colorectal cancer. It is given in combination with trastuzumab (with or without capecitabine) rather than as monotherapy.

## Key facts

- **Mechanism:** HER2 tyrosine kinase inhibitor that blocks HER2/HER3 phosphorylation, suppressing downstream MAPK and AKT signaling; ~1000x more selective for HER2 than EGFR, limiting off-target toxicity [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2023/213411s004lbl.pdf).
- **Breast cancer indication:** in combination with trastuzumab and capecitabine for advanced/metastatic HER2+ breast cancer (including brain metastases) after one or more prior anti-HER2 regimens [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2023/213411s004lbl.pdf).
- **HER2CLIMB pivotal trial:** in patients pretreated with trastuzumab, pertuzumab, and T-DM1, the tucatinib arm improved OS (median 24.7 vs 19.2 mo; HR 0.73, 95% CI 0.59–0.90) and PFS (7.8 vs 5.6 mo; HR 0.54) [HER2CLIMB OS](https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(21)04879-1/fulltext).
- **Intracranial activity:** among patients with brain metastases in HER2CLIMB, CNS-PFS was 7.6 vs 5.4 mo (HR 0.61) with reduced risk of intracranial progression or death [HER2CLIMB CNS](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.20.00775).
- **Colorectal cancer indication:** accelerated approval (Jan 2023) with trastuzumab for unresectable/metastatic RAS wild-type, HER2+ colorectal cancer after fluoropyrimidine, oxaliplatin, and irinotecan [FDA CRC approval](https://www.cancer.gov/news-events/cancer-currents-blog/2023/fda-tucatinib-her2-colorectal-cancer).
- **MOUNTAINEER pivotal trial (CRC):** in 84 patients on tucatinib + trastuzumab, ORR was 38% (95% CI 28–49) with median duration of response 12.4 months [FDA CRC approval](https://www.cancer.gov/news-events/cancer-currents-blog/2023/fda-tucatinib-her2-colorectal-cancer).
- **Notable adverse events:** diarrhea (81%; Grade ≥3 in ~13%) and severe hepatotoxicity (ALT/AST >5x ULN in 6–8%); ALT, AST, and bilirubin must be monitored every 3 weeks [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2023/213411s004lbl.pdf).
