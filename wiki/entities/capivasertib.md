# capivasertib

## Overview

Capivasertib (Truqap) is an oral small-molecule inhibitor of the serine/threonine kinase AKT. It was approved by the FDA in November 2023 in combination with fulvestrant for HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer harboring PI3K/AKT-pathway alterations. It is the first AKT-targeted agent approved in this setting and is given on an intermittent (4 days on, 3 days off) weekly schedule.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: oral pan-AKT kinase inhibitor blocking AKT1, AKT2, and AKT3, suppressing phosphorylation of downstream substrates in the PI3K/AKT/PTEN signaling pathway [[FDA Approval Summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11588547/)].
- Principal FDA indication: in combination with fulvestrant for adults with HR-positive, HER2-negative locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer with one or more PIK3CA/AKT1/PTEN alterations, after progression on at least one endocrine-based regimen [[FDA Approval Summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11588547/)].
- Pivotal trial: the phase 3 CAPItello-291 study (n=708) randomized patients 1:1 to capivasertib plus fulvestrant versus placebo plus fulvestrant [[CAPItello-291](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2214131)].
- Efficacy (overall population): median PFS 7.2 vs 3.6 months, hazard ratio 0.60 (95% CI 0.51–0.71, P<0.001) [[CAPItello-291](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2214131)].
- Efficacy (biomarker-altered population): median PFS 7.3 vs 3.1 months, hazard ratio 0.50 (95% CI 0.38–0.65, P<0.0001) — the basis for the biomarker-restricted approval [[FDA Approval Summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11588547/)].
- Notable adverse events: diarrhea (77% all-grade, 12% grade 3–4), cutaneous reactions/rash (56% all-grade, 15% grade 3–4), and hyperglycemia (19% all-grade), reflecting on-target PI3K/AKT pathway inhibition [[FDA Approval Summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11588547/)].
- Predictive biomarker testing for PIK3CA, AKT1, or PTEN alterations is required before initiating therapy [[FDA Approval Summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11588547/)].
