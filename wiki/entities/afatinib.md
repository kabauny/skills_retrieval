# Afatinib

## Overview
Afatinib (Gilotrif) is an oral, irreversible inhibitor of the ErbB family of receptor tyrosine kinases (EGFR/ErbB1, HER2/ErbB2, and ErbB4). It is approved for first-line treatment of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) harboring common EGFR mutations (exon 19 deletions, exon 21 L858R) and certain non-resistant uncommon mutations, as well as second-line treatment of advanced squamous-cell NSCLC after platinum chemotherapy.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: covalently and irreversibly binds the kinase domains of EGFR (ErbB1), HER2 (ErbB2), and ErbB4, blocking downstream proliferation/survival signaling — a distinction from reversible first-generation TKIs like gefitinib/erlotinib [FDA/DailyMed label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=fd638e5e-8032-e7ca-0179-95e96ab5d387&amp=&audience=consumer).
- Principal indications: first-line metastatic NSCLC with EGFR exon 19 deletions or exon 21 L858R substitutions (2013 approval), expanded to include non-resistant uncommon mutations L861Q, G719X, and S768I; also second-line advanced squamous NSCLC after platinum-based chemotherapy [Targeted Oncology / FDA expansion](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/afatinib-approval-for-lung-cancer-expanded-by-fda).
- Pivotal LUX-Lung 3: vs cisplatin/pemetrexed, median PFS 11.1 vs 6.9 mo (HR 0.58); in the Del19/L858R subgroup PFS 13.6 vs 6.9 mo (HR 0.47, P=0.001) [Afatinib EGFR review (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7448811/).
- Pivotal LUX-Lung 6 (Asian patients): vs gemcitabine/cisplatin, median PFS 11.0 vs 5.6 mo (HR 0.28, P<0.0001); pre-specified Del19 OS benefit (e.g., 31.4 vs 18.4 mo, HR 0.64) [LUX-Lung 6 (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24439929/).
- Squamous NSCLC LUX-Lung 8: vs erlotinib second-line, median OS 7.9 vs 6.8 mo (HR 0.81, P=0.0077) and PFS 2.6 vs 1.9 mo (HR 0.81, P=0.0103) [LUX-Lung 8 (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26156651/).
- Most common adverse reactions (≥20%): diarrhea, rash/acneiform dermatitis, stomatitis, paronychia, dry skin, decreased appetite, nausea, vomiting, pruritus; dose reductions required in ~57% of patients [Gilotrif prescribing information](https://www.drugs.com/pro/gilotrif.html).
- Serious risks/warnings: diarrhea (can cause dehydration/renal failure), bullous and exfoliative skin disorders, interstitial lung disease (ILD, led to ~0.9% of discontinuations), hepatotoxicity, keratitis, and embryo-fetal toxicity [Gilotrif prescribing information](https://www.drugs.com/pro/gilotrif.html).
