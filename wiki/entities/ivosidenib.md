# Ivosidenib

## Overview
Ivosidenib (Tibsovo) is an oral, small-molecule inhibitor of mutated isocitrate dehydrogenase-1 (IDH1). By blocking the neomorphic mutant enzyme it suppresses production of the oncometabolite 2-hydroxyglutarate (2-HG), restoring cellular differentiation. It is FDA-approved across IDH1-mutated acute myeloid leukemia (AML) and previously treated IDH1-mutated cholangiocarcinoma.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: selectively inhibits mutant IDH1 (e.g., R132 variants), lowering 2-HG and promoting differentiation; multiple 500 mg daily doses reduced 2-HG ~93% in marrow of hematologic patients and ~82% in cholangiocarcinoma tumor biopsies ([FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/211192s009lbl.pdf)).
- Indications: IDH1-mutated newly diagnosed AML (monotherapy or with azacitidine in adults ≥75 yr or unfit for intensive chemo), relapsed/refractory AML, and previously treated locally advanced/metastatic IDH1-mutated cholangiocarcinoma ([FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/211192s009lbl.pdf)).
- Pivotal AML trial AGILE (phase 3, ivosidenib + azacitidine vs placebo + azacitidine, n=148): significantly improved event-free survival, complete remission rate, and overall survival; long-term median OS 29.3 vs 7.9 months (HR 0.42, P<.0001) ([AGILE](https://ashpublications.org/bloodadvances/article/9/20/5177/546354/Long-term-results-from-the-AGILE-study-of)).
- Pivotal cholangiocarcinoma trial ClarIDHy (phase 3 vs placebo): PFS HR 0.37 (95% CI 0.25–0.54, P<.001); median OS 10.3 vs 7.5 mo (HR 0.79, P=.09 ITT), but HR 0.49 after RPSFT crossover adjustment ([ClarIDHy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8461552/)).
- Serious adverse events: differentiation syndrome and QTc prolongation are boxed/warning toxicities in AML; can be fatal if untreated ([FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/211192s009lbl.pdf)).
- Common cholangiocarcinoma toxicities (grade ≥3) included ascites (9%), anemia (7%), elevated bilirubin (6%), and hyponatremia (6%) ([ClarIDHy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8461552/)).
- Dosing: 500 mg orally once daily in continuous 28-day cycles ([ClarIDHy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8461552/)).
