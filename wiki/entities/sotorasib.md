# Sotorasib

## Overview
Sotorasib (Lumakras) is a first-in-class small-molecule inhibitor that covalently and irreversibly binds the mutant KRAS G12C protein, locking it in an inactive GDP-bound state. It received FDA accelerated approval in May 2021 as the first targeted therapy for KRAS G12C-mutated non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) after at least one prior systemic therapy. It is given orally at 960 mg once daily.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: irreversibly binds the cysteine of mutant KRAS G12C in the switch II pocket, locking the protein in its inactive GDP-bound state, blocking downstream signaling and proliferation selectively in KRAS G12C tumor cells [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/214665Orig1s009correctedlbl.pdf).
- Principal FDA indication: adults with KRAS G12C-mutated locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC who have received at least one prior systemic therapy (accelerated approval, May 2021) [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-sotorasib-kras-g12c-mutated-nsclc).
- Pivotal phase 2 CodeBreaK 100 (NCT03600883): objective response rate 37.1% (95% CI 28.6-46.2) with median duration of response 11.1 months in pretreated KRAS G12C NSCLC [CodeBreaK 100 NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2103695).
- Confirmatory phase 3 CodeBreaK 200 (n=345) vs docetaxel: median PFS 5.6 vs 4.5 months, HR 0.66 (95% CI 0.51-0.86, P=0.0017); ORR 28.1% vs 13.2% [CodeBreaK 200 Lancet](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(23)00221-0/abstract).
- Recommended dose is 960 mg orally once daily, with or without food, until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/214665Orig1s009correctedlbl.pdf).
- Most common adverse reactions (≥20%): diarrhea, musculoskeletal pain, nausea, fatigue, hepatotoxicity, and cough; hepatotoxicity (elevated transaminases) is a notable serious risk requiring monitoring [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-sotorasib-kras-g12c-mutated-nsclc).
- KRAS G12C is the targeting biomarker; activity in KRAS G12C colorectal cancer as monotherapy is limited, prompting combination strategies with anti-EGFR agents [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-sotorasib-kras-g12c-mutated-nsclc).
