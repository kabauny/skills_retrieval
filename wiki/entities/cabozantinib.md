# cabozantinib

## Overview

Cabozantinib (CABOMETYX, COMETRIQ) is an orally administered multi-targeted tyrosine kinase inhibitor used across several advanced solid tumors. It is approved for advanced renal cell carcinoma (alone or with nivolumab), previously treated hepatocellular carcinoma, progressive metastatic medullary thyroid cancer, and previously treated unresectable pancreatic and extra-pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors. Its activity derives from simultaneous blockade of angiogenic and oncogenic receptor kinases.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: inhibits the tyrosine kinase activity of MET, AXL, RET, and VEGFR-1/2/3, disrupting tumor angiogenesis, growth, metastasis, and resistance pathways; standard CABOMETYX dose is 60 mg orally once daily [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2021/208692s010lbl.pdf).
- In the phase 3 METEOR trial (advanced RCC after prior VEGFR-TKI), cabozantinib beat everolimus with median OS 21.4 vs 16.5 mo (HR 0.66, 95% CI 0.53-0.83) and ORR 21% vs 5% [METEOR](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27279544/).
- In first-line advanced RCC, the phase 3 CheckMate 9ER trial showed nivolumab + cabozantinib vs sunitinib improved median PFS 16.6 vs 8.3 mo (HR 0.51) plus OS and ORR benefit, establishing it as a standard of care [CheckMate 9ER](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2026982).
- In the phase 3 CELESTIAL trial (previously treated advanced HCC), cabozantinib vs placebo extended median OS 10.2 vs 8.0 mo (HR 0.76, 95% CI 0.63-0.92; P=0.005) and median PFS 5.2 vs 1.9 mo (HR 0.44) [CELESTIAL](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1717002).
- In progressive metastatic medullary thyroid cancer (EXAM trial), cabozantinib improved median PFS to 11.2 vs 4.0 mo vs placebo (HR 0.28, 95% CI 0.19-0.40; P<0.0001), supporting the COMETRIQ approval [EXAM](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2012.48.4659).
- Common adverse events include diarrhea, palmar-plantar erythrodysesthesia, hypertension, fatigue, stomatitis, decreased appetite/weight, and nausea [EXAM](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4093460/).
- Serious/boxed risks include hemorrhage, GI perforation and fistula, hypertensive crisis, thrombotic events, reversible posterior leukoencephalopathy syndrome, and impaired wound healing; hold before surgery [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2021/208692s010lbl.pdf).
