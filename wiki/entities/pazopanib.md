# Pazopanib

## Overview
Pazopanib (Votrient) is an oral multi-targeted tyrosine kinase inhibitor that blocks angiogenesis-related receptors, dosed 800 mg once daily. It is FDA-approved for advanced renal cell carcinoma and for advanced soft tissue sarcoma after prior chemotherapy. It carries a boxed warning for severe and fatal hepatotoxicity.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: ATP-competitive inhibitor of VEGFR-1/2/3, PDGFR-α/β, FGFR-1/3, and KIT, suppressing tumor angiogenesis and growth signaling [DailyMed label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=eeaaaf38-fb86-4d9f-a19d-0f61daac2fd7).
- Indicated for advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC) and for advanced soft tissue sarcoma (STS) in patients who received prior chemotherapy; efficacy not shown for adipocytic STS or GIST [DailyMed label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=eeaaaf38-fb86-4d9f-a19d-0f61daac2fd7).
- Pivotal RCC trial VEG105192 (phase III, n=435): median PFS 9.2 vs 4.2 months vs placebo (HR 0.46, p<0.0001); response rate 30% vs 3% [VEG105192 (JCO)](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2009.23.9764).
- Pivotal sarcoma trial PALETTE (EORTC 62072, phase III): median PFS 4.6 vs 1.6 months vs placebo (HR 0.31, p<0.0001); OS not significantly improved (12.5 vs 10.7 mo, HR 0.86, p=0.25) [PALETTE (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22595799/).
- Boxed warning: severe and fatal hepatotoxicity; monitor liver chemistries at baseline and during treatment [DailyMed label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=eeaaaf38-fb86-4d9f-a19d-0f61daac2fd7).
- Other serious adverse effects include QT prolongation/torsades de pointes, cardiac dysfunction (decreased LVEF, CHF), and hemorrhagic events (pulmonary/GI hemorrhage) [DailyMed label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=eeaaaf38-fb86-4d9f-a19d-0f61daac2fd7).
- Most common adverse reactions (≥20%): diarrhea, hypertension, hair color changes, nausea, anorexia, vomiting, and fatigue [DailyMed label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=eeaaaf38-fb86-4d9f-a19d-0f61daac2fd7).
