# Entrectinib

## Overview
Entrectinib (Rozlytrek) is an orally available, CNS-penetrant tyrosine kinase inhibitor of the TRK family (TRKA/B/C, encoded by NTRK1/2/3), ROS1, and ALK. It is FDA-approved for ROS1-positive metastatic NSCLC and, in a tumor-agnostic indication, for NTRK fusion-positive solid tumors. Its strong blood-brain barrier penetration makes it particularly useful in patients with CNS metastases.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: potent oral inhibitor of TRKA/B/C (NTRK1/2/3 gene products), ROS1, and ALK kinases; designed for CNS penetration to address brain metastases [Rozlytrek prescribing information](https://www.drugs.com/pro/rozlytrek.html)
- Principal indications: (1) adults with ROS1-positive metastatic NSCLC; (2) tumor-agnostic — adult and pediatric (≥1 month) patients with NTRK fusion-positive solid tumors that are metastatic/unresectable and lacking other satisfactory options [NCI](https://www.cancer.gov/news-events/cancer-currents-blog/2019/fda-entrectinib-ntrk-fusion)
- Pivotal data came from an integrated analysis of the phase II STARTRK-2, phase I STARTRK-1, and phase I ALKA-372-001 trials [OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/fda-approves-entrectinib-for-ntrk-tumors-and-ros1-nsclc)
- NTRK fusion-positive solid tumors: ORR 57% (31/54), with 4 complete responses; 61% of responders maintained response ≥9 months [NCI](https://www.cancer.gov/news-events/cancer-currents-blog/2019/fda-entrectinib-ntrk-fusion)
- ROS1-positive NSCLC: ORR 78% (40/51), with 3 complete responses; >50% of responders had duration ≥12 months, plus documented intracranial responses (5/7 with CNS metastases) [NCI](https://www.cancer.gov/news-events/cancer-currents-blog/2019/fda-entrectinib-ntrk-fusion)
- Serious adverse events: congestive heart failure (3.4%, including Grade 3 2.3%; assess LVEF at baseline), QTc prolongation, and CNS effects; skeletal fractures and hepatotoxicity are also labeled warnings [Rozlytrek prescribing information](https://www.drugs.com/pro/rozlytrek.html)
- Most common adverse reactions (≥20%) include dizziness (38%) and cognitive impairment (27%), along with fatigue, constipation, dysgeusia, and weight gain [Rozlytrek prescribing information](https://www.drugs.com/pro/rozlytrek.html)
