# Dabrafenib

## Overview
Dabrafenib (Tafinlar) is an oral, ATP-competitive RAF kinase inhibitor that targets the constitutively activated mutant BRAF V600E/V600K/V600D enzymes driving tumor growth in melanoma and other cancers. It is given almost always in combination with the MEK inhibitor trametinib to deepen pathway blockade and reduce paradoxical activation. Approved indications span BRAF V600-mutant unresectable/metastatic melanoma, resected stage III melanoma (adjuvant), metastatic NSCLC, and BRAF V600E anaplastic thyroid cancer. It is not indicated for wild-type BRAF tumors.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: selective ATP-competitive inhibitor of wild-type BRAF/CRAF and mutant BRAF V600E/V600K/V600D kinases, blocking constitutive MAPK pathway signaling; dosed 150 mg twice daily, typically combined with trametinib [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2014/202806s002lbl.pdf)
- Indications: BRAF V600E/V600K-mutant unresectable/metastatic melanoma; adjuvant therapy of resected stage III melanoma with nodal involvement; metastatic BRAF V600E NSCLC; and locally advanced/metastatic BRAF V600E anaplastic thyroid cancer [FDA NSCLC approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-regular-approval-dabrafenib-and-trametinib-combination-metastatic-nsclc-braf-v600e)
- COMBI-d (vs dabrafenib monotherapy): adding trametinib improved median OS to 25.1 vs 18.7 months (HR 0.71, 95% CI 0.55-0.92) in metastatic BRAF V600E/K melanoma [COMBI-d](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5834102/)
- COMBI-v (vs vemurafenib): dabrafenib plus trametinib reduced death risk with 12-month OS 72% vs 65% (HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.53-0.89, P=0.005); pooled 5-year OS reached 34% [COMBI-v](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1412690)
- COMBI-AD (adjuvant, stage III): 3-year relapse-free survival 58% vs 39% with placebo (HR for relapse or death 0.47, 95% CI 0.39-0.58, P<0.001) [COMBI-AD](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1708539)
- NSCLC/thyroid efficacy: BRAF V600E metastatic NSCLC showed ~65% response rate and median PFS ~10-11 months; combination is the first regimen with robust activity in BRAF V600E anaplastic thyroid cancer [Anaplastic thyroid JCO](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2017.73.6785)
- Adverse events: most common (≥20%) include pyrexia, fatigue, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, rash, chills, and hemorrhage; pyrexia is the dominant dose-limiting toxicity and can cause rigors, dehydration, hypotension, and acute renal insufficiency [Pyrexia/AE management](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6656467/)
