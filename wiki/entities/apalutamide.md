# Apalutamide

## Overview
Apalutamide (ERLEADA) is an orally administered, second-generation androgen receptor (AR) inhibitor used to treat advanced prostate cancer. It is given with continued androgen deprivation therapy (a GnRH analog or after bilateral orchiectomy). It is FDA-approved for non-metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (nmCRPC) and metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer (mCSPC), and is dosed at 240 mg orally once daily.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: binds directly to the ligand-binding domain of the androgen receptor, blocking AR nuclear translocation, DNA binding, and AR-mediated transcription, thereby inhibiting androgen-driven tumor growth [FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d1cda4f7-cb33-46ea-b9ac-431f6452b1a5).
- Indication 1: non-metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (nmCRPC), with continued androgen deprivation therapy [FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d1cda4f7-cb33-46ea-b9ac-431f6452b1a5).
- Indication 2: metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer (mCSPC), approved in 2019 with continued androgen deprivation therapy [J&J/FDA approval](https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/u-s-fda-approves-supplemental-new-drug-application-snda-for-erleada-apalutamide-for-the-treatment-of-patients-with-metastatic-castration-sensitive-prostate-cancer-mcspc).
- Pivotal trial (nmCRPC): SPARTAN (phase 3) showed median metastasis-free survival of 40.5 vs 16.2 months (HR 0.28, 95% CI 0.23-0.35, P<0.001), with later OS benefit of 73.9 vs 59.9 months (HR 0.78) [SPARTAN-NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1715546).
- Pivotal trial (mCSPC): TITAN (phase 3) improved overall survival, 24-month OS 82.4% vs 73.5% (HR for death 0.67, 95% CI 0.51-0.89, P=0.005) [TITAN-NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1903307).
- Common adverse reactions (>=10%): fatigue, arthralgia, rash, decreased appetite, fall, weight loss, hypertension, hot flush, diarrhea, and fracture [FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d1cda4f7-cb33-46ea-b9ac-431f6452b1a5).
- Serious risks: severe cutaneous adverse reactions (SJS/TEN, DRESS), ischemic cardiovascular and cerebrovascular events, seizure (0.4%, requiring permanent discontinuation), and falls/fractures with increased incidence in the elderly [FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d1cda4f7-cb33-46ea-b9ac-431f6452b1a5).
