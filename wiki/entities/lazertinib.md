# Lazertinib

## Overview

Lazertinib (brand name Lazcluze) is a brain-penetrant, irreversible third-generation EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor that targets the common activating EGFR mutations (exon 19 deletion and L858R) and the T790M resistance mutation while sparing wild-type EGFR. In the U.S. it is approved in combination with the EGFR-MET bispecific antibody amivantamab for first-line locally advanced or metastatic EGFR-mutated NSCLC; in South Korea it is approved as monotherapy across treatment lines.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: potent, CNS-penetrant, irreversible third-generation EGFR-TKI targeting Ex19del, L858R, and T790M mutations while sparing wild-type EGFR [Lazertinib: First Approval, PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8217052/)
- FDA approved Aug 19, 2024 with amivantamab-vmjw for first-line locally advanced/metastatic NSCLC with EGFR exon 19 deletions or exon 21 L858R substitutions [FDA approval announcement](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-lazertinib-amivantamab-vmjw-non-small-lung-cancer)
- Pivotal trial MARIPOSA (NCT04487080): randomized active-controlled phase 3, N=1074, treatment-naive advanced EGFR-mutated NSCLC, comparing amivantamab + lazertinib vs osimertinib [MARIPOSA, NEJM/PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38924756/)
- Primary endpoint: median PFS 23.7 vs 16.6 months for the combination vs osimertinib, HR 0.70 (95% CI 0.58-0.85; P<0.001) [MARIPOSA, NEJM/PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38924756/)
- Overall survival benefit confirmed at median 37.8 mo follow-up: HR for death 0.75 (95% CI 0.61-0.92; P=0.005); 3-year OS 60% vs 51% [MARIPOSA OS, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa2503001)
- As monotherapy in LASER301 (phase 3, N=393, lazertinib vs gefitinib first-line), lazertinib roughly doubled median PFS, a ~55% lower risk of progression/death [LASER301, JCO](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.23.00515)
- Notable toxicity with the amivantamab combination: grade ≥3 adverse events in 80% (vs 52% with osimertinib), driven by skin-related events, venous thromboembolism, and infusion-related reactions [MARIPOSA OS, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa2503001)
- Positioned as the first chemotherapy-free combination regimen showing superiority over osimertinib in first-line EGFR-mutated NSCLC [FDA approval announcement](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-lazertinib-amivantamab-vmjw-non-small-lung-cancer)
