# Topotecan

## Overview
Topotecan is a semisynthetic camptothecin derivative that inhibits topoisomerase I. It is approved for relapsed small cell lung cancer (SCLC), platinum-pretreated metastatic ovarian cancer, and—combined with cisplatin—for advanced/recurrent cervical cancer. It is available in both intravenous and oral formulations and is dose-limited primarily by myelosuppression.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: binds and stabilizes the topoisomerase I–DNA cleavage complex, preventing religation of single-strand breaks; collision with the replication fork generates lethal double-strand DNA damage during S-phase [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2010/020671s016s017lbl.pdf).
- FDA-approved indications: metastatic ovarian carcinoma after failure of prior chemotherapy; sensitive-relapse SCLC after failure of first-line therapy; and Stage IVB/recurrent/persistent cervical cancer in combination with cisplatin [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2010/020671s016s017lbl.pdf).
- O'Brien phase III trial (relapsed SCLC): oral topotecan plus best supportive care prolonged overall survival vs supportive care alone—median 25.9 vs 13.9 weeks (log-rank P = .0104) [O'Brien 2006 JCO](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2006.06.5821).
- Oral-vs-IV phase III (sensitive-relapse SCLC): comparable efficacy, with median survival 33.0 weeks (oral) vs 35.0 weeks (IV) and response rates 18.3% vs 21.9% [Eckardt 2007 JCO](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2006.08.3998).
- GOG-0179 phase III (cervical cancer): cisplatin + topotecan improved median OS to 9.4 vs 6.5 months and median PFS to 4.6 vs 2.9 months vs cisplatin alone [GOG-0179 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2503656/).
- Standard IV dosing for ovarian/SCLC is 1.5 mg/m² over 30 min on days 1–5 of a 21-day cycle; cervical regimen is 0.75 mg/m² days 1–3 with cisplatin [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2010/020671s016s017lbl.pdf).
- Notable toxicities: severe, dose-limiting myelosuppression (grade 4 neutropenia up to ~64% with IV dosing, thrombocytopenia, anemia) with risk of neutropenic sepsis; also diarrhea, fatigue, nausea, and alopecia [Eckardt 2007 JCO](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2006.08.3998).
