# Pancreatic cancer

## Overview

Pancreatic cancer is among the most lethal solid tumors, with a 5-year relative survival of roughly 13% and an estimated 67,440 new U.S. diagnoses and 51,980 deaths in 2025, making it the third-leading cause of cancer death. The dominant histology is pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC), which accounts for the vast majority of cases and carries an even lower (~8%) 5-year survival. Surgical resectability — resectable, borderline resectable, locally advanced, or metastatic — is the central determinant of whether intent is curative or palliative. More than 90% of PDAC harbors KRAS mutation, and germline/somatic alterations (BRCA, MMR/MSI-high) increasingly gate targeted and maintenance therapy.

## Key facts

- Epidemiology/histology: PDAC dominates; pancreatic cancer is the 3rd-leading cause of U.S. cancer death (~67,440 diagnoses, ~51,980 deaths in 2025) with a 5-year relative survival stalled at ~13% [ACS/PanCAN](https://pancan.org/press-releases/pancreatic-cancer-diagnoses-and-mortality-rates-climb-five-year-survival-rate-for-pancreatic-cancer-stalls-at-13/), [SEER](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/pancreas.html).
- Staging/intent: Resectability (resectable, borderline, locally advanced, metastatic) sets curative-intent surgery + adjuvant chemotherapy versus palliative systemic therapy; only ~10-20% present with resectable disease [SEER](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/pancreas.html).
- Adjuvant standard (PRODIGE 24/CCTG PA6): modified FOLFIRINOX vs gemcitabine after resection improved median OS to 53.5 vs 35.5 mo (OS HR 0.68, 95% CI 0.54-0.85) and median DFS 21.4 vs 12.8 mo (HR 0.66) [PRODIGE 24/NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1809775).
- First-line metastatic (NAPOLI 3): NALIRIFOX vs nab-paclitaxel/gemcitabine gave median OS 11.1 vs 9.2 mo (HR 0.83, 95% CI 0.70-0.99; p=0.036) in treatment-naive mPDAC [NAPOLI 3/Lancet](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37708904/).
- First-line metastatic (MPACT): nab-paclitaxel + gemcitabine vs gemcitabine improved median OS 8.5 vs 6.7 mo (HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.62-0.84; p<0.001), an option for patients unfit for FOLFIRINOX [MPACT/NEJM](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24131140/).
- Biomarker-gated maintenance (POLO): in germline BRCA1/2-mutated mPDAC without progression on platinum, maintenance olaparib vs placebo improved PFS (HR 0.53, 95% CI 0.35-0.82; p=0.004), though OS was not significantly extended [POLO/NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1903387).
- Molecular landscape: >90% of PDAC carries KRAS mutation (G12D/G12V/G12R most common), historically "undruggable"; broad RAS inhibitors such as daraxonrasib received FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation in 2025, and MSI-high tumors are eligible for immunotherapy [Cancers review](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6694/17/15/2453).
