# Tamoxifen

## Overview

Tamoxifen is a selective estrogen receptor modulator (SERM) that competitively antagonizes the estrogen receptor in breast tissue while acting as a partial agonist in bone and endometrium. It is the historical anchor of adjuvant endocrine therapy for hormone receptor-positive (HR+) breast cancer and remains in active use, especially for premenopausal patients and those who decline or cannot tolerate ovarian function suppression. Its anti-estrogenic activity does not depend on menopausal status, so it works in both pre- and postmenopausal women.

## Key facts

- **Mechanism:** competitive estrogen-receptor antagonism in breast tissue, with tissue-selective partial-agonist effects (bone, endometrium); it does not require ovarian suppression and is active in pre- and postmenopausal women [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/021807s005lbl.pdf).
- **Principal FDA indications:** adjuvant treatment of ER-positive early breast cancer (and reduction of contralateral breast cancer), metastatic breast cancer, ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS) after surgery/radiation, and risk reduction in women at high risk of breast cancer [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/021807s005lbl.pdf).
- **Pivotal efficacy (EBCTCG meta-analysis, 20 trials, 21,457 women):** ~5 years of tamoxifen cut recurrence by roughly half in years 0–4 (RR 0.53) and a third in years 5–9 (RR 0.68), and reduced breast-cancer mortality by about a third across 15 years (RR 0.70, p<0.00001); 10-year survival improved 10.9% (node-positive) and 5.6% (node-negative) [EBCTCG Lancet 2011](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(11)60993-8/fulltext).
- **Extended therapy (ATLAS, n=12,894):** continuing tamoxifen to 10 vs 5 years lowered recurrence by 25% and breast-cancer mortality by 29% in the second decade, reducing cumulative breast-cancer death (years 5–14) from 15.0% to 12.2% [ATLAS Lancet 2013](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanonc/article/PIIS0140-6736(12)61963-1/fulltext).
- **Serious adverse events:** increased risk of endometrial cancer/uterine sarcoma, thromboembolic events (DVT, PE, stroke), and cataracts; common effects include hot flashes, vaginal discharge/dryness, and mood changes [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/021807s005lbl.pdf).
- **Activation/interactions:** tamoxifen is bioactivated via CYP2D6 to endoxifen; strong CYP2D6 inhibitors (e.g., paroxetine, fluoxetine) may reduce active metabolite levels — venlafaxine is often preferred for co-managing hot flashes [Tamoxifen, StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK532905/).
- **Use with [[ovarian-function-suppression]]:** OFS + tamoxifen is an alternative to OFS + AI, generally reserved for lower-risk premenopausal patients within the OFS-eligible group, since OFS + AI was superior for higher-risk disease in [[soft-text-trials|SOFT/TEXT]] [[premenopausal-intermediate-oncotype-2026]].
