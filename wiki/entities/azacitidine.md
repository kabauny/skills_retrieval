# Azacitidine

## Overview
Azacitidine is a pyrimidine nucleoside analog of cytidine that acts as a hypomethylating agent (HMA). After phosphorylation and incorporation into DNA and RNA, it irreversibly inhibits DNA methyltransferase, causing DNA hypomethylation that restores normal differentiation and growth control in malignant hematopoietic cells, while also exerting direct cytotoxicity. It is a backbone therapy for myelodysplastic syndromes (MDS), chronic myelomonocytic leukemia (CMML), and acute myeloid leukemia (AML), and now anchors the venetoclax doublet in patients unfit for intensive chemotherapy.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: incorporation into DNA/RNA with irreversible inhibition of DNA methyltransferase, producing DNA hypomethylation plus direct cytotoxicity to abnormal hematopoietic cells [FDA label](https://packageinserts.bms.com/pi/pi_vidaza.pdf).
- Injectable Vidaza is FDA-approved across MDS FAB subtypes — refractory anemia (RA/RARS with cytopenias or transfusion need), RAEB, RAEB-T, and CMML — at 75 mg/m²/day SC or IV for 7 days per 28-day cycle [FDA label](https://packageinserts.bms.com/pi/pi_vidaza.pdf).
- Pivotal AZA-001 phase 3 trial in higher-risk MDS: median OS 24.4 vs 15.0 months vs conventional care, HR 0.58, a ~74% relative survival improvement [AZA-001](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3234073/).
- VIALE-A phase 3 (NEJM 2020): azacitidine + venetoclax vs azacitidine + placebo in untreated AML unfit for intensive chemo gave median OS 14.7 vs 9.6 months (HR 0.66, 95% CI 0.52–0.85, p<0.001) [VIALE-A](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2012971).
- VIALE-A composite remission (CR+CRi) was 66.4% with the doublet vs 28.3% with azacitidine alone [VIALE-A](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2012971).
- Most common adverse reactions (>30%) include nausea, anemia, thrombocytopenia, vomiting, pyrexia, leukopenia, diarrhea, injection-site erythema, constipation, and neutropenia; myelosuppression requires frequent CBC monitoring [FDA label](https://packageinserts.bms.com/pi/pi_vidaza.pdf).
- An oral formulation (Onureg, CC-486) is separately approved for continued/maintenance treatment of adult AML patients in first remission after induction chemotherapy [Onureg label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/050974s034lbl.pdf).
