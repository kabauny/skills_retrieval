# Pertuzumab

## Overview

Pertuzumab (Perjeta) is a recombinant humanized monoclonal antibody that binds the HER2 dimerization domain (subdomain II), a different epitope than trastuzumab. By blocking ligand-dependent HER2 heterodimerization (especially HER2:HER3), it complements trastuzumab to deliver more complete HER2 signaling blockade. It is given with trastuzumab plus chemotherapy for HER2-positive breast cancer in the metastatic, neoadjuvant, and adjuvant settings.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: humanized IgG1 monoclonal antibody binding HER2 subdomain II, inhibiting ligand-initiated HER2 heterodimerization (HER2:HER3) and downstream signaling, plus ADCC; complementary to trastuzumab's distinct epitope [FDA label](https://www.gene.com/download/pdf/perjeta_prescribing.pdf).
- Approved indications: with trastuzumab and docetaxel for HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer (first line), and with trastuzumab and chemotherapy for neoadjuvant and adjuvant treatment of HER2-positive early breast cancer [FDA label](https://www.gene.com/download/pdf/perjeta_prescribing.pdf).
- CLEOPATRA (first-line metastatic): adding pertuzumab to trastuzumab/docetaxel raised median overall survival to 56.5 vs 40.8 months (+15.7 mo; OS HR 0.68, 95% CI 0.56-0.84) [CLEOPATRA (NEJM)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1413513).
- NeoSphere (neoadjuvant): pertuzumab + trastuzumab + docetaxel nearly doubled pathologic complete response vs trastuzumab + docetaxel (45.8% vs 29.0%) [NeoSphere (Lancet Oncol)](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanonc/article/PIIS1470-2045(11)70336-9/abstract).
- APHINITY (adjuvant): adding pertuzumab improved invasive disease-free survival (HR 0.81, 95% CI 0.66-1.00, p=0.045), with benefit concentrated in node-positive disease [APHINITY (NEJM)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1703643).
- APHINITY final analysis at 11.3 years confirmed an overall survival benefit (HR 0.83, 95% CI 0.69-1.00, p=0.044; 10-year OS 91.6% vs 89.8%) [APHINITY final (ESMO Open)](https://www.esmoopen.com/article/S2059-7029(25)00981-0/fulltext).
- Notable adverse events: diarrhea, rash, neutropenia/febrile neutropenia, nausea, fatigue, and asymptomatic LVEF decline / left ventricular dysfunction; embryo-fetal toxicity is the boxed warning concern shared with trastuzumab [FDA label](https://www.gene.com/download/pdf/perjeta_prescribing.pdf).
