# COBRA Trial

## Overview

COBRA (NRG-GI005) was a phase II/III randomized trial testing whether circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) could direct adjuvant chemotherapy in resected stage IIA colon cancer lacking traditional high-risk features. Patients were randomized to active surveillance versus ctDNA assay-directed therapy, in which only ctDNA-positive patients received 6 months of FOLFOX or CAPOX. The trial was halted in 2024 after the pre-planned phase II analysis failed for futility: chemotherapy did not improve ctDNA clearance among ctDNA-positive patients versus observation.

## Key facts

- Design: phase II/III, 1:1 randomization, ~635 patients with resected low-risk stage IIA colon cancer (no perineural invasion, perforation, or obstruction) [ASCO Post, Jan 2024](https://ascopost.com/news/january-2024/role-of-postoperative-ctdna-in-patients-with-low-risk-stage-iia-colon-cancer/).
- Intervention: ctDNA assay-directed therapy arm — ctDNA-positive patients received 6 months of adjuvant FOLFOX or CAPOX; comparator arm was standard surveillance/observation [ASCO press release](https://old-prod.asco.org/about-asco/press-center/news-releases/new-study-adds-ambiguity-role-post-operative-ctdna-guide).
- Phase II primary endpoint was ctDNA clearance rate among baseline ctDNA-positive patients; chemotherapy was expected to raise clearance versus surveillance [NRG-GI005 JCO 2024](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2024.42.3_suppl.5).
- Result (first 16 ctDNA-positive patients): clearance 3/7 (43%) on the surveillance arm vs 1/9 (11%) on the chemotherapy arm — opposite the hypothesized direction, p=0.98 [ASCO Post, Jan 2024](https://ascopost.com/news/january-2024/role-of-postoperative-ctdna-in-patients-with-low-risk-stage-iia-colon-cancer/).
- Futility rule: one-sided Fisher exact test on the first 16 ctDNA-detected patients; the study stopped if p>0.35, which it did [NRG-GI005 JCO 2024](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2024.42.3_suppl.5).
- Status: trial closed early in 2024 for futility, never advancing to the phase III recurrence-free survival comparison [ASCO Daily News](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/closure-cobra-trial-creates-uncertainty-ctdna-guided-adjuvant-therapy-stage-ii-colon).
- Significance: results underscore that a ctDNA assay/endpoint must be clinically validated in the specific disease context before being used to guide therapy escalation in low-risk stage II disease [OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/presence-of-ctdna-does-not-predict-benefit-of-adjuvant-chemotherapy-in-stage-ii-colon-cancer).
