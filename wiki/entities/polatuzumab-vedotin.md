# Polatuzumab vedotin

## Overview
Polatuzumab vedotin (Polivy) is a CD79b-directed antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) used in diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL). It links an anti-CD79b monoclonal antibody to the microtubule inhibitor monomethyl auristatin E (MMAE), delivering cytotoxic payload to B cells. It is approved both for previously untreated DLBCL (with R-CHP) and for relapsed/refractory DLBCL (with bendamustine and rituximab).

## Key facts
- Mechanism: anti-CD79b antibody conjugated to MMAE, a microtubule inhibitor; CD79b is a B-cell receptor component expressed on >95% of DLBCL, enabling targeted internalization and payload delivery [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/761121s000lbl.pdf).
- First-line indication: in combination with rituximab, cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, and prednisone (R-CHP) for previously untreated DLBCL NOS or high-grade B-cell lymphoma (HGBL) with IPI score ≥2 (approved April 19, 2023) [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-polatuzumab-vedotin-piiq-previously-untreated-diffuse-large-b-cell-lymphoma-not).
- Relapsed/refractory indication: with bendamustine and a rituximab product for R/R DLBCL after ≥2 prior therapies (accelerated approval June 10, 2019) [FDA approval summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11649458/).
- Pivotal first-line trial POLARIX (n=879): Pola-R-CHP vs R-CHOP improved progression-free survival (HR 0.73; 2-yr PFS 76.7% vs 70.2%); overall survival did not differ at 2 years (HR 0.94) [POLARIX/NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2115304).
- Pivotal R/R trial GO29365 (randomized phase 2): Pola-BR vs BR improved complete response rate (40% vs 18%) and median OS (12.4 vs 4.7 months) [Blood Advances GO29365](https://ashpublications.org/bloodadvances/article/6/2/533/477889/Polatuzumab-vedotin-plus-bendamustine-and).
- Notable adverse events: peripheral neuropathy, myelosuppression (grade ≥3 neutropenia ~42%, thrombocytopenia ~40%, anemia ~24%, febrile neutropenia), serious/opportunistic infections, infusion-related reactions, tumor lysis syndrome, hepatotoxicity, and PML [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/761121s000lbl.pdf).
