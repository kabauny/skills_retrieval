# R-CHOP

## Overview

R-CHOP is a five-drug combination immunochemotherapy regimen given on a 21-day cycle, combining the anti-CD20 monoclonal antibody rituximab with CHOP (cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, vincristine, and prednisone). It is the international standard first-line regimen for diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) and is also used in other aggressive and indolent CD20-positive B-cell lymphomas. Typically 6 cycles are given for advanced disease, with rituximab dosed at 375 mg/m² on day 1 of each cycle. Its adoption in the early 2000s established immunochemotherapy as curative for the majority of DLBCL patients.

## Key facts

- Components: rituximab (anti-CD20 mAb) plus cyclophosphamide (alkylator), doxorubicin (anthracycline topoisomerase-II inhibitor), vincristine (vinca alkaloid), and prednisone (corticosteroid); rituximab dosed 375 mg/m² day 1 each 21-day cycle [GELA LNH-98.5, Blood](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/116/12/2040/27477/Long-term-outcome-of-patients-in-the-LNH-98-5).
- The pivotal GELA LNH-98.5 trial randomized 399 previously untreated DLBCL patients aged 60-80 (197 CHOP vs 202 R-CHOP); primary endpoint was event-free survival [GELA LNH-98.5, Blood](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2951853/).
- At 10-year follow-up, R-CHOP improved progression-free survival to 36.5% vs 20.1% and overall survival to 43.5% vs 27.6% versus CHOP alone [GELA LNH-98.5, Blood](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2951853/).
- Median overall survival was 7 years 9 months with R-CHOP versus 37 months with CHOP, a benefit sustained over a decade [GELA LNH-98.5, Blood](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/116/12/2040/27477/Long-term-outcome-of-patients-in-the-LNH-98-5).
- In younger good-prognosis patients (18-60 yrs), the phase 3 MInT trial showed adding rituximab raised 6-year event-free and overall survival, confirming benefit across age groups [MInT, Lancet Oncol](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21940214/).
- For the most favorable MInT subgroup (aaIPI 0, no bulk), 3-year outcomes were excellent: EFS 89%, PFS 95%, OS 98% [MInT, ASH/Blood](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/116/21/2826/112283/The-Role-of-the-Rituximab-Partner-Chemotherapy).
- Notable adverse events: myelosuppression with febrile neutropenia and infection, cumulative doxorubicin-related cardiotoxicity, vincristine peripheral neuropathy, and rituximab infusion reactions; the rituximab addition did not materially increase toxicity over CHOP [GELA LNH-98.5, Blood](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/116/12/2040/27477/Long-term-outcome-of-patients-in-the-LNH-98-5).
