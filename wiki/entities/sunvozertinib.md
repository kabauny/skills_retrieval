# sunvozertinib

## Overview

Sunvozertinib (DZD9008, brand name Zegfrovy) is an oral, irreversible EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor selectively targeting EGFR exon 20 insertion mutations while sparing wild-type EGFR. On July 2, 2025, the FDA granted it accelerated approval for adults with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC harboring EGFR exon 20 insertion mutations whose disease progressed on or after platinum-based chemotherapy. It is taken at 200 mg orally once daily with food, with an FDA-approved companion diagnostic (Oncomine Dx Express Test) for mutation detection.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: oral, irreversible (covalent) EGFR TKI designed to inhibit EGFR exon 20 insertion mutations with relative sparing of wild-type EGFR [FDA approval announcement](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-sunvozertinib-metastatic-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-egfr-exon-20).
- Approved (accelerated) July 2, 2025 for metastatic NSCLC with EGFR exon 20 insertion mutations after progression on platinum chemotherapy; the third agent approved in this second-line setting [OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-to-sunvozertinib-for-nsclc-with-egfr-exon-20-insertion-mutations).
- Pivotal trial WU-KONG1B (NCT03974022), an open-label multinational study; primary efficacy population of 85 patients dosed at 200 mg once daily [FDA approval announcement](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-sunvozertinib-metastatic-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-egfr-exon-20).
- Efficacy: confirmed overall response rate of 46% with median duration of response 11.1 months in WU-KONG1B [Oncology Nursing News](https://www.oncnursingnews.com/view/sunvozertinib-granted-fda-accelerated-approval-in-egfr-exon-20-nsclc).
- Supporting phase 2 WU-KONG6 (second-line) showed a confirmed ORR of 60.8% (95% CI, 50.4%-70.6%) with a disease control rate of 87.6% [WU-KONG1B/6, Annals of Oncology](https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(24)02836-9/fulltext).
- Most common any-grade adverse events: diarrhea (~67%), blood CPK increase (~58%), rash (~54%), and anemia (~49%); grade ≥3 events were mostly CPK increase (~17%) and diarrhea [CancerNetwork](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/sunvozertinib-yields-tolerability-efficacy-in-egfr-nsclc).
- Labeled warnings and precautions: interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis, gastrointestinal (diarrhea), dermatologic, ocular toxicity, and embryo-fetal toxicity [Zegfrovy prescribing information](https://www.drugs.com/pro/zegfrovy.html).
- Early first-line data (WU-KONG1, 300 mg) reported a confirmed ORR of 78.6% and median PFS 12.4 months; first-line use remains investigational [Targeted Oncology](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/sunvozertinib-shows-activity-tolerable-safety-in-egfr-exon20-insertion-positive-nsclc).
