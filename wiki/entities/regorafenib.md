# Regorafenib

## Overview
Regorafenib (Stivarga) is an oral small-molecule multikinase inhibitor that blocks angiogenic, stromal, and oncogenic kinases including VEGFR1-3, TIE2, PDGFR, FGFR, KIT, RET, and RAF. It is approved as later-line therapy in metastatic colorectal cancer, hepatocellular carcinoma after sorafenib, and gastrointestinal stromal tumor after imatinib and sunitinib. It carries a boxed warning for severe and fatal hepatotoxicity.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: orally active multikinase inhibitor of angiogenic (VEGFR1-3, TIE2), stromal (PDGFR-beta, FGFR), and oncogenic (KIT, RET, RAF, BRAF) kinases, dosed 160 mg once daily for the first 21 days of each 28-day cycle [FDA label](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/regorafenib).
- Approved for metastatic colorectal cancer previously treated with fluoropyrimidine-, oxaliplatin-, and irinotecan-based chemotherapy, anti-VEGF therapy, and (if RAS wild-type) anti-EGFR therapy [FDA label](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/regorafenib).
- CORRECT (phase 3, n=760 refractory mCRC): improved median OS to 6.4 vs 5.0 months versus placebo, OS HR 0.77 (95% CI 0.64-0.94), with PFS HR 0.49 [CORRECT](https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(20)34319-2/fulltext).
- RESORCE (phase 3, n=573 HCC progressing on sorafenib): median OS 10.6 vs 7.8 months, OS HR 0.62 (95% CI 0.50-0.78, p<0.001), supporting the 2017 HCC approval [RESORCE](https://ascopost.com/issues/february-25-2017/regorafenib-improves-survival-in-patients-with-hepatocellular-carcinoma-progressing-on-sorafenib/).
- GRID (phase 3, n=199 GIST after imatinib and sunitinib): median PFS 4.8 vs 0.9 months versus placebo (HR 0.27), the basis for the GIST indication [GRID](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/fda-approves-regorafenib-stivarga-gist).
- Most common adverse events include hand-foot skin reaction, fatigue/asthenia, diarrhea, hypertension, mucositis, anorexia, and weight loss; grade >=3 hand-foot skin reaction and hypertension are notable [GRID](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-09132-1).
- Boxed warning for severe and sometimes fatal hepatotoxicity; monitor liver function, and other serious risks include hemorrhage, GI perforation, wound-healing complications, and posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome [FDA label](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/regorafenib).
