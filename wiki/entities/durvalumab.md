# Durvalumab


## Brief

An immunotherapy drug (PD-L1 inhibitor) that has recently been approved in combination with BCG for the treatment of adult patients with BCG-naïve, high-risk non-muscle invasive bladder cancer.

## Why this matters

Durvalumab represents the first immunotherapy combination regimen approved for high-risk NMIBC in over 30 years, offering a new treatment option.
