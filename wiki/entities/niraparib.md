# Niraparib

## Overview
Niraparib is an orally bioavailable poly(ADP-ribose) polymerase (PARP-1 and PARP-2) inhibitor used as maintenance therapy in epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube, and primary peritoneal cancer. By trapping PARP at sites of DNA damage, it exploits synthetic lethality in tumors with homologous recombination deficiency (HRD), including BRCA-mutated disease. It is given once daily and is notable for not requiring concomitant chemotherapy during maintenance.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: inhibits PARP-1/PARP-2 and traps PARP-DNA complexes, blocking single-strand break repair and inducing synthetic lethality in homologous-recombination-deficient (e.g., BRCA-mutant) cells [FDA approval](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1910962).
- Principal indications: first-line maintenance for advanced ovarian/fallopian tube/primary peritoneal cancer responding to platinum chemotherapy; second-line maintenance after platinum response (restricted to germline BRCA-mutated patients following the 2022 OS review) [NOVA OS analysis](https://www.onclive.com/view/restricted-indication-for-maintenance-niraparib-therapy-in-recurrent-ovarian-cancer-further-supported-by-final-survival-analysis-of-nova-trial).
- Pivotal trial PRIMA/ENGOT-OV26/GOG-3012 (first-line maintenance): overall-population median PFS 13.8 vs 8.2 mo (HR 0.62, 95% CI 0.50-0.76, P<0.0001) [PRIMA](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1910962).
- In PRIMA, the HRD-positive subgroup showed median PFS 21.9 vs 10.4 mo (HR 0.43, 95% CI 0.31-0.59) [PRIMA](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1910962).
- Pivotal trial NOVA (recurrent, platinum-sensitive maintenance): gBRCA-mutated cohort median PFS 21.0 vs 5.5 mo (HR 0.27); non-gBRCA cohort 9.3 vs 3.9 mo (HR 0.45) [NOVA](https://www.onclive.com/view/restricted-indication-for-maintenance-niraparib-therapy-in-recurrent-ovarian-cancer-further-supported-by-final-survival-analysis-of-nova-trial).
- Notable adverse events: dose-related myelosuppression — grade >=3 thrombocytopenia (~40%), anemia (~32%), and neutropenia (~21%); rare myelodysplastic syndrome/AML (~1.2%) [PRIMA 3.5-yr follow-up](https://www.ejcancer.com/article/S0959-8049(23)00225-3/fulltext).
- Hypertension and fatigue are also common; an individualized starting dose by baseline weight/platelet count mitigates hematologic toxicity [PRIMA 3.5-yr follow-up](https://www.ejcancer.com/article/S0959-8049(23)00225-3/fulltext).
