# Bacillus Calmette-Guérin


## Brief

An attenuated strain of Mycobacterium bovis used as an immunotherapy delivered intravesically (into the bladder) for the treatment of early-stage bladder cancer, particularly NMIBC.

## Why this matters

BCG has been the standard of care for high-risk NMIBC for decades, and its combination with durvalumab marks a significant advancement.
