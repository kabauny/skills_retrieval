# Binimetinib

## Overview
Binimetinib (MEKTOVI) is an oral, reversible inhibitor of MEK1 and MEK2, kinases downstream of BRAF in the MAPK/ERK signaling pathway. It is approved in combination with the BRAF inhibitor encorafenib for unresectable or metastatic melanoma harboring a BRAF V600E or V600K mutation. Dual BRAF/MEK blockade improves efficacy and mitigates the paradoxical MAPK reactivation seen with BRAF inhibitor monotherapy.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: reversible inhibitor of MEK1/MEK2, upstream regulators of ERK; suppresses ERK phosphorylation and viability of BRAF-mutant melanoma cells [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/210498s011lbl.pdf).
- FDA-approved (2018) in combination with encorafenib for unresectable or metastatic melanoma with a BRAF V600E/V600K mutation, confirmed by an FDA-approved test [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/210498s011lbl.pdf).
- Pivotal phase 3 COLUMBUS trial (n=577) randomized patients to encorafenib + binimetinib, encorafenib alone, or vemurafenib [COLUMBUS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30219628/).
- COLUMBUS overall survival: median OS 33.6 months with encorafenib + binimetinib vs 16.9 months with vemurafenib (HR 0.61, 95% CI 0.47-0.79; p<0.0001) [COLUMBUS](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanonc/article/PIIS1470-2045(18)30497-2/fulltext).
- The combination also improved progression-free survival versus vemurafenib (primary endpoint reported in the initial COLUMBUS analysis) [COLUMBUS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30219628/).
- Most common (≥25%) adverse reactions with encorafenib: fatigue, nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, abdominal pain [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/210498s011lbl.pdf).
- Serious/notable toxicities: cardiomyopathy/left ventricular dysfunction (~7%), serous retinopathy and retinal vein occlusion, hemorrhage, venous thromboembolism, hepatotoxicity, and increased CPK/rhabdomyolysis [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/210498s011lbl.pdf).
