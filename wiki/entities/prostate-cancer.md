# Prostate cancer

## Overview
Prostate cancer is the most common non-skin cancer in U.S. men and the second leading cause of male cancer death; nearly 99% are adenocarcinomas. Disease is risk-stratified by Gleason grade group, PSA, and TNM stage, which set curative-intent local therapy versus systemic management. The biology is androgen-driven, and the central clinical axis is whether disease remains castration-sensitive (mHSPC) or has become castration-resistant (mCRPC) despite androgen deprivation therapy (ADT).

## Key facts
- Most common male malignancy in the U.S., with an estimated 313,780 new cases and 35,770 deaths in 2025; ~99% are adenocarcinomas, and late-stage incidence is rising ~4.6-4.8%/year [ACS prostate cancer statistics 2025](https://acsjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.3322/caac.70028).
- Localized disease is risk-stratified by Gleason grade group, PSA, and TNM stage: low/favorable-intermediate-risk may be managed with active surveillance, while higher-risk localized disease gets curative-intent radical prostatectomy or radiotherapy [NCCN Prostate Cancer v3.2026](https://jnccn.org/abstract/journals/jnccn/23/11/article-p469.xml).
- Key biomarkers gate therapy: germline/somatic HRR mutations (especially BRCA2) qualify patients for PARP inhibitors, MSI-high/dMMR enables pembrolizumab, and PSMA-PET positivity selects radioligand therapy [NCCN Prostate Cancer v3.2026](https://jnccn.org/abstract/journals/jnccn/23/11/article-p469.xml).
- In metastatic hormone-sensitive disease (mHSPC), ARASENS showed adding darolutamide to ADT + docetaxel reduced death risk by 32.5% (OS HR 0.68, 95% CI 0.57-0.80; 4-yr OS 62.7% vs 50.4%) [ARASENS, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2119115).
- In mCRPC with HRR alterations, the phase 3 PROfound trial of olaparib improved median radiographic PFS to 7.4 vs 3.6 mo overall, and in BRCA-altered tumors rPFS 9.8 vs 3.0 mo with median OS 20.1 vs 14.4 mo [PROfound, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1911440).
- For PSMA-positive mCRPC after an ARSI and taxane, the phase 3 VISION trial showed 177Lu-PSMA-617 plus standard of care improved OS (HR 0.62, median 15.3 vs 11.3 mo) and rPFS (HR 0.40, median 8.7 vs 3.4 mo) [VISION, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2107322).
- mCRPC treatment sequencing continues ADT and layers androgen-receptor pathway inhibitors (abiraterone, enzalutamide), taxane chemotherapy (docetaxel, cabazitaxel), radium-223 for bone-predominant disease, and biomarker-gated PARP inhibitors, immunotherapy, or radioligand therapy [NCCN Prostate Cancer v3.2026](https://jnccn.org/abstract/journals/jnccn/23/11/article-p469.xml).
