# Belzutifan

## Overview

Belzutifan (WELIREG) is an oral small-molecule inhibitor of hypoxia-inducible factor-2 alpha (HIF-2α), the first-in-class agent of its kind. It is FDA-approved for von Hippel-Lindau (VHL) disease-associated tumors not requiring immediate surgery and for advanced clear cell renal cell carcinoma (RCC) after prior PD-1/PD-L1 and VEGF-TKI therapy. Its use in paraganglioma remains investigational/off-label, of interest in genetically driven pseudohypoxic (e.g. VHL/EPAS1-pathway) disease.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: binds HIF-2α and blocks its dimerization with HIF-1β; when VHL function is impaired HIF-2α accumulates, so belzutifan reduces transcription of downstream oncogenic genes (VEGF, cyclin D1, GLUT1) to produce anti-tumor activity at the standard 120 mg once-daily dose [FDA label](https://www.merck.com/product/usa/pi_circulars/w/welireg/welireg_pi.pdf).
- Indication 1: adults with VHL disease who require therapy for associated RCC, CNS hemangioblastomas, or pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors, not requiring immediate surgery [FDA Approval Summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9669093/).
- Indication 2: adults with advanced RCC with a clear cell component following a PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor and a VEGF-TKI [FDA label](https://www.merck.com/product/usa/pi_circulars/w/welireg/welireg_pi.pdf).
- Pivotal VHL trial LITESPARK-004 (phase 2, single-arm): in VHL-associated RCC the objective response rate was 67% (95% CI 54-79%; 11% complete responses) at ~50-month follow-up, with median duration of response not reached [LITESPARK-004](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanonc/article/PIIS1470-2045(25)00099-3/abstract).
- Pivotal advanced-RCC trial LITESPARK-005 (phase 3, n=746) vs everolimus: PFS HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.63-0.90; p<0.001); overall survival did not reach statistical significance (HR 0.88, 95% CI 0.73-1.07) [LITESPARK-005 (NEJM)](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2313906).
- Boxed warning: embryo-fetal toxicity (embryo-fetal lethality and skeletal malformations in animals) — verify pregnancy status and use effective contraception [FDA label](https://www.merck.com/product/usa/pi_circulars/w/welireg/welireg_pi.pdf).
- Notable adverse events: dose-dependent anemia (can be severe, may require transfusion) and hypoxia (Grade 3 in ~10%, often requiring oxygen) are mechanism-based class effects; common reactions include fatigue, musculoskeletal pain, dyspnea, nausea, and headache [FDA label](https://www.merck.com/product/usa/pi_circulars/w/welireg/welireg_pi.pdf).
