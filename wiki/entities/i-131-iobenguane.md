# I-131 iobenguane


## Brief

A high-specific-activity radiopharmaceutical agent (marketed as Azedra) approved for patients with unresectable, locally advanced, or metastatic paragangliomas that show uptake on an MIBG scan.

## Why this matters

It provides targeted radiation delivery directly to paraganglioma cells that express specific receptors, representing a crucial radiopharmaceutical therapy option for this rare cancer.
