# docetaxel

## Overview

Docetaxel (Taxotere) is a semisynthetic taxane antineoplastic that promotes assembly and stabilization of microtubules, blocking depolymerization and arresting mitosis. It is a backbone cytotoxic across multiple solid tumors, with FDA approvals in breast, non-small cell lung, prostate, gastric, and head and neck cancers. It is most often dosed at 75 mg/m² every 3 weeks, frequently with prednisone or in combination regimens, and is limited primarily by myelosuppression.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: binds β-tubulin to promote assembly of stable, dysfunctional microtubule bundles and inhibit their disassembly, halting cells in mitosis and triggering apoptosis [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2013/020449s071lbl.pdf).
- FDA-approved indications include breast cancer (adjuvant node-positive and metastatic), non-small cell lung cancer, hormone-refractory metastatic prostate cancer (with prednisone), advanced gastric adenocarcinoma, and head and neck cancer [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2013/020449s071lbl.pdf).
- TAX 327 (mCRPC): docetaxel 75 mg/m² q3w plus prednisone vs mitoxantrone improved median OS to 18.9 vs 16.5 months, HR for death 0.76 (95% CI 0.62-0.94, p=0.009), establishing docetaxel as first-line chemotherapy for castration-resistant disease [TAX 327](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa040720).
- CHAARTED (E3805, mHSPC): adding docetaxel to ADT extended median OS to 57.6 vs 44.0 months (HR 0.61, p=0.0003), with the largest benefit in high-volume disease [CHAARTED](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26244877/).
- TAX 317 (second-line NSCLC): docetaxel 75 mg/m² vs best supportive care improved median OS to 7.5 vs 4.6 months (p=0.010) and 1-year survival to 37% vs 11% [TAX 317](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2002.20.5.1344).
- Serious/dose-limiting toxicities include neutropenia (grade 4 in ~75-85% at 60-100 mg/m²), febrile neutropenia, hypersensitivity reactions, fluid retention, and toxic deaths; premedication with corticosteroids reduces hypersensitivity and fluid retention [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2013/020449s071lbl.pdf).
- Common adverse events: alopecia, peripheral neuropathy, fatigue/asthenia, mucositis, nail changes, diarrhea, nausea, and myalgia [Docetaxel StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK537242/).
