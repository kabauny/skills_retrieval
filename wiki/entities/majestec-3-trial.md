# MajesTEC-3 trial

## Overview

MajesTEC-3 is a phase 3, randomized, open-label trial testing the BCMA×CD3 bispecific antibody teclistamab plus subcutaneous daratumumab (Tec-Dara) against investigator's choice of daratumumab-based triplets (DPd or DVd) in relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma after 1–3 prior lines. Reported at ASH 2025, it showed a striking progression-free survival benefit (HR 0.17) and a statistically significant overall survival improvement at the first interim analysis. The results support teclistamab-based therapy as a potential standard of care as early as second line.

## Key facts

- Design: phase 3, randomized 1:1, open-label; N=587 with relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma after 1–3 prior lines; excluded prior BCMA-directed therapy and anti-CD38-refractory disease (median age 65) [[MajesTEC-3, Blood ASH 2025 LBA-6](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/146/Supplement%202/LBA-6/556932/Phase-3-randomized-study-of-teclistamab-plus)].
- Intervention vs comparator: teclistamab + subcutaneous daratumumab (Tec-Dara) versus investigator's choice of daratumumab + pomalidomide/dexamethasone (DPd) or daratumumab + bortezomib/dexamethasone (DVd) [[MajesTEC-3, Blood ASH 2025 LBA-6](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/146/Supplement%202/LBA-6/556932/Phase-3-randomized-study-of-teclistamab-plus)].
- Primary endpoint PFS: median not reached with Tec-Dara vs 18.1 months for control; HR 0.17 (95% CI 0.12–0.23; p<0.001); 36-month PFS 83.4% vs 29.7% [[OncoDaily](https://oncodaily.com/oncolibrary/majestec-3)].
- Overall survival: 36-month OS 83.3% vs 65.0%, HR 0.46 (95% CI 0.32–0.65; p<0.0001), a ~54% reduction in risk of death [[OncoDaily](https://oncodaily.com/oncolibrary/majestec-3)].
- Depth of response: complete response or better 81.8% vs ~32%, overall response 89.0% vs 75.3%, MRD negativity (10⁻⁵) 58.4% vs 17.1% [[Multiple Myeloma Hub](https://multiplemyelomahub.com/medical-information/majestec3-teclistamab-daratumumab-for-rrmm)].
- Safety: cytokine release syndrome largely grade 1–2; grade 3/4 infections 54.1% vs 43.4% (mitigated by IVIG/prophylaxis); ICANS 1.1%; serious AEs 70.7% vs 62.4% [[OncoDaily](https://oncodaily.com/oncolibrary/majestec-3)].
- Significance: confirms teclistamab efficacy at second line; teclistamab/daratumumab combination drew an FDA approval discussion and is positioned as a potential new standard of care for RRMM [[CancerNetwork](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/teclistamab-sc-daratumumab-improves-survival-in-r-r-multiple-myeloma)].
