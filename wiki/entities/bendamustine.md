# Bendamustine

## Overview
Bendamustine is a bifunctional nitrogen-mustard alkylating agent with structural features of both an alkylator and a purine analog. It is FDA-approved for chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) and for indolent B-cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma (NHL), and—combined with rituximab—is a widely used first-line regimen for indolent and mantle-cell lymphomas. It is given intravenously and carries substantial myelosuppression and infection risk.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: alkylating agent that forms intra- and inter-strand DNA cross-links via its nitrogen-mustard group, causing durable double-strand breaks that are less readily repaired than those from melphalan, cyclophosphamide, or carmustine [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2008/022303lbl.pdf).
- FDA indications: CLL, and indolent B-cell NHL that has progressed during or within 6 months of a rituximab-containing regimen [FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=39d53698-57fa-7c99-fc5b-f52a55684826).
- Pivotal first-line trial (StiL NHL1, phase 3): bendamustine+rituximab (BR) vs R-CHOP in indolent/mantle-cell lymphoma gave median PFS 69.5 vs 31.2 months (HR ~0.58, ~42% reduction in progression risk, P<.001) [StiL NHL1 (Lancet 2013)](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(12)61763-2/abstract).
- StiL NHL1 also showed higher CR rate for BR (40%) vs R-CHOP (31%) and ORR ~92.7% vs 91.3% with a more favorable toxicity profile [StiL NHL1 (Lancet 2013)](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(12)61763-2/abstract).
- Rituximab-refractory indolent NHL pivotal trial: single-agent bendamustine 120 mg/m² (days 1–2, q21d) gave ORR 75%, median PFS 9.3 months, median DOR 9.2 months [Bendamustine in rituximab-refractory NHL](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01289223).
- Hematologic toxicity: severe (Grade 3–4) myelosuppression occurred in ~98% of NHL-study patients; serious adverse reactions in ~37%, most commonly febrile neutropenia and pneumonia [FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=39d53698-57fa-7c99-fc5b-f52a55684826).
- Other serious risks: infections/infection reactivation, anaphylactic and infusion reactions, tumor lysis syndrome, severe/fatal skin reactions (SJS, TEN, DRESS), and secondary malignancies (MDS) [FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=39d53698-57fa-7c99-fc5b-f52a55684826).
