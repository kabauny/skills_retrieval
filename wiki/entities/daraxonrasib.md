# daraxonrasib

## Overview

Daraxonrasib (RMC-6236) is an orally administered, direct RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor developed by Revolution Medicines that targets the active, GTP-bound (ON) state of multiple oncogenic RAS variants (KRAS, NRAS, HRAS) across G12, G13, and Q61 positions, as well as RAS wild-type signaling. It is investigational (not yet FDA-approved as of mid-2026) and is furthest advanced in previously treated metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC). The FDA granted it Breakthrough Therapy Designation for KRAS G12-mutant pretreated metastatic PDAC and has permitted an expanded access protocol pending approval.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: a tri-complex RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor that binds active GTP-bound RAS, blocking a broad spectrum of oncogenic RAS drivers rather than a single allele [OncLive, RAS(ON) variants](https://www.onclive.com/view/daraxonrasib-demonstrates-efficacy-potential-to-inhibit-major-ras-on-variants-in-ras-pdac).
- Pivotal phase 3 RASolute 302 (NCT06625320): global, randomized, open-label trial of ~501 patients with metastatic PDAC after one prior line, randomized 1:1 to daraxonrasib 300 mg orally once daily vs investigator's choice chemotherapy; co-primary endpoints PFS and OS [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06625320](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06625320).
- RASolute 302 first interim analysis: median OS 13.2 vs 6.7 months (HR 0.40; P<.0001) in the ITT population — roughly a doubling and a 60% reduction in risk of death [OncLive, RASolute 302](https://www.onclive.com/view/daraxonrasib-yields-significant-survival-advantages-vs-chemotherapy-in-metastatic-pancreatic-cancer).
- RASolute 302 PFS: median 7.2 vs 3.6 months (HR 0.49; 95% CI 0.36-0.64) overall; in the RAS G12 subgroup PFS 7.3 vs 3.5 months (HR 0.45) and OS 13.2 vs 6.6 months (HR 0.40) [Clinical Advisor, RASolute-302](https://www.clinicaladvisor.com/news/rasolute-302-daraxonrasib-improves-survival-orr-in-metastatic-pdac/).
- Phase 1 RMC-6236-001 (300 mg, RAS-mutant PDAC): ORR 29% and median OS 15.6 months across all RAS mutations (n=38); ORR 35%, PFS 8.5 mo, OS 13.1 mo in RAS G12 (n=26) [CancerNetwork, RMC-6236-001](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/daraxonrasib-shows-anti-tumor-activity-in-pretreated-ras-pancreatic-cancer).
- Most common treatment-related adverse events are rash (~90%), stomatitis/mucositis, diarrhea, and nausea; grade ≥3 events in ~30-34%, with rash and stomatitis the leading causes of dose reduction [CancerNetwork, RMC-6236-001](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/daraxonrasib-shows-anti-tumor-activity-in-pretreated-ras-pancreatic-cancer).
- Regulatory status: FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation (June 2025) for previously treated metastatic PDAC with KRAS G12 mutations, plus Orphan Drug Designation and an FDA-permitted expanded access protocol [OncLive, BTD](https://www.onclive.com/view/fda-grants-breakthrough-therapy-designation-to-daraxonrasib-for-kras-g12x-mutant-metastatic-pdac).
- Benefit extended across both RAS-mutant and RAS wild-type PDAC in RASolute 302, supporting the multi-selective mechanism rather than single-allele targeting [OncLive, RASolute 302](https://www.onclive.com/view/daraxonrasib-yields-significant-survival-advantages-vs-chemotherapy-in-metastatic-pancreatic-cancer).
