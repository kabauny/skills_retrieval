# Panitumumab

## Overview
Panitumumab (Vectibix) is a fully human IgG2 monoclonal antibody that targets the epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR). It is approved for RAS wild-type metastatic colorectal cancer (mCRC), used both first-line with FOLFOX and as monotherapy after chemotherapy failure. Benefit is restricted to tumors lacking KRAS/NRAS mutations, and is greatest in left-sided primaries.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: fully human anti-EGFR monoclonal antibody that blocks endogenous ligand binding to the EGFR extracellular domain and promotes receptor internalization and degradation, interrupting downstream proliferative signaling [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/125147s213lbl.pdf).
- Principal indications: RAS wild-type (no KRAS or NRAS mutation in exons 2/3/4) mCRC — first-line with FOLFOX and as monotherapy after fluoropyrimidine-, oxaliplatin-, and irinotecan-containing chemotherapy [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/125147s213lbl.pdf).
- RAS biomarker gating: KRAS/NRAS mutations confer resistance; the indication was refined in 2017 from KRAS wild-type to extended RAS wild-type based on retrospective PRIME analysis [Medical Genetics Summaries](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK564800/).
- Pivotal PRIME trial: in RAS wild-type patients, panitumumab + FOLFOX4 vs FOLFOX4 improved PFS (HR 0.72, +2.4 mo) and OS (HR 0.77; median OS 40.2 vs 24.1 mo, +5.4 mo) [PRIME final results](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S092375341936692X).
- ASPECCT trial: panitumumab was non-inferior to cetuximab for OS in chemorefractory KRAS wild-type mCRC (1010 patients randomized) [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/125147s213lbl.pdf).
- Sidedness: across PRIME/PEAK/study 181, anti-EGFR benefit is striking in left-sided RAS wild-type tumors but absent in right-sided primaries [BJC analysis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41416-018-0165-z).
- Notable adverse events: dermatologic toxicity in ~90% (Grade 3+ in 15%; acneiform dermatitis, paronychia, fissures), hypomagnesemia, diarrhea, and infusion/anaphylactic reactions [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/125147s213lbl.pdf).
