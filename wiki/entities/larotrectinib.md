# Larotrectinib

## Overview
Larotrectinib (Vitrakvi) is a first-in-class, oral, highly selective small-molecule inhibitor of the tropomyosin receptor kinases TRKA, TRKB, and TRKC, encoded by NTRK1/2/3. It was the first tissue-agnostic targeted therapy approved for any solid tumor harboring an NTRK gene fusion, in both adults and children. Activity is restricted to tumors driven by oncogenic NTRK fusions and is undermined by acquired kinase-domain resistance mutations.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: selectively inhibits TRKA/B/C; constitutively activated chimeric TRK fusion proteins act as oncogenic drivers, and larotrectinib blocks their downstream proliferation/survival signaling [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/210861s000lbl.pdf).
- Indication: tissue-agnostic approval (accelerated Nov 2018, full approval 2020) for adult and pediatric patients with solid tumors bearing an NTRK gene fusion without a known acquired resistance mutation, that are metastatic or where resection would cause severe morbidity, with no satisfactory alternatives or after progression [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-disco-fda-approval-larotrectinib-advanced-malignancies-ntrk-gene-fusion).
- Pivotal evidence came from three single-arm trials — LOXO-TRK-14001 (NCT02122913), the pediatric SCOUT study (NCT02637687), and the adult/adolescent basket NAVIGATE study (NCT02576431) [FDA DISCO](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-disco-fda-approval-larotrectinib-advanced-malignancies-ntrk-gene-fusion).
- Primary integrated analysis (first 55 patients, 17 tumor types): overall response rate 75% (44/55), including 22% complete and 53% partial responses [Drilon NEJM 2018](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1714448).
- Durable benefit: median duration of response 35.2 months and median progression-free survival 25.8 months in the primary cohort [Drilon NEJM 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5857389/).
- Pooled long-term efficacy across all three trials (n≈339) showed ORR 60% (95% CI 55–65%), with deep responses including ~24% complete responses [Targeted Oncology](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/fda-awards-full-approval-to-larotrectinib-in-ntrk-solid-tumors).
- Notable adverse events: transaminase (ALT/AST) elevations, dizziness, fatigue, nausea, anemia, and neutropenia; serious labeled risks include hepatotoxicity and neurologic/CNS effects, prompting dose reductions in ~15% of the primary cohort [Drilon NEJM 2018](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1714448).
