# Epirubicin

## Overview
Epirubicin (brand name Ellence) is an anthracycline cytotoxic antibiotic, the 4'-epimer of doxorubicin. It works by intercalating into DNA and inhibiting topoisomerase II, blocking DNA replication and transcription. It was FDA-approved in 1999 as a component of adjuvant therapy for node-positive breast cancer and is also widely used in regimens for gastric and other cancers.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: intercalates between DNA base pairs and triggers topoisomerase II-mediated DNA cleavage, blocking replication/transcription; also generates cytotoxic free radicals causing lipid peroxidation [DrugBank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epirubicin).
- Principal FDA indication: adjuvant therapy in patients with evidence of axillary node tumor involvement following resection of primary breast cancer (approved 1999) [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2014/050778s021lbl.pdf).
- Pivotal FASG-05 trial (node-positive early breast cancer): FEC 100 vs FEC 50 improved 10-year disease-free survival to 50.7% vs 45.3% (Wilcoxon P=.036) [FASG-05](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15837983/).
- FASG-05: FEC 100 also improved 10-year overall survival to 54.8% vs 50.0% with FEC 50 (Wilcoxon P=.038), establishing a dose-response benefit [FASG-05](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15837983/).
- Boxed warnings: dose-dependent cardiotoxicity/congestive heart failure (incidence ~0.9% at 550 mg/m2, 1.6% at 700 mg/m2, 3.3% at 900 mg/m2 cumulative), severe myelosuppression, and secondary acute myeloid leukemia [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2014/050778s021lbl.pdf).
- Cumulative dose of 900 mg/m2 should only be exceeded with extreme caution due to the rising risk of irreversible cardiomyopathy [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2014/050778s021lbl.pdf).
- Other notable toxicities: vesicant extravasation causing tissue necrosis, severe nausea/vomiting, alopecia, mucositis, and amenorrhea [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2014/050778s021lbl.pdf).
- Typically dosed at 100-120 mg/m2 IV per cycle, often combined with fluorouracil and cyclophosphamide (FEC regimen); has a more favorable cardiac profile per milligram than doxorubicin allowing higher dosing [FASG-05](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15837983/).
