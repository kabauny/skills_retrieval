# Cetuximab

## Overview
Cetuximab (ERBITUX) is a recombinant human/mouse chimeric monoclonal antibody that targets the epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR). By binding the extracellular domain of EGFR it blocks ligand-driven activation of the EGFR/RAS/RAF/MAPK pathway and may also engage antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity. It is approved for *RAS* wild-type metastatic colorectal cancer and for squamous cell carcinoma of the head and neck.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: EGFR antagonist that binds the receptor's extracellular domain, downregulating EGFR/KRAS/RAF/MAPK signaling; activity requires tumors to be *RAS* wild-type in colorectal cancer [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/125084s273lbl.pdf).
- Indicated for EGFR-expressing *KRAS* wild-type metastatic colorectal cancer (with chemotherapy or as monotherapy after oxaliplatin/irinotecan failure) and, with encorafenib, for *BRAF* V600E-mutant mCRC after prior therapy [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/125084s273lbl.pdf).
- Indicated for SCCHN: with radiotherapy for locally advanced disease and with platinum-based chemotherapy for first-line recurrent/metastatic disease [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/125084s273lbl.pdf).
- CRYSTAL trial (first-line *KRAS* wild-type mCRC): adding cetuximab to FOLFIRI improved median OS to 23.5 vs 20.0 mo (HR 0.796, p=0.0093) and PFS (HR 0.696) [CRYSTAL](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa0805019).
- Bonner trial (locally advanced SCCHN): cetuximab plus radiotherapy improved OS vs radiotherapy alone (5-yr OS 45.6% vs 36.4%; HR 0.73, 95% CI 0.56-0.95, p=0.018) [Bonner/Lancet Oncol](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanonc/article/PIIS1470-2045(09)70311-0/abstract).
- EXTREME trial (first-line recurrent/metastatic SCCHN): cetuximab added to platinum/5-FU improved median OS to 10.1 vs 7.4 mo (p=0.04) and PFS to 5.6 vs 3.3 mo [EXTREME](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19828002/).
- Boxed warnings: infusion reactions and cardiopulmonary arrest/sudden death (2-3% in SCCHN); other key toxicities include severe dermatologic reactions (acneiform rash), hypomagnesemia (55% of patients), pulmonary toxicity, and embryo-fetal toxicity [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/125084s273lbl.pdf).
