# Exemestane

## Overview
Exemestane (Aromasin) is an oral steroidal (type I) aromatase inhibitor that irreversibly inactivates the aromatase enzyme, depriving estrogen-dependent breast cancers of estrogen in postmenopausal women. It is approved for adjuvant treatment of postmenopausal women with ER-positive early breast cancer after 2–3 years of tamoxifen and for advanced breast cancer that has progressed on tamoxifen. It is not indicated in premenopausal women.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: a steroidal, irreversible ("suicide") aromatase inhibitor that binds and permanently inactivates aromatase, the enzyme converting adrenal/ovarian androgens to estrogens in peripheral tissue; standard dose 25 mg once daily after a meal [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/020753s020lbl.pdf).
- Approved for adjuvant treatment of postmenopausal ER+ early breast cancer in patients who switch after 2–3 years of tamoxifen to complete ~5 years of endocrine therapy, and for advanced breast cancer progressing after tamoxifen [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/020753s020lbl.pdf).
- Pivotal Intergroup Exemestane Study (IES): switching to exemestane after 2–3 years of tamoxifen improved disease-free survival vs. continuing tamoxifen (HR 0.76, 95% CI 0.66–0.88, p=0.0001) at 55.7-month follow-up [IES Lancet](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17307102/).
- IES long-term follow-up (~10 years) confirmed a durable ER+/unknown DFS benefit (HR 0.82, 95% CI 0.73–0.92, p=0.0009) and an overall survival benefit (HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.75–0.99, p=0.04) [IES long-term follow-up](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6175047/).
- Prevention (MAP.3, NEJM): in 4,560 high-risk postmenopausal women, exemestane reduced annual incidence of invasive breast cancer by 65% (0.19% vs 0.55%; 11 vs 32 cancers) at 35-month median follow-up [MAP.3 NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/nejmoa1103507).
- Common adverse events (adjuvant): hot flushes (~21%), fatigue (~16%), arthralgia (~15%), headache, insomnia, and increased sweating [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/020753s020lbl.pdf).
- Serious risks: reduced bone mineral density and increased fracture risk (monitor BMD at baseline and during therapy) and a higher rate of cardiac ischemic events vs. tamoxifen (1.6% vs 0.6%) [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/020753s020lbl.pdf).
