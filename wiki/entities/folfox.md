# FOLFOX

## Overview

FOLFOX is a cytotoxic combination chemotherapy regimen combining oxaliplatin (a third-generation platinum DNA cross-linking agent) with infusional/bolus 5-fluorouracil (a fluoropyrimidine thymidylate synthase inhibitor) and leucovorin (folinic acid, which potentiates 5-FU). It is a backbone regimen in colorectal cancer, used both as adjuvant therapy after curative resection of stage III (and selected high-risk stage II) colon cancer and as first-line treatment of metastatic disease. FOLFOX (typically as the FOLFOX4 or mFOLFOX6 schedule) is also a chemotherapy backbone in gastric/gastroesophageal and other GI cancers, often combined with biologics.

## Key facts

- Mechanism/components: oxaliplatin forms platinum-DNA adducts blocking replication, while leucovorin-potentiated 5-FU inhibits thymidylate synthase; the combination is synergistic versus 5-FU/leucovorin alone [FOLFOX/FLOX review, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2588473/).
- Adjuvant approval was driven by the phase 3 MOSAIC trial (N=2,246, resected stage II/III colon cancer) randomizing FOLFOX4 vs LV5FU2 (infusional 5-FU/leucovorin) for 6 months [MOSAIC, NEJM 2004](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa032709).
- MOSAIC met its primary endpoint of disease-free survival: 5-year DFS 73.3% (FOLFOX4) vs 67.4% (LV5FU2), HR 0.80 (95% CI 0.68–0.93; P=.003) [MOSAIC OS update, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19451431/).
- In stage III disease, FOLFOX4 improved 6-year overall survival to 72.9% vs 68.7% (HR 0.80; 95% CI 0.65–0.97; P=.023); no OS benefit was seen in stage II [MOSAIC OS update, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19451431/).
- In metastatic CRC, the phase 3 N9741 trial (N=795) showed first-line FOLFOX gave longer median OS (~19–20 mo) than IFL (irinotecan/bolus 5-FU/LV, ~15 mo), establishing FOLFOX as a first-line standard [N9741 review, CancerNetwork](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/first-line-folfox4-ups-survival-advanced-colon-cancer).
- The signature dose-limiting toxicity is oxaliplatin-induced cumulative peripheral sensory neuropathy; in MOSAIC grade 3 neuropathy was ~12% at end of treatment, falling to 1.3% at 12 months and 0.7% at 48 months [MOSAIC OS update, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19451431/).
- Other notable adverse events include neutropenia, diarrhea, nausea/vomiting, mucositis, and acute cold-induced dysesthesias characteristic of oxaliplatin [FOLFOX/FLOX review, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2588473/).
- Beyond CRC, FOLFOX serves as a chemotherapy backbone in gastric/gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma, where it is combined with agents such as anti-HER2 therapy in HER2-positive disease [FOLFOX/FLOX review, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2588473/).
