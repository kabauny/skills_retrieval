# Enfortumab vedotin

## Overview
Enfortumab vedotin (Padcev) is a Nectin-4-directed antibody-drug conjugate that delivers the microtubule inhibitor monomethyl auristatin E (MMAE) to urothelial tumor cells. After binding Nectin-4 (highly expressed on urothelial carcinoma), the conjugate is internalized and MMAE is released, disrupting the microtubule network and triggering apoptosis. It is a standard-of-care agent in advanced urothelial cancer, both with pembrolizumab in the first line and as monotherapy after prior therapy.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: fully human anti-Nectin-4 monoclonal antibody linked via a cleavable linker to MMAE; internalization releases MMAE, causing microtubule disruption and cell death [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2023/761137s018.pdf).
- Approved for locally advanced or metastatic urothelial cancer: with pembrolizumab in the first line, and as monotherapy after prior platinum chemotherapy and PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor therapy [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2023/761137s018.pdf).
- Pivotal first-line EV-302/KEYNOTE-A39: enfortumab vedotin plus pembrolizumab vs chemotherapy roughly doubled median OS (31.5 vs 16.1 mo; HR 0.47, 95% CI 0.38-0.58) [EV-302 NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2312117).
- EV-302 progression-free survival was also nearly doubled (median 12.5 vs 6.3 mo; HR 0.45), establishing the chemotherapy-free doublet as first-line standard [EV-302 NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2312117).
- Pivotal monotherapy EV-301: in previously treated advanced disease, EV improved median OS vs chemotherapy (12.9 vs 9.0 mo; HR 0.70) and median PFS (5.6 vs 3.7 mo) [EV-301 NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2035807).
- Boxed warning: severe and fatal cutaneous reactions, including Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS) and toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN), occurring predominantly during cycle 1 [Padcev label](https://www.padcev.com/Content/hcp/pdf/PADCEV_label.pdf).
- Other notable toxicities: peripheral neuropathy, hyperglycemia, ocular disorders, pneumonitis, and infusion-site extravasation; skin reactions occurred in 55% of treated patients (Grade 3-4 in 13%) [Padcev label](https://www.padcev.com/Content/hcp/pdf/PADCEV_label.pdf).
