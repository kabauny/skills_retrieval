# Everolimus

## Overview
Everolimus (Afinitor) is an oral inhibitor of the mammalian target of rapamycin (mTOR) serine-threonine kinase. By binding the intracellular protein FKBP-12 to form a complex that inhibits mTORC1, it blocks downstream signaling (S6K1, 4E-BP1) and reduces HIF and VEGF expression, suppressing tumor cell proliferation and angiogenesis. It is approved across hormone receptor-positive breast cancer, advanced renal cell carcinoma, and well-differentiated neuroendocrine tumors.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: binds FKBP-12 to inhibit mTORC1, reducing phosphorylation of S6K1 and 4E-BP1 and downregulating HIF-1 and VEGF, thereby inhibiting proliferation and angiogenesis [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2010/022334s6lbl.pdf).
- Principal indications: HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer (with exemestane after letrozole/anastrozole failure); advanced renal cell carcinoma after sunitinib/sorafenib; and progressive well-differentiated non-functional GI/lung neuroendocrine tumors plus advanced pancreatic NET [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2010/022334s6lbl.pdf).
- BOLERO-2 (phase 3, breast cancer): everolimus + exemestane vs placebo + exemestane improved median PFS to 7.8 vs 3.2 months by investigator review (HR 0.45, 95% CI 0.38–0.54; central review 11.0 vs 4.1 mo, HR 0.38) [BOLERO-2 NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1109653).
- RECORD-1 (phase 3, advanced RCC): everolimus reduced risk of progression/death by 70%, median PFS 4.9 vs 1.9 months vs placebo (HR 0.30, 95% CI 0.22–0.40) [RECORD-1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18653228/).
- RADIANT-3 (phase 3, advanced pancreatic NET): median PFS 11.0 vs 4.6 months vs placebo (HR 0.35, 95% CI 0.27–0.45) [RADIANT-3 NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1009290).
- RADIANT-4 (phase 3, nonfunctional GI/lung NET): median PFS 11.0 vs 3.9 months vs placebo, ~52% reduction in risk of progression or death [RADIANT-4 RECORD-1 NET review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12325-010-0045-2).
- Notable adverse events: stomatitis/mouth ulcers (~40–67%, the most common dose-limiting toxicity), non-infectious pneumonitis (~13.5%, can be severe), infections, hyperglycemia/hyperlipidemia, rash, and myelosuppression [RECORD-1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18653228/), [everolimus stomatitis meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4769991/).
