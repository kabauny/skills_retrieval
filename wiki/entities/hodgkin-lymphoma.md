# Hodgkin lymphoma

## Overview
Hodgkin lymphoma is a B-cell–derived lymphoma defined by rare malignant Hodgkin/Reed-Sternberg (HRS) cells in a reactive inflammatory background, with a bimodal age distribution peaking in young adults. Classical HL (cHL) comprises ~95% of cases and is one of the most curable malignancies, so contemporary care emphasizes response-adapted therapy and minimizing late toxicity (cardiopulmonary disease, secondary cancers). Stage and interim PET response drive treatment intensity, and CD30 expression on HRS cells underpins targeted therapy.

## Key facts
- cHL accounts for ~95% of cases (the remainder being nodular lymphocyte-predominant HL); its histologic subtypes are nodular sclerosis (~70%), mixed cellularity (~25%), lymphocyte-rich (~5%), and lymphocyte-depleted (<1%), with nodular sclerosis predominating in young adults [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK499969/).
- Ann Arbor stage and disease bulk set treatment intent and intensity: limited (early) stage favors abbreviated chemotherapy ± involved-site radiation, while advanced (stage III/IV) disease uses full-course combination chemoimmunotherapy — both with curative intent in the large majority of patients [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK499969/).
- Interim FDG-PET after 2 cycles (PET2, scored by Deauville criteria) is the key dynamic biomarker: in the phase 3 RATHL trial PET2-negative patients (Deauville 1–3) had 3-year PFS ~85.7% vs ~67.5% for PET2-positive, and PET2-negative patients could safely drop bleomycin (AVD) without loss of efficacy [RATHL](https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(19)31794-6/fulltext).
- CD30 is uniformly expressed on HRS cells and gates the antibody-drug conjugate brentuximab vedotin; in advanced disease A+AVD improved survival over ABVD in the phase 3 ECHELON-1 trial (6-year OS 93.9% vs 89.4%; mortality HR 0.59, 95% CI 0.40–0.88, P=0.009) [ECHELON-1 OS](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2206125).
- PD-1 blockade is now front-line in advanced disease: the phase 3 SWOG S1826 trial showed nivolumab-AVD beat brentuximab vedotin-AVD with ~2-year PFS 92% vs 83% (roughly halving progression/death risk) and better tolerability, with less peripheral neuropathy and fewer treatment discontinuations [S1826 NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa2405888).
- Treatment landscape by line/stage: early-stage cHL uses 2–4 cycles ABVD ± involved-site RT; advanced-stage front-line is shifting to nivolumab-AVD (or A+AVD); relapsed/refractory disease is treated with salvage chemotherapy and autologous stem-cell transplant, with brentuximab vedotin and PD-1 inhibitors (nivolumab, pembrolizumab) for transplant-ineligible or post-transplant settings [S1826 NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa2405888).
