# Abemaciclib

## Overview
Abemaciclib (Verzenio) is an oral inhibitor of cyclin-dependent kinases 4 and 6 (CDK4/6) used in hormone receptor-positive (HR+), HER2-negative breast cancer. Unlike other CDK4/6 inhibitors, it is dosed continuously and crosses the blood-brain barrier. It is approved both for advanced/metastatic disease in combination with endocrine therapy and as adjuvant therapy for high-risk, node-positive early breast cancer.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: selectively inhibits CDK4 and CDK6, blocking phosphorylation of retinoblastoma protein, arresting cells at the G1-to-S transition and suppressing tumor proliferation [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/208716s017lbl.pdf).
- FDA-approved for HR+/HER2- advanced or metastatic breast cancer: with an aromatase inhibitor as initial therapy, with fulvestrant after endocrine progression, and as monotherapy after endocrine therapy plus chemotherapy [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/208716s017lbl.pdf).
- Also approved with endocrine therapy for adjuvant treatment of HR+/HER2-, node-positive, high-risk early breast cancer [FDA expansion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8987222/).
- monarchE (adjuvant): abemaciclib plus endocrine therapy improved invasive disease-free survival, IDFS HR 0.680, with 5-year IDFS 83.6% vs 76.0% [monarchE](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38194616/).
- MONARCH-2 (with fulvestrant, advanced): median OS 46.7 vs 37.3 months, HR 0.757 (P=.01), a ~9.4-month benefit [MONARCH-2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31563959/).
- Notable adverse events: diarrhea (very common, dose-limiting), neutropenia, fatigue, increased transaminases/hepatotoxicity, interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis, and venous thromboembolism [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/208716s017lbl.pdf).
