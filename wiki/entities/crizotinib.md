# Crizotinib

## Overview
Crizotinib is an oral, ATP-competitive small-molecule tyrosine kinase inhibitor of ALK, ROS1, and MET (c-Met/HGFR). It was the first ALK inhibitor approved for non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) and remains FDA-approved for ALK-positive and ROS1-positive metastatic NSCLC, as well as ALK-positive anaplastic large cell lymphoma and inflammatory myofibroblastic tumors in pediatric and young adult patients. In ALK-positive disease it has largely been superseded as first-line therapy by next-generation inhibitors (e.g., alectinib, lorlatinib), but it retains an important role in ROS1-positive NSCLC.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: selective ATP-competitive inhibitor of ALK, ROS1, and MET receptor tyrosine kinases and their oncogenic fusion variants, shutting off the driver survival signal in rearranged tumors [FDA/PMC review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4978556/).
- Principal indications: locally advanced/metastatic ALK-positive NSCLC (accelerated approval 2011) and metastatic ROS1-positive NSCLC (approved March 2016) [FDA/PMC review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4978556/).
- Pivotal trial PROFILE 1014 (first-line ALK+ NSCLC, n=343): crizotinib vs platinum/pemetrexed gave median PFS 10.9 vs 7.0 months (HR 0.45, P<0.001) [PROFILE 1014](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1408440).
- PROFILE 1014 objective response rate 74% vs 45% (P<0.001), with median duration of response 11.3 vs 5.3 months [PROFILE 1014](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1408440).
- PROFILE 1001 (ROS1-rearranged NSCLC, n=50): ORR 72%, median PFS 19.2 months, median duration of response 17.6 months, establishing ROS1 as a second crizotinib-sensitive subgroup [PROFILE 1001](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1406766).
- Notable adverse events: visual disturbances, peripheral/periorbital edema, nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, and elevated transaminases; serious risks include hepatotoxicity, interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis, QT prolongation, and bradycardia [PROFILE 1014](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1408440).
- Standard dosing is 250 mg orally twice daily [Final OS analysis, JCO](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29768118/).
