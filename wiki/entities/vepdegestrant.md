# Vepdegestrant

## Overview

Vepdegestrant (brand name Veppanu, developed by Arvinas/Pfizer) is an oral PROTAC (proteolysis-targeting chimera) estrogen receptor degrader — a heterobifunctional small molecule that recruits the ubiquitin-proteasome system to drive ER degradation, overcoming ESR1-mediated ligand-independent signaling. On May 1, 2026 the FDA approved it for ER-positive, HER2-negative advanced/metastatic breast cancer harboring an ESR1 mutation after progression on prior endocrine therapy. It is the first PROTAC degrader of any class to win FDA approval.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: oral heterobifunctional PROTAC that harnesses the ubiquitin-proteasome system to induce robust ER degradation, addressing ESR1-driven endocrine resistance [[VERITAC-2, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2505725)].
- FDA approved May 1, 2026 (first-ever PROTAC approval) for ESR1-mutated, ER+/HER2- advanced/metastatic breast cancer after progression on prior endocrine therapy [Arvinas press release](https://ir.arvinas.com/news-releases/news-release-details/arvinas-announces-fda-approval-veppanu-vepdegestrant-treatment).
- Recommended dose: 200 mg orally once daily with food until progression or unacceptable toxicity [AJMC](https://www.ajmc.com/view/fda-approves-vepdegestrant-for-esr1-mutated-er-positive-her2-negative-advanced-breast-cancer).
- Pivotal trial: phase 3 VERITAC-2 (N=624), randomized open-label vs fulvestrant, in ER+/HER2- advanced breast cancer progressing after a CDK4/6 inhibitor + endocrine therapy [[VERITAC-2, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2505725)].
- Efficacy in ESR1-mutated patients (n=270): median PFS 5.0 vs 2.1 months for fulvestrant (HR 0.58; 95% CI 0.43-0.78; P<0.001) [[VERITAC-2, NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2505725)].
- Objective response rate 19% vs 4% with fulvestrant in the ESR1-mutated population; benefit in the all-comer ITT population was not significant (PFS 3.8 vs 3.6 mo, HR 0.83; P=0.07) [OncologyNewsCentral](https://www.oncologynewscentral.com/drugs/info/fda-approves-vepdegestrant-for-esr1-mutated-advanced-breast-cancer).
- Most common adverse events: fatigue (27%), increased ALT (14%), nausea (13%), anemia (12%), neutropenia (12%), back pain (11%); generally well tolerated with ~3% discontinuation [Targeted Oncology](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/vepdegestrant-shows-pfs-advantage-in-esr1-mutant-er-her2-breast-cancer).
