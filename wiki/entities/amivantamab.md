# Amivantamab

## Overview

Amivantamab (Rybrevant, amivantamab-vmjw) is a fully human EGFR-MET bispecific antibody for EGFR-mutated non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). It targets both the EGFR and MET receptors, additionally engaging immune effector cells via antibody-dependent cytotoxicity. It is notable for activity against EGFR exon 20 insertion mutations, which are resistant to conventional EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs), and is also approved in combination with lazertinib for common EGFR mutations (exon 19 deletion / L858R).

## Key facts

- Mechanism: fully human bispecific antibody co-targeting EGFR and MET, with immune-cell-directed activity; first such agent approved for EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC [FDA, OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/fda-approves-amivantamab-plus-chemo-for-egfr-exon-20-insertion-nsclc).
- First approved (accelerated, May 2021) as monotherapy for EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC progressing on/after platinum chemotherapy [ASCO Post](https://ascopost.com/issues/july-25-2024/amivantamab-vmjw-in-nsclc-with-egfr-exon-20-insertion-mutations/).
- On March 1, 2024, FDA approved amivantamab + carboplatin/pemetrexed for first-line EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC, with the prior indication converted to traditional approval [OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/fda-approves-amivantamab-plus-chemo-for-egfr-exon-20-insertion-nsclc).
- PAPILLON (phase 3, N=308, 1L exon 20 insertion): amivantamab + chemo vs chemo improved median PFS to 11.4 vs 6.7 months (HR ~0.40, P<.001) [PAPILLON, OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/fda-approves-amivantamab-plus-chemo-for-egfr-exon-20-insertion-nsclc).
- MARIPOSA (phase 3, N=1074, 1L common EGFR mutations): amivantamab + lazertinib vs osimertinib improved median PFS to 23.7 vs 16.6 months (HR 0.70; 95% CI 0.58-0.85; P<.001) [MARIPOSA, Targeted Oncology](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/mariposa-confirms-os-benefit-for-amivantamab-lazertinib-in-egfr-nsclc).
- MARIPOSA final OS (median follow-up 37.8 mo): significant OS benefit, HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.61-0.92; P<.005); median OS not reached vs ~3 years with osimertinib [MARIPOSA OS, ASCO Post](https://ascopost.com/issues/may-10-2025/amivantamab-vmjw-plus-lazertinib-vs-osimertinib-in-egfr-mutated-non-small-cell-lung-cancer/).
- PALOMA-3 (phase 3): subcutaneous amivantamab + lazertinib was noninferior to IV (median PFS 6.1 vs 4.3 mo) with fewer infusion-related reactions, supporting an SC formulation [PALOMA-3, Targeted Oncology](https://www.targetedonc.com/view/leighl-covers-paloma-3-trial-of-sc-amivantamab-in-nsclc).
- Notable adverse events: infusion-related reactions (~63%), paronychia (~68%), rash (~62%, grade ≥3 ~15%), dermatitis acneiform, hypoalbuminemia, and venous thromboembolism (~37% with lazertinib combination) [MARIPOSA safety, ESMO](https://dailyreporter.esmo.org/european-lung-cancer-congress-2025/highlights-from-elcc-2025/overall-survival-benefit-achieved-with-amivantamab-plus-lazertinib-in-advanced-egfr-mutated-nsclc).
