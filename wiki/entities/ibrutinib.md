# Ibrutinib

## Overview
Ibrutinib (Imbruvica) is a first-in-class oral Bruton's tyrosine kinase (BTK) inhibitor that forms a covalent bond with a cysteine residue (Cys481) in the BTK active site, blocking B-cell receptor signaling that drives malignant B-cell proliferation and survival. It is approved across several B-cell malignancies including chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL), mantle cell lymphoma, Waldenström's macroglobulinemia, and marginal zone lymphoma, as well as chronic graft-versus-host disease. The CLL dose is 420 mg once daily until progression or unacceptable toxicity.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: irreversible covalent inhibitor of BTK (binds Cys481), shutting down B-cell receptor signaling required for malignant B-cell survival and microenvironment interaction [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2017/205552s016lbl.pdf).
- Principal FDA indications: CLL/SLL (first-line and relapsed), mantle cell lymphoma, Waldenström's macroglobulinemia, relapsed/refractory marginal zone lymphoma, and chronic graft-versus-host disease after failure of ≥1 line of systemic therapy [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/210563s000lbl.pdf).
- Pivotal trial RESONATE (phase 3, relapsed/refractory CLL/SLL, ibrutinib vs ofatumumab, n=391): median PFS not reached vs 8.1 mo, HR 0.215, and OS HR 0.434 (95% CI 0.238–0.789) at initial analysis [RESONATE NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1400376).
- RESONATE long-term follow-up (median 44 mo): sustained PFS benefit (HR 0.133) and OS HR 0.591 (95% CI 0.378–0.926, after crossover), with overall response reaching 91% [RESONATE Blood](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30842083/).
- First-line approval (March 2016) supported by RESONATE-2 in treatment-naïve CLL, demonstrating durable PFS benefit with up to 10-year follow-up confirming survival advantage [RESONATE-2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41375-019-0602-x).
- Serious/labeled risks: hemorrhage (~19–22%), atrial fibrillation/flutter (6–9%), hypertension (up to ~30%), infections, cytopenias, second primary malignancies, tumor lysis syndrome, and embryo-fetal toxicity [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2017/205552s016lbl.pdf).
- Common adverse reactions (≥20%) in B-cell malignancies: thrombocytopenia, neutropenia, diarrhea, anemia, musculoskeletal pain, rash, bruising, nausea, and fatigue [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/210563s000lbl.pdf).
