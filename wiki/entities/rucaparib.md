# Rucaparib

## Overview
Rucaparib (Rubraca) is an oral small-molecule inhibitor of poly(ADP-ribose) polymerase (PARP-1, -2, and -3), exploiting synthetic lethality in tumors with deficient homologous-recombination DNA repair (e.g. BRCA1/2 mutation). It is FDA-approved as maintenance therapy for platinum-sensitive recurrent ovarian cancer and for BRCA-mutated metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC). Its principal toxicity concern is myeloid (anemia, cytopenias, and rare MDS/AML).

## Key facts
- Mechanism: inhibits PARP enzymatic activity and traps PARP-DNA complexes, producing unrepaired DNA damage and apoptosis that is amplified in BRCA1/2- and HR-deficient cells [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/209115s014s015lbl.pdf).
- Indication 1 — maintenance treatment of recurrent epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube, or primary peritoneal cancer in complete or partial response to platinum-based chemotherapy [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-rucaparib-maintenance-treatment-recurrent-ovarian-fallopian-tube-or-primary-peritoneal).
- ARIEL3 (phase 3, n=561) — maintenance rucaparib vs placebo: ITT median PFS 10.8 vs 5.4 mo (HR 0.36, 95% CI 0.30–0.45); BRCA-mutant subgroup median PFS 16.6 vs 5.4 mo (HR 0.23) [ARIEL3](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanonc/article/PIIS1470-2045(20)30061-9/abstract).
- Indication 2 — BRCA1/2-mutated (germline or somatic) metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer; December 2025 expansion permits use prior to chemotherapy [FDA mCRPC approval](https://www.pharmaand.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FDA_sNDA_Rubraca_mCRPC_Approval_Announcement_FINAL_V2.pdf).
- TRITON3 (phase 3) — in the BRCA1/2 population, rucaparib vs physician's choice (docetaxel or second ARPI): median radiographic PFS 11.2 vs 6.4 mo (HR 0.50, 95% CI 0.36–0.69; P<0.0001) [TRITON3](https://ascopost.com/issues/march-10-2023/rucaparib-extends-progression-free-survival-in-selected-patients-with-metastatic-castration-resistant-prostate-cancer/).
- Common adverse reactions: nausea, fatigue/asthenia, anemia, elevated AST/ALT and creatinine, vomiting, thrombocytopenia, and neutropenia [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/209115s014s015lbl.pdf).
- Serious risks: myelodysplastic syndrome/acute myeloid leukemia (MDS/AML, ~1.6% of treated patients, sometimes fatal — monitor CBC monthly) and embryo-fetal harm [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/209115s014s015lbl.pdf).
