# Brentuximab vedotin

## Overview
Brentuximab vedotin (Adcetris) is a CD30-directed antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) that delivers the microtubule-disrupting agent monomethyl auristatin E (MMAE) to CD30-expressing malignant cells. It is approved across multiple CD30-positive lymphomas, including classical Hodgkin lymphoma, systemic anaplastic large-cell lymphoma and other peripheral T-cell lymphomas, and CD30-expressing cutaneous T-cell lymphoma. It is used both in frontline combination regimens and in the relapsed/refractory setting.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: an anti-CD30 chimeric IgG1 monoclonal antibody conjugated via a protease-cleavable valine-citrulline linker to MMAE; after binding CD30 and internalization, MMAE is released, binds tubulin, and induces cell-cycle arrest and apoptosis [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/125388s099lbl.pdf).
- Principal FDA-approved indications include previously untreated stage III/IV classical Hodgkin lymphoma (with AVD), previously untreated systemic anaplastic large-cell lymphoma and other CD30-expressing PTCL (with CHP), relapsed Hodgkin lymphoma and post-autologous-transplant consolidation, and CD30-expressing cutaneous T-cell lymphoma after prior systemic therapy [FDA approval - PTCL](https://www.cancer.gov/news-events/cancer-currents-blog/2022/fda-brentuximab-children-hodgkin-lymphoma).
- ECHELON-1 (frontline stage III/IV Hodgkin lymphoma, 1,334 patients): A+AVD vs ABVD improved the primary endpoint of modified progression-free survival (HR 0.77, 95% CI 0.60-0.98) [ECHELON-1](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1708984).
- ECHELON-1 long-term: at median 73 months, A+AVD improved overall survival vs ABVD (HR 0.59, 95% CI 0.40-0.88; 6-year OS 93.9% vs 89.4%), a 41% reduction in risk of death [ECHELON-1 OS](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2206125).
- ECHELON-2 (CD30-positive PTCL, 452 patients): A+CHP vs CHOP improved progression-free survival (HR 0.71, 95% CI 0.54-0.93) and overall survival (5-year OS 70.1% vs 61.0%, HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.53-0.99) [ECHELON-2 5-year](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9447792/).
- Notable adverse events: peripheral sensory neuropathy (often cumulative/dose-limiting), neutropenia and febrile neutropenia, infusion reactions, and a boxed warning for progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy (PML) due to JC virus reactivation [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2018/125388s099lbl.pdf).
