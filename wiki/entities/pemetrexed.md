# pemetrexed

## Overview

Pemetrexed (Alimta, Pemfexy) is a multitargeted antifolate antineoplastic agent first FDA-approved in 2004. It is a backbone cytotoxic for nonsquamous non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) and malignant pleural mesothelioma, used in platinum doublets, as continuation maintenance, and in modern chemoimmunotherapy regimens. It is explicitly not indicated for squamous NSCLC.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: a folate analog that inhibits multiple folate-dependent enzymes — thymidylate synthase (TS), dihydrofolate reductase (DHFR), and glycinamide ribonucleotide formyltransferase (GARFT) — disrupting purine and thymidine synthesis and DNA replication [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/208419s004lbl.pdf).
- Principal FDA indications: nonsquamous NSCLC (with cisplatin first-line, as continuation maintenance, and as single-agent second-line) and unresectable malignant pleural mesothelioma in combination with cisplatin; not indicated for squamous NSCLC [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/208419s004lbl.pdf).
- Mesothelioma pivotal trial (Vogelzang, phase III, n=448): pemetrexed plus cisplatin vs cisplatin alone improved median OS to 12.1 vs 9.3 months and response rate to 41% vs 17% [Vogelzang JCO 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12860938/).
- PARAMOUNT phase III maintenance trial: continuation pemetrexed vs placebo after pemetrexed/cisplatin induction improved median OS 13.9 vs 11.0 mo (HR 0.78, P=.0195) and PFS (HR 0.62) [PARAMOUNT JCO 2013](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2012.47.1102).
- KEYNOTE-189 phase III: adding pembrolizumab to pemetrexed-platinum in metastatic nonsquamous NSCLC improved OS (HR 0.56, final analysis; initial HR 0.49), establishing pemetrexed as the chemo backbone of first-line chemoimmunotherapy [KEYNOTE-189 5-yr JCO 2023](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.22.01989).
- Serious adverse events include myelosuppression (neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, anemia), fatigue, mucositis/stomatitis, nausea, rash, and renal toxicity; mandatory folic acid plus vitamin B12 supplementation and dexamethasone prophylaxis reduce hematologic and dermatologic toxicity [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/208419s004lbl.pdf).
