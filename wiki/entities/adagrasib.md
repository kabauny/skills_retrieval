# Adagrasib

## Overview
Adagrasib (KRAZATI, formerly MRTX849) is an orally available, irreversible small-molecule inhibitor of the KRAS G12C oncoprotein. It received FDA accelerated approval in December 2022 as monotherapy for previously treated KRAS G12C–mutated NSCLC, and in June 2024 in combination with cetuximab for previously treated KRAS G12C–mutated metastatic colorectal cancer. Activity required prior systemic therapy and a validated KRAS G12C companion diagnostic.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: covalently and irreversibly binds the mutant cysteine of KRAS G12C, locking the protein in its inactive GDP-bound state and blocking downstream MAPK signaling and proliferation [[FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=0b8bf078-34c2-4f45-9012-38a8ac082b01)].
- NSCLC indication: single-agent treatment of adults with KRAS G12C–mutated locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC after at least one prior systemic therapy (accelerated approval) [[FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=0b8bf078-34c2-4f45-9012-38a8ac082b01)].
- Pivotal NSCLC trial KRYSTAL-1 (phase 2): ORR 43% (95% CI 34–53), disease control rate 80%, median DOR 8.5 months [[OncLive/KRYSTAL-1](https://www.onclive.com/view/fda-approves-adagrasib-for-locally-advanced-or-metastatic-kras-g12-nsclc)].
- CRC indication: combination with cetuximab for previously treated KRAS G12C–mutated metastatic colorectal cancer (accelerated approval, June 2024) [[FDA CRC approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-adagrasib-cetuximab-kras-g12c-mutated-colorectal-cancer)].
- Pivotal CRC data KRYSTAL-1 (adagrasib + cetuximab, n=94): ORR 34.0%, DCR 85.1%, median DOR 5.8 mo, median PFS 6.9 mo, median OS 15.9 mo [[Cancer Discovery 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11152245/)].
- Most common NSCLC adverse reactions (≥25%): nausea (70%), diarrhea (70%), fatigue (59%), vomiting (57%), musculoskeletal pain (41%), hepatotoxicity (37%), renal impairment (36%) [[FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=0b8bf078-34c2-4f45-9012-38a8ac082b01)].
- Serious warnings: gastrointestinal toxicity, QTc interval prolongation (mean QTcF +18 ms at steady state), hepatotoxicity, and interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis — requiring ECG, electrolyte, and liver-enzyme monitoring [[FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=0b8bf078-34c2-4f45-9012-38a8ac082b01)].
