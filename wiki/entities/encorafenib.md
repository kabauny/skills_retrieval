# encorafenib

## Overview

Encorafenib (BRAFTOVI) is an oral small-molecule kinase inhibitor of mutant BRAF, used to block constitutive MAPK pathway signaling in BRAF V600-driven tumors. It is given in combination depending on tumor type: with binimetinib (a MEK inhibitor) in melanoma and NSCLC, and with cetuximab (an EGFR antibody) in colorectal cancer. The combination partner counteracts pathway reactivation/resistance and improves efficacy over BRAF inhibition alone.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: encorafenib inhibits mutant BRAF kinase (e.g., V600E), suppressing the BRAF-MEK-ERK (MAPK) pathway; it is paired with a MEK inhibitor or EGFR antibody to delay resistance from pathway reactivation [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/210496s018lbl.pdf).
- Approved indications: with binimetinib for unresectable/metastatic BRAF V600E/V600K melanoma; with binimetinib for metastatic BRAF V600E NSCLC; and with cetuximab for BRAF V600E metastatic colorectal cancer after prior therapy [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-traditional-approval-encorafenib-metastatic-colorectal-cancer-braf-v600e-mutation).
- Melanoma pivotal trial COLUMBUS (phase 3): encorafenib + binimetinib vs vemurafenib gave median OS 33.6 vs 16.9 months, OS HR 0.61 (95% CI 0.48-0.79) [COLUMBUS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30219628/).
- Colorectal pivotal trial BEACON CRC (phase 3): encorafenib + cetuximab doublet vs control chemotherapy gave median OS 8.4 vs 5.4 months, death HR 0.60 (95% CI 0.45-0.79; P<0.001) [BEACON CRC](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1908075).
- NSCLC pivotal trial PHAROS (phase 2): encorafenib + binimetinib achieved confirmed ORR of 75% (95% CI 62-85) in treatment-naive and 46% in previously treated BRAF V600E metastatic NSCLC [PHAROS](https://ascopost.com/issues/october-25-2023/encorafenib-and-binimetinib-in-braf-v600e-mutant-metastatic-nsclc/).
- Warnings/precautions: new primary cutaneous and noncutaneous malignancies, tumor promotion in BRAF wild-type tumors, cardiomyopathy, hepatotoxicity, hemorrhage, uveitis (~4% with binimetinib in COLUMBUS), dose-dependent QTc prolongation, and embryo-fetal toxicity [Pfizer warnings](https://www.pfizermedical.com/braftovi/warnings).
