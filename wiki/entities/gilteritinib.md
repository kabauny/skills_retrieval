# Gilteritinib

## Overview
Gilteritinib (Xospata, ASP2215) is an oral small-molecule tyrosine kinase inhibitor of FLT3 and AXL. It is the first single-agent FLT3 inhibitor approved for relapsed or refractory acute myeloid leukemia (AML) bearing a FLT3 mutation, where it improves overall survival versus salvage chemotherapy. It targets both FLT3 internal tandem duplication (ITD) and tyrosine kinase domain (D835/I836) mutations.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: oral dual inhibitor of FLT3 and AXL, blocking signaling driven by FLT3-ITD and FLT3 tyrosine kinase domain (D835/I836) activating mutations that promote leukemic proliferation [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/211349s001lbl.pdf).
- Indication: adults with relapsed or refractory AML with a FLT3 mutation (ITD or TKD) as detected by an FDA-approved test (LeukoStrat CDx FLT3 Mutation Assay); dosed at 120 mg orally once daily [FDA approval summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8506653/).
- Pivotal trial ADMIRAL (phase 3, NCT02421939) randomized 371 patients 2:1 to gilteritinib vs salvage chemotherapy; median OS 9.3 vs 5.6 months (HR for death 0.64; 95% CI 0.49-0.83; P<0.001) [ADMIRAL NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1902688).
- ADMIRAL response: CR/CRh rate 22.6% (55/243) with gilteritinib and median duration of response 7.4 months [FDA approval summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8506653/).
- Serious warnings: differentiation syndrome (~3% of patients, potentially fatal — treat with corticosteroids), QT/QTc prolongation (interrupt and reduce if QTcF >500 msec), and posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome (PRES — discontinue) [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/211349s001lbl.pdf).
- Most frequent serious nonhematologic adverse reactions: fever (13%), dyspnea (9%), renal impairment (8%), transaminase elevation (6%), and noninfectious diarrhea (5%) [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/211349s001lbl.pdf).
