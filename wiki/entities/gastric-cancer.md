# Gastric cancer

## Overview

Gastric (stomach) cancer is the fifth most common malignancy and third leading cause of cancer death worldwide, predominantly adenocarcinoma. It is molecularly heterogeneous, and biomarker testing (HER2, PD-L1, MMR/MSI, and increasingly Claudin18.2) now gates first-line therapy. Localized disease is treated with curative-intent surgery plus perioperative chemotherapy, while locally advanced unresectable or metastatic disease is managed with palliative-intent systemic therapy.

## Key facts

- Gastric cancer is the fifth most common cancer and third leading cause of cancer mortality globally; adenocarcinoma dominates, with the TCGA defining four molecular subtypes (EBV, MSI, chromosomal instability [~50%], genomically stable) and Lauren histology splitting tumors into intestinal vs diffuse types [NCCN Gastric Cancer v2.2025](https://jnccn.org/view/journals/jnccn/23/5/article-p169.xml?print=).
- Staging and resectability set treatment intent: resectable disease is managed with curative-intent gastrectomy plus perioperative/adjuvant chemotherapy, whereas locally advanced unresectable or metastatic disease is treated with palliative-intent systemic therapy [NCCN Gastric Cancer v2.2025](https://jnccn.org/view/journals/jnccn/23/5/article-p169.xml?print=).
- HER2 overexpression/amplification occurs in ~20% of tumors and is predictive; the pivotal ToGA phase 3 trial showed trastuzumab plus chemotherapy improved median OS to 13.8 vs 11.1 months (HR 0.74, 95% CI 0.60-0.91) [ToGA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20728210/).
- For HER2-positive disease, adding pembrolizumab to trastuzumab plus chemotherapy (KEYNOTE-811) improved median OS to 20.0 vs 16.8 months (HR 0.80, 95% CI 0.67-0.94), with FDA full approval restricted to PD-L1 CPS >=1 [FDA KEYNOTE-811 approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-pembrolizumab-her2-positive-gastric-or-gastroesophageal-junction-adenocarcinoma).
- PD-L1 (by CPS) gates checkpoint inhibition in HER2-negative disease: in CheckMate 649, first-line nivolumab plus chemotherapy in PD-L1 CPS >=5 tumors gave OS HR 0.71 (95% CI 0.61-0.81) at 5-year follow-up [CheckMate 649](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.23.01601).
- Claudin18.2 is an emerging biomarker: in the SPOTLIGHT phase 3 trial, first-line zolbetuximab plus mFOLFOX6 in CLDN18.2-positive, HER2-negative disease improved median OS to 18.2 vs 15.5 months (HR 0.75) [SPOTLIGHT](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(23)00620-7/abstract).
- MMR/MSI status is tested universally; MSI-high/dMMR tumors derive outsized benefit from PD-1 blockade, and biomarker testing (HER2, PD-L1, MMR/MSI) is recommended at diagnosis to direct first-line therapy [NCCN Gastric Cancer v2.2025](https://jnccn.org/view/journals/jnccn/23/5/article-p169.xml?print=).
