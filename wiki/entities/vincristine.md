# Vincristine

## Overview
Vincristine (Oncovin, Vincasar PFS) is a vinca alkaloid chemotherapy agent given intravenously, almost always as part of multi-agent regimens. It is a backbone component of CHOP/R-CHOP for non-Hodgkin lymphoma and of induction regimens for acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL), and is also used in several pediatric solid tumors. Its defining clinical liabilities are dose-limiting peripheral neuropathy and the fact that inadvertent intrathecal administration is uniformly fatal.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: binds tubulin and prevents its polymerization into microtubules, disrupting mitotic spindle formation and causing metaphase (M-phase) arrest and apoptosis in dividing cells [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK537122/).
- FDA-approved indications: acute leukemia, and (in combination with other agents) Hodgkin lymphoma, non-Hodgkin lymphomas, rhabdomyosarcoma, neuroblastoma, and Wilms tumor [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2014/071484s042lbl.pdf).
- It is a core component of CHOP and R-CHOP (rituximab, cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, vincristine, prednisone), the global standard for aggressive B-cell NHL such as DLBCL [LiverTox vinca alkaloids](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK547989/).
- Dose-limiting toxicity is a length-dependent, mixed sensorimotor and autonomic peripheral neuropathy (numbness/tingling, weakness, loss of deep tendon reflexes, constipation/paralytic ileus, urinary retention) that may be slow to reverse [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK537122/).
- BOXED-WARNING-level hazard: vincristine is for intravenous use only — intrathecal administration causes a fatal ascending myeloencephalopathy, so syringes must be labeled "For IV use only. Fatal if given by other routes" [Intrathecal vinca alkaloid review (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4959612/).
- Other notable adverse effects include alopecia, SIADH with hyponatremia, mild myelosuppression, and gastrointestinal autonomic effects; relatively low myelosuppression makes it a useful combination partner [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK537122/).
