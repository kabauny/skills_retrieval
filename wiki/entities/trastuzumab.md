# Trastuzumab

## Overview

Trastuzumab (Herceptin) is a humanized IgG1 monoclonal antibody directed against the extracellular domain of the HER2/neu receptor. It is the foundational HER2-targeted agent, used across HER2-positive breast and gastric cancers in both curative-intent and metastatic settings, typically combined with chemotherapy or other HER2 agents. Its approval transformed HER2-positive disease from an aggressive, poor-prognosis phenotype into one of the more treatable molecular subtypes.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: binds the HER2 extracellular domain, blocking ligand-independent HER2 dimerization and downstream PI3K/MAPK signaling, while also mediating antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) [FDA/StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK532246/).
- Principal FDA indications: HER2-overexpressing breast cancer (adjuvant with anthracycline/taxane-based chemotherapy, and metastatic as monotherapy or with paclitaxel) and HER2-positive metastatic gastric/gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma with cisplatin-based chemotherapy [FDA/StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK532246/).
- Pivotal metastatic breast trial (Slamon, NEJM 2001): adding trastuzumab to first-line chemotherapy improved median overall survival from 20.3 to 25.1 months and increased time to progression and response rate [Slamon 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11248153/).
- Pivotal adjuvant breast trials (NSABP B-31 / NCCTG N9831, NEJM 2005): 1 year of trastuzumab added to chemotherapy reduced recurrence (DFS HR ~0.48) and death (OS HR 0.67), with longer follow-up confirming a ~37% relative OS improvement (HR 0.63) [NSABP B-31/N9831](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa052122).
- Pivotal gastric trial (ToGA, Lancet 2010): trastuzumab plus chemotherapy improved median OS to 13.8 vs 11.1 months (HR 0.74, 95% CI 0.60–0.91, p=0.0046) in HER2-positive advanced gastric/GEJ cancer [ToGA](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20728210/).
- Serious adverse events: cardiomyopathy/decline in left ventricular ejection fraction and congestive heart failure (boxed warning), infusion reactions, pulmonary toxicity, and embryo-fetal toxicity; cardiac risk rises when given with anthracyclines [FDA/StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK532246/).
