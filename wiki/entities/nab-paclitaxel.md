# nab-paclitaxel

## Overview

nab-Paclitaxel (Abraxane) is a solvent-free, albumin-bound (130-nm nanoparticle) formulation of the taxane paclitaxel. By eliminating the Cremophor EL vehicle of solvent-based paclitaxel, it avoids the need for premedication against hypersensitivity and exploits albumin-mediated transport to deliver paclitaxel to tumor tissue. It is FDA-approved across metastatic breast cancer, advanced NSCLC, and metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: paclitaxel is an antimicrotubule agent that promotes assembly and stabilization of microtubules, inhibiting their dynamic reorganization and arresting mitosis; the albumin-bound nanoparticle uses gp60-mediated endothelial transcytosis and tumor SPARC binding to concentrate drug at the tumor [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/021660s047lbl.pdf).
- Approved indications: metastatic breast cancer after failure of combination chemotherapy; first-line locally advanced/metastatic NSCLC in combination with carboplatin (not candidates for curative surgery/radiation); and first-line metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma in combination with gemcitabine [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/021660s047lbl.pdf).
- Pancreatic pivotal trial (MPACT, phase 3): nab-paclitaxel plus gemcitabine vs gemcitabine improved median OS from 6.6 to 8.7 months (HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.62–0.83, P<0.001), with 1-year survival rising from 22% to 35% [MPACT JNCI](https://academic.oup.com/jnci/article/107/2/dju413/902453).
- Breast pivotal trial (CA012, phase 3): nab-paclitaxel 260 mg/m² q3w vs solvent-based paclitaxel 175 mg/m² q3w nearly doubled overall response rate (33% vs 19%, P=0.001) with superior PFS [CA012 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5361712/).
- NSCLC pivotal trial (CA031, phase 3, n=1052): carboplatin plus nab-paclitaxel vs carboplatin plus solvent-based paclitaxel raised overall response rate from 25% to 33% (P=0.005), the primary endpoint, supporting first-line approval [CA031 PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4958019/).
- Serious/notable adverse events: neutropenia (boxed warning — do not give if baseline neutrophils <1500/mm³), peripheral neuropathy (grade ≥3 occurred in ~10.5% vs 2.2% with solvent-based paclitaxel in CA012), anemia, thrombocytopenia, fatigue, alopecia, and sensory neuropathy [CA012 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5361712/).
- Administration advantage: solvent-free formulation removes the Cremophor EL hypersensitivity risk, so corticosteroid/antihistamine premedication is not routinely required [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/021660s047lbl.pdf).
