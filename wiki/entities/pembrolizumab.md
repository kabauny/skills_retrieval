# Pembrolizumab

## Overview

Pembrolizumab (Keytruda) is a humanized IgG4 monoclonal antibody and immune checkpoint inhibitor that targets the programmed death-1 (PD-1) receptor on T cells. By blocking PD-1 from engaging its ligands PD-L1 and PD-L2, it releases tumor-mediated inhibition of the T-cell immune response, restoring anti-tumor immunity. It is one of the most broadly approved oncology agents, with indications spanning non-small cell lung cancer, melanoma, head and neck squamous cell carcinoma, urothelial carcinoma, MSI-H/dMMR tumors, triple-negative breast cancer, and many others. Its activity is accompanied by a distinct profile of immune-related adverse events that can affect any organ system.

## Key facts

- **Mechanism:** Anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody that blocks PD-1/PD-L1 (and PD-L2) ligation on tumor-infiltrating T cells, reversing T-cell exhaustion and restoring effector function in the tumor microenvironment. [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/125514s172lbl.pdf)
- **First-line metastatic non-squamous NSCLC ([KEYNOTE-189](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1801005)):** Pembrolizumab + pemetrexed + platinum significantly improved survival irrespective of PD-L1 expression — median OS 22.0 vs 10.7 mo, **HR 0.56** (95% CI 0.45–0.70). Standard dosing is 200 mg IV q3w or 400 mg IV q6w. [KEYNOTE-189](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1801005)
- **Advanced melanoma ([KEYNOTE-006](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1503093)):** Pembrolizumab vs ipilimumab improved OS (median 32.7 vs 15.9 mo; **HR ~0.70**) with durable long-term benefit (7-year OS 37.8% vs 25.3%). [KEYNOTE-006 10-yr](https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(24)03910-3/fulltext)
- **First-line recurrent/metastatic HNSCC ([KEYNOTE-048](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.21.02508)):** Pembrolizumab (±chemo) vs cetuximab-EXTREME improved OS in PD-L1 CPS≥20 (**HR 0.61**) and CPS≥1 (**HR 0.74**) populations; non-inferior in the total population (HR 0.81). [KEYNOTE-048](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.21.02508)
- **Second-line advanced urothelial carcinoma ([KEYNOTE-045](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1613683)):** In platinum-refractory disease, median OS 10.3 vs 7.4 mo vs chemotherapy, **HR 0.73** (95% CI 0.59–0.91; P=0.002), with fewer treatment-related adverse events. [KEYNOTE-045](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1613683)
- **High-risk early-stage TNBC ([[keynote-522]]):** Neoadjuvant pembrolizumab + chemotherapy followed by adjuvant pembrolizumab improved 5-year OS 86.6% vs 81.7% (**HR 0.66**, P=0.0015). [[tnbc-keynote-522-irae-rechallenge-2026]]
- **Serious/notable adverse events:** Immune-mediated reactions that may be severe or fatal in any organ — pneumonitis (3.4% of patients, including fatal 0.1%), colitis, hepatitis, endocrinopathies (thyroid, adrenal insufficiency, hypophysitis, type 1 diabetes), nephritis, severe dermatologic reactions, and infusion reactions. Grade ≥3 irAEs occurred in 13.0% in KEYNOTE-522. [FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=9333c79b-d487-4538-a9f0-71b91a02b287)
