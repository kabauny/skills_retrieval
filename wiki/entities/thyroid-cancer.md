# Thyroid cancer

## Overview
Thyroid cancer is the most common endocrine malignancy, with roughly 44,000 new U.S. cases annually. The great majority are differentiated thyroid cancers (papillary and follicular/Hürthle cell), which are largely indolent and curable with surgery and radioiodine; rarer medullary and anaplastic subtypes behave more aggressively. Molecular profiling (BRAF, RET, NTRK, RAS) has made specific subsets highly actionable with targeted therapy, especially in the advanced/radioiodine-refractory setting.

## Key facts
- Histology: differentiated thyroid cancer (papillary + follicular + Hürthle cell) makes up 90-95% of cases; medullary thyroid cancer (from calcitonin-secreting C cells) is ~1-2%; anaplastic thyroid cancer is <1% but nearly uniformly lethal [NCCN/StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK459299/).
- Curative vs palliative intent is set by stage/resectability: localized differentiated disease is treated with thyroidectomy ± neck dissection, risk-adapted radioactive iodine ablation, and TSH-suppressive levothyroxine, achieving >90% survival; widely metastatic or anaplastic disease shifts to systemic/palliative intent [ATA/Thyroid.org](https://www.thyroid.org/patient-thyroid-information/ct-for-patients/march-2026/vol-19-issue-2-p-11-12/).
- Biomarker BRAF V600E gates targeted therapy in anaplastic thyroid cancer: dabrafenib + trametinib produced a 61-69% objective response rate in BRAF V600E ATC, with an 80% 12-month overall survival estimate, transforming a historically dismal prognosis [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-dabrafenib-plus-trametinib-anaplastic-thyroid-cancer-braf-v600e-mutation).
- RET alterations gate selpercatinib: in LIBRETTO-001, ORR was 82.5% in RET-mutant medullary thyroid cancer (treatment-naive) and 95.8% in RET fusion-positive thyroid cancer (naive), supporting FDA approval [NEJM 2020](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2005651).
- NTRK gene fusions gate larotrectinib (tumor-agnostic accelerated approval Nov 2018); in NTRK fusion thyroid cancers the agent achieved ~77% ORR with durable responses [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/fda-approves-larotrectinib-solid-tumors-ntrk-gene-fusions-0).
- For radioiodine-refractory differentiated thyroid cancer, multikinase angiogenesis inhibitors are standard: lenvatinib (SELECT trial) extended median PFS to 18.3 vs 3.6 months (ORR 64.8%), and sorafenib (DECISION) extended PFS 10.8 vs 5.8 months [SELECT/NCBI](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK552390/).
- Papillary cancer spreads via lymphatics and is generally indolent; follicular cancer spreads hematogenously with greater predilection for distant metastases, informing the role of radioiodine for distant disease [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK459299/).
