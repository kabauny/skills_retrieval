# Cyclophosphamide

## Overview

Cyclophosphamide is an oxazaphosphorine nitrogen-mustard alkylating agent used as a cytotoxic backbone across many hematologic malignancies and solid tumors. It is a prodrug requiring hepatic activation, and is a core component of long-standing combination regimens such as CHOP/R-CHOP for lymphoma and AC/TC for breast cancer. It is also used at lower doses as an immunosuppressant in nonmalignant conditions.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: cyclophosphamide is a prodrug activated by hepatic cytochrome P450 to phosphoramide mustard, which alkylates and cross-links tumor-cell DNA, interfering with growth of rapidly proliferating malignant cells [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/203856s003lbl.pdf).
- FDA-approved indications include malignant lymphomas (Ann Arbor stage III/IV), Hodgkin disease, lymphocytic/Burkitt lymphoma, multiple leukemias (CLL, AML, ALL, CML), and biopsy-proven minimal-change nephrotic syndrome refractory to corticosteroids in children [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2012/012141s089lbl.pdf).
- As the "C" in CHOP, it anchors first-line DLBCL therapy; the pivotal GELA LNH98-5 trial (Coiffier, NEJM 2002) showed adding rituximab to CHOP reduced risk of death with OS hazard ratio ~0.64 (95% CI 0.45–0.89) and raised complete response 76% vs 63% [LNH98-5](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/nejmoa011795).
- As the "C" in AC and TC adjuvant breast-cancer regimens (cyclophosphamide 600 mg/m²); the US Oncology 9735 phase 3 trial found docetaxel+cyclophosphamide (TC) superior to doxorubicin+cyclophosphamide (AC), with 7-year OS 87% vs 82% (p=0.032) [JCO 9735](https://ascopubs.org/doi/abs/10.1200/JCO.2006.06.5391).
- Common adverse events: myelosuppression (neutropenia, neutropenic fever, thrombocytopenia, anemia), nausea/vomiting, and alopecia [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK553087/).
- Serious/notable toxicities: hemorrhagic cystitis (mitigated with mesna and hydration), severe immunosuppression with risk of fatal infection, gonadal suppression/infertility (amenorrhea, oligospermia), and secondary malignancies [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2019/203856s003lbl.pdf).
