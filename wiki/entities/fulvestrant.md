# Fulvestrant

## Overview
Fulvestrant (Faslodex) is a selective estrogen receptor degrader (SERD) given by intramuscular injection. It binds the estrogen receptor, blocks receptor dimerization, and accelerates receptor degradation, downregulating ER signaling in hormone receptor (HR)-positive breast cancer. It is used as monotherapy and in combination with CDK4/6 inhibitors for advanced HR-positive disease.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: a pure ER antagonist that competitively binds the estrogen receptor and triggers its degradation (downregulation), without the partial agonist activity seen with tamoxifen [FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=141f439e-078c-4a59-acff-ebf7685f55b8).
- Approved as monotherapy for HR-positive advanced breast cancer in postmenopausal women, both endocrine-therapy-naïve and after progression on prior endocrine therapy [FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=141f439e-078c-4a59-acff-ebf7685f55b8).
- Approved in combination with CDK4/6 inhibitors — ribociclib, palbociclib, or abemaciclib — for HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced/metastatic breast cancer [FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=141f439e-078c-4a59-acff-ebf7685f55b8).
- CONFIRM (phase 3): fulvestrant 500 mg vs 250 mg gave median OS 26.4 vs 22.3 months (HR 0.81, 95% CI 0.69–0.96), establishing 500 mg as the standard dose [CONFIRM final OS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24317176/).
- FALCON (phase 3): in endocrine-naïve patients, fulvestrant 500 mg beat anastrozole on median PFS, 16.6 vs 13.8 months (HR 0.797, p=0.0486) [FALCON](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(16)32389-3/abstract).
- Common adverse reactions (≥5%, monotherapy): injection site pain, nausea, bone pain, arthralgia, headache, fatigue, hot flash, and elevated hepatic enzymes [FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=141f439e-078c-4a59-acff-ebf7685f55b8).
- Notable cautions: bleeding risk in patients on anticoagulants, sciatica/neuropathic pain at the dorsogluteal injection site, dose reduction to 250 mg in moderate hepatic impairment, and embryo-fetal toxicity [FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=141f439e-078c-4a59-acff-ebf7685f55b8).
