# ipilimumab

## Overview

Ipilimumab (YERVOY) is a fully human IgG1 monoclonal antibody against cytotoxic T-lymphocyte antigen 4 (CTLA-4), the first immune checkpoint inhibitor approved by the FDA (2011). By blocking CTLA-4-mediated downregulation of T-cell activation, it amplifies antitumor T-cell responses. It is used as monotherapy in melanoma but most often in combination with the PD-1 inhibitor nivolumab across melanoma, renal cell carcinoma, MSI-H/dMMR colorectal cancer, and hepatocellular carcinoma.

## Key facts

- Mechanism: binds CTLA-4 on T cells, blocking its interaction with B7 ligands (CD80/CD86) on antigen-presenting cells, thereby removing inhibitory modulation and enhancing T-cell activation and proliferation [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/125377s135lbl.pdf).
- Principal FDA indications: unresectable/metastatic melanoma (single agent or with nivolumab, ages ≥12), and in combination with nivolumab for advanced renal cell carcinoma, MSI-H/dMMR metastatic colorectal cancer, and hepatocellular carcinoma [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/125377s135lbl.pdf).
- Pivotal melanoma trial CheckMate 067: nivolumab plus ipilimumab vs ipilimumab alone gave median OS 71.9 vs 19.9 months (HR 0.53, 95% CI 0.44-0.65); 10-year OS 43% vs 19% [CheckMate 067 NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1709684).
- Pivotal RCC trial CheckMate 214: nivolumab plus ipilimumab vs sunitinib in intermediate/poor-risk advanced RCC gave OS HR 0.69 (95% CI 0.59-0.81) sustained at long-term follow-up [CheckMate 214](https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(24)01516-3/fulltext).
- Serious immune-mediated colitis occurred in 12% (Grade 3-5 in 7%) of patients on single-agent ipilimumab 3 mg/kg, requiring systemic corticosteroids; may be fatal [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/125377s135lbl.pdf).
- Other notable immune-mediated toxicities include hepatitis (4.1% single-agent), hypophysitis/endocrinopathies (4.4% with nivolumab), pneumonitis, nephritis, and dermatologic reactions, which can occur during or after treatment [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/125377s135lbl.pdf).
- General management: withhold for severe (Grade 3) and permanently discontinue for life-threatening (Grade 4) immune-mediated adverse reactions [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/125377s135lbl.pdf).
