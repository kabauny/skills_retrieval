# Erdafitinib

## Overview
Erdafitinib (Balversa) is an oral pan-fibroblast growth factor receptor (FGFR1–4) tyrosine kinase inhibitor. It is approved for adults with locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma harboring susceptible FGFR3 genetic alterations whose disease has progressed on or after at least one prior line of systemic therapy. It is the first targeted therapy approved for FGFR-altered bladder cancer.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: binds and inhibits the enzymatic activity, phosphorylation, and downstream signaling of FGFR1–4, blocking proliferation and inducing death of FGFR-altered tumor cells [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-erdafitinib-fgfr3-altered-metastatic-urothelial-carcinoma).
- Principal indication: locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma with susceptible FGFR3 alterations after at least one prior line of systemic therapy; not recommended for patients eligible for but not yet given a PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor (full approval January 19, 2024) [DailyMed label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=2a8aa5c0-6c92-4566-8c45-e8f4d1fc20ee).
- Pivotal trial: phase 3 THOR cohort 1 randomized 266 previously treated, PD-1/PD-L1-exposed patients with FGFR3/2 alterations to erdafitinib vs investigator's choice chemotherapy (docetaxel or vinflunine) [THOR NEJM 2023](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2308849).
- Survival benefit: median OS 12.1 vs 7.8 months (HR 0.64), a 36% reduction in risk of death favoring erdafitinib [THOR ASCO Post](https://ascopost.com/news/december-2023/erdafitinib-vs-chemotherapy-in-previously-treated-fgfr-mutated-advanced-urothelial-carcinoma/).
- Secondary efficacy: median PFS 5.6 vs 2.7 months (HR 0.58) and objective response rate 45.6% vs 11.5% versus chemotherapy [THOR ASCO Post](https://ascopost.com/news/december-2023/erdafitinib-vs-chemotherapy-in-previously-treated-fgfr-mutated-advanced-urothelial-carcinoma/).
- Class effect toxicity: hyperphosphatemia in 76% of patients (a pharmacodynamic marker of FGFR inhibition), requiring monthly phosphate monitoring [DailyMed label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=2a8aa5c0-6c92-4566-8c45-e8f4d1fc20ee).
- Serious ocular toxicity: central serous retinopathy / retinal pigment epithelial detachment in 22% (median onset 46 days), mandating monthly ophthalmologic exams for the first 4 months; dry eye in 26% [Balversa HCP safety](https://www.balversahcp.com/important-safety-information/).
- Other notable adverse events leading to dose reduction: nail disorders (21%), stomatitis (15%), palmar-plantar erythrodysesthesia (7%), and fatigue (6%) [Balversa HCP safety](https://www.balversahcp.com/important-safety-information/).
