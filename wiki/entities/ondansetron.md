# Ondansetron


## Brief

An antiemetic medication used to prevent nausea and vomiting, commonly prescribed as premedication for patients undergoing chemotherapy or radiation.

## Why this matters

It is widely used in oncology as supportive care to improve patient tolerance to chemotherapy and maintain quality of life.
