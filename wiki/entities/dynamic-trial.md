# DYNAMIC Trial

## Overview

DYNAMIC was a randomized phase 2 trial (Australia/New Zealand, N=455) testing whether circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA)-guided management could reduce adjuvant chemotherapy use in resected stage II colon cancer without compromising recurrence outcomes. Patients were randomized 2:1 to ctDNA-guided versus standard clinicopathologic management, with postoperative ctDNA assessed at weeks 4 and 7 using a tumor-informed assay. It established proof of concept that a negative MRD result can safely spare patients from chemotherapy while a positive result identifies those who benefit from it.

## Key facts

- Randomized phase 2 trial enrolling 455 patients with stage II colon cancer (2015–2019), 2:1 to ctDNA-guided vs standard management [[DYNAMIC, NEJM 2022](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2200075)].
- In the ctDNA-guided arm, only ctDNA-positive patients received adjuvant chemotherapy; ctDNA-negative patients were observed [[DYNAMIC, NEJM 2022](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2200075)].
- ctDNA-guided management roughly halved chemotherapy use: 15.3% treated vs 27.9% in the standard arm [[ASCO Post](https://ascopost.com/news/june-2022/ctdna-may-help-identify-the-need-for-adjuvant-therapy-in-stage-ii-colon-cancer/)].
- Primary endpoint met: 2-year RFS was non-inferior, 93.5% (ctDNA-guided) vs 92.4% (standard), difference 1.1% (95% CI −4.1 to 6.2) [[DYNAMIC, NEJM 2022](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2200075)].
- ctDNA-positive patients who received adjuvant chemotherapy had a 3-year RFS of 86.4%, supporting benefit of treating MRD-positive disease [[DYNAMIC, NEJM 2022](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2200075)].
- ctDNA-negative patients managed without chemotherapy had 3-year RFS of 92.5% (96.7% in the clinically low-risk subgroup) [[DYNAMIC, NEJM 2022](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2200075)].
- At median 59.7-month follow-up, 5-year RFS (88% vs 87%) and 5-year OS (93.8% vs 93.3%) remained similar between arms, confirming durability [[DYNAMIC 5-year, Nature Medicine 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03579-w)].
