# Imatinib

## Overview
Imatinib (Gleevec) is an oral small-molecule tyrosine kinase inhibitor and the first targeted therapy approved for chronic myeloid leukemia. It inhibits the BCR-ABL fusion kinase that drives Philadelphia chromosome-positive (Ph+) CML, as well as KIT and PDGFR. Its principal uses are Ph+ CML and KIT-positive gastrointestinal stromal tumors (GIST), where it transformed both diseases from often-fatal to chronically manageable conditions.

## Key facts
- Mechanism: competitively inhibits the ATP-binding site of the constitutively active BCR-ABL tyrosine kinase, and also inhibits KIT (c-kit) and PDGFR, blocking downstream proliferative/anti-apoptotic signaling in CML and GIST cells [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2016/021588s047lbl.pdf).
- Principal FDA indications: Ph+ CML (chronic phase, accelerated phase, blast crisis) in adults and children, and KIT (CD117)-positive unresectable/metastatic GIST plus adjuvant treatment of resected KIT-positive GIST [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2016/021588s047lbl.pdf).
- Pivotal CML trial (IRIS): randomized 1,106 newly diagnosed chronic-phase CML patients to imatinib 400 mg/day vs interferon-alfa plus cytarabine; complete cytogenetic response 76% vs 15% at 18 months [IRIS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3266053/).
- IRIS long-term: estimated 10-year overall survival 83.3% on imatinib, with durable responses and no unacceptable late cumulative toxicity [IRIS 10-year NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1609324).
- Pivotal adjuvant GIST trial (ACOSOG Z9001): 1 year of adjuvant imatinib vs placebo after resection of primary GIST improved recurrence-free survival (1-year RFS 98% vs 83%; HR 0.35, 95% CI 0.22-0.53, P<0.0001) [ACOSOG Z9001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24638003/).
- Common/serious adverse events: edema and fluid retention (including pleural effusion, ascites, pulmonary edema), nausea, muscle cramps, rash, and myelosuppression (anemia, neutropenia, thrombocytopenia) more frequent in advanced disease [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2016/021588s047lbl.pdf).
- Hepatotoxicity, occasionally severe, can occur; liver function should be monitored at baseline and monthly, and CBCs checked frequently early in treatment [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2016/021588s047lbl.pdf).
