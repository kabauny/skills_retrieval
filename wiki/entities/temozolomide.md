# Temozolomide


## Brief

An oral alkylating chemotherapy drug primarily used in the treatment of certain brain cancers, such as glioblastoma, typically administered in conjunction with radiation.

## Why this matters

It is a key chemotherapy agent in the standard treatment protocol for glioblastoma.
