# BCG-naïve, high-risk non-muscle invasive bladder cancer


## Brief

A specific subgroup of non-muscle invasive bladder cancer characterized by high risk features and no prior exposure to Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) therapy.

## Why this matters

This specific patient population represents a significant unmet need in bladder cancer, as it previously lacked effective new therapies for over 30 years.
