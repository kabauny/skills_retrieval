# DESTINY-Breast11

## Overview

DESTINY-Breast11 (DB-11) is a randomized, open-label, multicenter Phase 3 trial of neoadjuvant (pre-surgical) therapy for high-risk HER2-positive early-stage breast cancer. It tested trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) followed by paclitaxel/trastuzumab/pertuzumab (THP) against the standard dose-dense doxorubicin + cyclophosphamide followed by THP (ddAC-THP). Reported at ESMO 2025, it is the first Phase 3 evidence that replacing anthracycline-based chemotherapy with an antibody-drug conjugate in the neoadjuvant setting improves pathologic complete response (pCR).

## Key facts

- Design: randomized 1:1:1, open-label, multicenter Phase 3 across ~147 sites in 18 countries; 927 patients with untreated high-risk HER2-positive early breast cancer; the third (T-DXd monotherapy) arm closed enrollment early on Independent Data Monitoring Committee recommendation, leaving T-DXd→THP vs ddAC-THP as the primary comparison [[AstraZeneca DB-11 press release]](https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2025/enhertu-followed-by-thp-before-surgery-resulted-in-a-pcr-in-67-of-patients-in-db11.html).
- Primary endpoint pCR: 67.3% with T-DXd→THP vs 56.3% with ddAC-THP (difference 11.2%, 95% CI 4.0–18.3; P=0.003) [DESTINY-Breast11, Annals of Oncology](https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534(25)04968-3/fulltext).
- Benefit was consistent across hormone-receptor subgroups: HR-positive 61.4% vs 52.3%; HR-negative 83.1% vs 67.1% [DESTINY-Breast11, ESMO 2025](https://oncodaily.com/oncolibrary/destiny-breast11-t-dxd-pcr-ebc).
- An early favorable event-free survival trend favored T-DXd→THP (HR 0.56, 95% CI 0.26–1.17), though immature [DESTINY-Breast11, ESMO 2025](https://oncodaily.com/oncolibrary/destiny-breast11-t-dxd-pcr-ebc).
- Lower toxicity: fewer grade ≥3 adverse events (37.5% vs 55.8%) and markedly lower left ventricular dysfunction (1.9% vs 9.0%) with T-DXd→THP, supporting anthracycline omission [DESTINY-Breast11, ESMO 2025](https://oncodaily.com/oncolibrary/destiny-breast11-t-dxd-pcr-ebc).
- Interstitial lung disease (a known T-DXd risk) was low and similar between arms: 4.4% (T-DXd→THP) vs 5.1% (ddAC-THP), mostly grade 1-2, with one grade 5 event in each arm [AstraZeneca DB-11 press release](https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/press-releases/2025/enhertu-followed-by-thp-before-surgery-resulted-in-a-pcr-in-67-of-patients-in-db11.html).
- Significance: a US supplemental BLA for ENHERTU followed by THP in this neoadjuvant setting has been accepted, positioning T-DXd→THP as a potential new neoadjuvant standard of care [Daiichi Sankyo sBLA acceptance](https://daiichisankyo.us/press-releases/-/article/enhertu%C2%AE-followed-by-thp-supplemental-biologics-license-application-accepted-in-the-us-for-patients-with-high-risk-her2-positive-early-stage-breast-cancer-prior-to-surgery).
