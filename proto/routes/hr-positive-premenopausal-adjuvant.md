## Prompt

Separate the node-negative (TAILORx) from node-positive (RxPONDER) evidence — patients conflate them. For intermediate RS premenopausal patients, distinguish true chemo benefit from a chemo-induced ovarian-suppression effect (CIOS hypothesis) before recommending chemo over OFS+AI. Tie the recurrence score to the matching trial population.
