## Prompt

Distinguish radiographic residual disease from molecular (MRD) positivity — they are not interchangeable. State the subtype context, since MRD-guided action differs across HER2+, HR+, and TNBC. Cite the MRD-enrichment trial when claiming an escalation benefit. Note where MRD-guided escalation is still investigational vs guideline-endorsed.
