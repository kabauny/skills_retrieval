## Prompt

Anchor on pCR status: pCR is itself a strong prognostic marker and reshapes the adjuvant risk/benefit. If a severe irAE occurred, weigh re-challenge toxicity against the (often small) marginal benefit in pCR patients, and flag where this sits in a guideline gap. Grade irAEs before recommending continuation.
