## Prompt

First establish residual-disease status after neoadjuvant therapy and nodal burden — these drive escalation. Weigh ILD risk (favoring T-DM1) against CNS activity and IDFS magnitude (favoring T-DXd). Note guideline vs PDUFA-timeline gaps explicitly. Cite the pivotal adjuvant trial for any efficacy claim.
