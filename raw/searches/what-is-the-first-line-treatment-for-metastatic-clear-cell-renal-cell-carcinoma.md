# What is the first-line treatment for metastatic clear cell renal cell carcinoma?

According to current clinical guidelines (such as those from the NCCN and ASCO), the first-line treatment for metastatic clear cell renal cell carcinoma (ccRCC) prioritizes combination regimens containing immune checkpoint inhibitors [nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40328529/). The specific strategy chosen often depends on a patient's International Metastatic RCC Database Consortium (IMDC) risk classification (favorable vs. intermediate/poor risk) [cloudfront.net](https://d56bochluxqnz.cloudfront.net/documents/full-guideline/EAU-Guidelines-on-Renal-Cell-Carcinoma-2024.pdf) [cabometyxhcp.com](https://www.cabometyxhcp.com/assets/pdfs/Kidney-Guidelines.pdf). 

The preferred standard-of-care (Category 1) first-line regimens include combinations of either two immunotherapies (IO/IO) or an immunotherapy paired with a targeted tyrosine kinase inhibitor (IO/TKI) [sitcancer.org](https://www.sitcancer.org/publications/cpg/kidney) [cabometyxhcp.com](https://www.cabometyxhcp.com/assets/pdfs/Kidney-Guidelines.pdf). These preferred combinations include:
*   **Pembrolizumab plus axitinib** (IO/TKI) [cabometyxhcp.com](https://www.cabometyxhcp.com/assets/pdfs/Kidney-Guidelines.pdf)
*   **Pembrolizumab plus lenvatinib** (IO/TKI) [cabometyxhcp.com](https://www.cabometyxhcp.com/assets/pdfs/Kidney-Guidelines.pdf)
*   **Nivolumab plus cabozantinib** (IO/TKI) [cabometyxhcp.com](https://www.cabometyxhcp.com/assets/pdfs/Kidney-Guidelines.pdf)
*   **Nivolumab plus ipilimumab** (IO/IO) [cabometyxhcp.com](https://www.cabometyxhcp.com/assets/pdfs/Kidney-Guidelines.pdf)

In highly select, favorable-risk patients who have asymptomatic, slow-growing, and low-volume disease, an initial period of active surveillance may be recommended before initiating systemic therapy [nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40328529/) [ascopubs.org](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.22.00868). 

Additionally, precision medicine approaches have emerged for specific genetically driven subtypes. For example, belzutifan, a targeted HIF-2α inhibitor, is FDA-approved for advanced renal cell carcinoma and tumors associated with von Hippel-Lindau (VHL) disease [[belzutifan]]. Belzutifan targets the HIF-2α/pseudohypoxic pathway, offering a specialized targeted treatment avenue [[belzutifan]]; however, the cornerstone first-line treatment for standard sporadic metastatic ccRCC remains the aforementioned ICI-based combination therapies.
