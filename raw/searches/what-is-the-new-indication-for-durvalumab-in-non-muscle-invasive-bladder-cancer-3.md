# What is the new indication for durvalumab in non muscle invasive bladder cancer?

On May 28, 2026, the FDA approved durvalumab (Imfinzi) in combination with Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) induction and maintenance therapy for the treatment of adult patients with BCG-naïve, high-risk non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC).  [fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-durvalumab-combination-bacillus-calmette-guerin-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder) [astrazeneca-us.com](https://www.astrazeneca-us.com/media/press-releases/2026/imfinzi-durvalumab-approved-in-the-us-in-only-immunotherapy-combination-for-patients-with-bcg-naive-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer.html)

This approval marks the first new therapy approved in over 30 years—and the first immunotherapy combination regimen—for patients with BCG-naïve, high-risk NMIBC in the early curative-intent setting. The r [astrazeneca-us.com](https://www.astrazeneca-us.com/media/press-releases/2026/imfinzi-durvalumab-approved-in-the-us-in-only-immunotherapy-combination-for-patients-with-bcg-naive-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer.html) [webmd.com](https://www.webmd.com/drugs/updates/imfinzi-fda-approves-combination-bcg)egulatory decision was based on positive results from the phase 3 POTOMAC trial. The t [astrazeneca-us.com](https://www.astrazeneca-us.com/media/press-releases/2026/imfinzi-durvalumab-approved-in-the-us-in-only-immunotherapy-combination-for-patients-with-bcg-naive-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer.html) [urologytimes.com](https://www.urologytimes.com/view/fda-approves-durvalumab-plus-bcg-for-bcg-naive-high-risk-nmibc)rial demonstrated that adding durvalumab to BCG induction and maintenance therapy significantly improved disease-free survival (DFS) compared to treatment with BCG alone, reducing the risk of disease recurrence, progression, or death by approximately 32%. 

The [astrazeneca-us.com](https://www.astrazeneca-us.com/media/press-releases/2026/imfinzi-durvalumab-approved-in-the-us-in-only-immunotherapy-combination-for-patients-with-bcg-naive-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer.html) [urologytimes.com](https://www.urologytimes.com/view/fda-approves-durvalumab-plus-bcg-for-bcg-naive-high-risk-nmibc) recommended dosage for patients weighing 30 kg or more is 1500 mg administered intravenously every 4 weeks for up to 13 cycles, given alongside BCG induction and maintenance therapy. Patie [urologytimes.com](https://www.urologytimes.com/view/fda-approves-durvalumab-plus-bcg-for-bcg-naive-high-risk-nmibc) [cancernetwork.com](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/durvalumab-plus-bcg-earns-fda-approval-for-high-risk-nmibc)nts are recommended to continue treatment until disease progression, recurrence of high-risk disease, unacceptable toxicity, or completion of the maximum 13 cycles.
