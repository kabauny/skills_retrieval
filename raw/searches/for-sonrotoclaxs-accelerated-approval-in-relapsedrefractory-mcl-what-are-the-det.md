# For Sonrotoclax's accelerated approval in relapsed/refractory MCL, what are the details regarding the planned confirmatory trials, and are there specific considerations for managing tumor lysis syndrome given its BCL-2 inhibitor mechanism?

Based on recent information, sonrotoclax (Beqalzi) received FDA accelerated approval in May 2026 for adult patients with relapsed or refractory mantle cell lymphoma (R/R MCL) who have received at least two prior lines of systemic therapy, including a Bruton's tyrosine kinase (BTK) inhibitor [search-sourced]. 

**Confirmatory Trial Details**
Because the drug was granted accelerated approval based on overall response rate (ORR) and duration of response (DOR) in early-phase studies, continued approval is contingent upon the verification of clinical benefit in a confirmatory trial [search-sourced]. 
* **Trial Identification:** The designated phase 3 confirmatory trial is **CELESTIAL-RRMCL** (NCT06742996) [search-sourced].
* **Design and Arms:** It is a randomized, double-blind, multicenter study comparing sonrotoclax in combination with zanubrutinib (a BTK inhibitor) versus placebo in combination with zanubrutinib [search-sourced].
* **Endpoints:** The primary endpoint is progression-free survival (PFS) as assessed by an independent review committee, with overall survival (OS) among the key secondary endpoints [search-sourced].

**Tumor Lysis Syndrome (TLS) Considerations**
As a highly potent next-generation BCL-2 inhibitor, sonrotoclax removes the "survival shield" of cancer cells, inducing rapid apoptosis (programmed cell death) [search-sourced]. Because cancer cells die quickly, their intracellular contents are released into the bloodstream, posing a severe risk for tumor lysis syndrome (TLS) [search-sourced]. Management considerations include:
* **Mandatory Dose Ramp-Up:** To mitigate the TLS risk, the FDA-approved dosing regimen requires a strict 4-week gradual dose ramp-up phase [search-sourced]. This slow dose escalation prevents a sudden, overwhelming tumor die-off before the patient reaches the target maintenance dose of 320 mg orally once daily [search-sourced].
* **Prophylaxis:** Patient management typically requires administration of mandatory hydration and antihyperuricemic prophylaxis prior to initiating the BCL-2 inhibitor [search-sourced].
* **Tumor Debulking:** In some clinical protocols and ongoing trials (such as the SONIC trial), patients undergo initial "debulking" therapy with an agent like zanubrutinib or rituximab before starting sonrotoclax [search-sourced]. This lowers the baseline tumor burden, thereby making the sonrotoclax ramp-up safer and less likely to trigger TLS [search-sourced].
