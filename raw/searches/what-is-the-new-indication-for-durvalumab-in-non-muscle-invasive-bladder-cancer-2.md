# What is the new indication for durvalumab in non muscle invasive bladder cancer?

Based on recent FDA announcements, the new indication for durvalumab (Imfinzi) in non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC) is as follows:

On May 28, 2026, the FDA approved durvalumab in combination with Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) induction and maintenance therapy for the treatment of adult patients with BCG-naïve, high-risk non-muscle invasive bladder cancer [ [fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-durvalumab-combination-bacillus-calmette-guerin-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder) [ajmc.com](https://www.ajmc.com/view/fda-approves-durvalumab-plus-bcg-as-first-immunotherapy-combo-regimen-for-high-risk-nmibc)search-sourced]. This marks the first new therapy approved for this patient population in over 30 years and the first immunotherapy combination regimen for high-risk NMIBC [ [astrazeneca-us.com](https://www.astrazeneca-us.com/media/press-releases/2026/imfinzi-durvalumab-approved-in-the-us-in-only-immunotherapy-combination-for-patients-with-bcg-naive-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer.html) [ajmc.com](https://www.ajmc.com/view/fda-approves-durvalumab-plus-bcg-as-first-immunotherapy-combo-regimen-for-high-risk-nmibc)search-sourced].

**Key Details of the Indication & Trial:**
*   **Patient Population:** Adults with high-risk NMIBC who have not previously received BCG therapy (BCG-naïve) [s [fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-durvalumab-combination-bacillus-calmette-guerin-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder) [webmd.com](https://www.webmd.com/drugs/updates/imfinzi-fda-approves-combination-bcg)earch-sourced]. High-risk features include having a T1 tumor, high-grade/grade 3 tumor, carcinoma in situ (CIS), or large/recurrent/multiple tumors [s [urologytimes.com](https://www.urologytimes.com/view/fda-approves-durvalumab-plus-bcg-for-bcg-naive-high-risk-nmibc) [oncnursingnews.com](https://www.oncnursingnews.com/view/fda-approves-durvalumab-and-bcg-for-high-risk-nmibc)earch-sourced].
*   **Clinical Efficacy (POTOMAC Trial):** The approval was based on the Phase III POTOMAC trial, which showed that adding durvalumab to BCG induction and maintenance significantly improved disease-free survival (DFS) [s [astrazeneca-us.com](https://www.astrazeneca-us.com/media/press-releases/2026/imfinzi-durvalumab-approved-in-the-us-in-only-immunotherapy-combination-for-patients-with-bcg-naive-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer.html) [urologytimes.com](https://www.urologytimes.com/view/fda-approves-durvalumab-plus-bcg-for-bcg-naive-high-risk-nmibc)earch-sourced]. The combination reduced the risk of high-risk disease recurrence, progression, or death by 32% compared to BCG alone (Hazard Ratio = 0.68) [s [astrazeneca-us.com](https://www.astrazeneca-us.com/media/press-releases/2026/imfinzi-durvalumab-approved-in-the-us-in-only-immunotherapy-combination-for-patients-with-bcg-naive-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer.html) [oncnursingnews.com](https://www.oncnursingnews.com/view/fda-approves-durvalumab-and-bcg-for-high-risk-nmibc)earch-sourced]. 
*   **Dosing:** For patients weighing 30 kg or more, the recommended dose of durvalumab is 1500 mg administered intravenously every 4 weeks for a maximum of 13 cycles, given alongside BCG induction and maintenance treatment [s [urologytimes.com](https://www.urologytimes.com/view/fda-approves-durvalumab-plus-bcg-for-bcg-naive-high-risk-nmibc) [cancernetwork.com](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/durvalumab-plus-bcg-earns-fda-approval-for-high-risk-nmibc)earch-sourced].
