# Please provide any FDA indication for cancer treatment in May and June 2026

While the internal wiki focuses heavily on clinical decision-making strategies, trials (like TAILORx, RxPONDER, and OFSET), and biomarkers (like AMH and ctDNA) for premenopausal HR+/HER2- early breast cancer [[premenopausal-intermediate-oncotype-2026]], it does not contain real-time FDA approval tracking. 

Based on web search, the FDA was highly active in the oncology space in May and June 2026, issuing multiple landmark approvals. Here are the FDA indications for cancer treatment granted during this time [search-sourced]:

**May 2026 Approvals:**
*   **Vepdegestrant (Veppanu):** Approved on May 1 for adults with estrogen receptor (ER)-positive, HER2-negative, ESR1-mutated advanced or metastatic breast cancer whose disease has progressed following at least one prior line of endocrine therapy. This marks the first-ever approval of a PROTAC (proteolysis-targeting chimera) in any indication [search-sourced].
*   **Zenocutuzumab-zbco (Bizengri):** Approved on May 8 for adults with advanced, unresectable, or metastatic cholangiocarcinoma harboring a neuregulin 1 (NRG1) gene fusion [search-sourced].
*   **Sonrotoclax (Beqalzi):** Granted accelerated approval on May 13 for adults with relapsed or refractory mantle cell lymphoma (MCL) after at least two lines of systemic therapy, including a BTK inhibitor [search-sourced].
*   **Decitabine and cedazuridine (Inqovi) + Venetoclax:** Approved on May 13 as a combination therapy for newly diagnosed acute myeloid leukemia (AML) in adults 75 years or older, or those with comorbidities precluding intensive chemotherapy [search-sourced].
*   **Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki (Enhertu):** Approved on May 15 for two separate indications in adult patients with HER2-positive early-stage breast cancer (both neoadjuvant and adjuvant settings) [search-sourced].
*   **Atezolizumab (Tecentriq):** Approved on May 15 for the adjuvant treatment of adults with muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC) who have circulating tumor DNA molecular residual disease (ctDNA MRD) post-cystectomy [search-sourced].
*   **Datopotamab deruxtecan-dlnk (Datroway):** Approved on May 22 for adult patients with unresectable or metastatic triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) who are not candidates for PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor therapy [search-sourced].
*   **Pivekimab sunirine-pvzy (Decnupaz):** Approved on May 27 to treat adults with blastic plasmacytoid dendritic cell neoplasm (BPDCN) [search-sourced].
*   **Durvalumab (Imfinzi) + BCG:** Approved on May 28 for adults with high-risk non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC) who have not previously received Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) [search-sourced].

**June 2026 Approvals (Through early June):**
*   **Eribulin mesylate (Generic):** In early June, the FDA approved Lupin and Natco's generic eribulin mesylate for pretreated metastatic breast cancer and unresectable or metastatic liposarcoma [search-sourced]. 

*(Note: During this period, the FDA also granted expanded access for the investigational drug daraxonrasib in previously treated pancreatic cancer and granted priority review/breakthrough therapy designations to upcoming oncology agents like giredestrant and calderasib [search-sourced].)*
