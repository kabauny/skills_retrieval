# What are some options for second line treatment for EGFR mutated NSCLC after progression on osimertinib

For patients with EGFR-mutated non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who experience disease progression on frontline osimertinib, treatment paradigms have evolved rapidly with several newly approved regimens. Upon progression, it is highly recommended to perform a tissue re-biopsy or liquid biopsy to identify specific molecular resistance mechanisms (such as MET amplification or C797S mutations) that can help tailor subsequent treatments [search-sourced] [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ND_Cy-uEa8) [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/choosing-treatment-after-osimertinib-patients-egfr--mutated-nsclc-met-amplification). 

Current standard and emerging second-line options include:

**1. Amivantamab plus Chemotherapy**
In September 2024, the FDA approved amivantamab (a bispecific antibody targeting EGFR and MET) in combination with carboplatin and pemetrexed for patients with advanced EGFR-mutated NSCLC whose disease has progressed on or after an EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI) [search-sourced] [fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-amivantamab-vmjw-carboplatin-and-pemetrexed-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-egfr-exon-19). This approval was based on the Phase 3 MARIPOSA-2 trial, which demonstrated that this combination yielded a significant improvement in progression-free survival (PFS) and objective response rates over chemotherapy alone [search-sourced] [fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-amivantamab-vmjw-carboplatin-and-pemetrexed-non-small-cell-lung-cancer-egfr-exon-19). Additionally, a subcutaneous formulation of amivantamab was FDA-approved in December 2025, which significantly reduces the administration time and the risk of infusion-related reactions compared to the intravenous form [search-sourced] [fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-amivantamab-and-hyaluronidase-lpuj-subcutaneous-injection) [cancernetwork.com](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/fda-oks-once-monthly-subcutaneous-amivantamab-dose-in-egfr-nsclc).

**2. Continuing Osimertinib plus Chemotherapy**
Recent data from the Phase 3 COMPEL trial, presented in late 2025, supports a strategy of continuing osimertinib therapy alongside platinum-pemetrexed chemotherapy for patients who experience non-central nervous system (CNS) progression [search-sourced] [ecancer.org](https://ecancer.org/en/news/26992-wclc-2025-continuing-osimertinib-treatment-through-progression-with-the-addition-of-chemo-improves-progression-free-survival-in-egfr-mutated-nsclc). This approach demonstrated a significant improvement in both median PFS (8.4 months vs. 4.4 months) and median overall survival (OS) compared to switching to chemotherapy alone, indicating that some tumor clones remain sensitive to osimertinib [search-sourced] [onclive.com](https://www.onclive.com/view/updated-os-data-reshape-first--and-second-line-treatment-selection-in-egfr-mutated-advanced-nsclc) [ecancer.org](https://ecancer.org/en/news/26992-wclc-2025-continuing-osimertinib-treatment-through-progression-with-the-addition-of-chemo-improves-progression-free-survival-in-egfr-mutated-nsclc). 

**3. Platinum-Based Doublet Chemotherapy**
Historically, platinum doublet chemotherapy (such as carboplatin or cisplatin combined with pemetrexed) has been the standard of care for the second-line setting after third-generation TKI failure [search-sourced] [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/choosing-treatment-after-osimertinib-patients-egfr--mutated-nsclc-met-amplification). It remains a widely utilized foundational treatment, whether used as a monotherapy approach or as a backbone for combination regimens [search-sourced] [medscape.com](https://decisionpoint.medscape.com/oncology/viewarticle/910568) [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/choosing-treatment-after-osimertinib-patients-egfr--mutated-nsclc-met-amplification).

**4. Datopotamab deruxtecan (Dato-DXd)**
Following accelerated FDA approval in mid-2025 for its use in advanced EGFR-mutated NSCLC previously treated with an EGFR-directed therapy and platinum-based chemotherapy, this TROP2-directed antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) was added to the late 2025 NCCN guidelines [search-sourced] [onclive.com](https://www.onclive.com/view/nccn-releases-nsclc-guideline-update-dato-dxd-designated-as-a-preferred-second-line-regimen-in-egfr-mutated-disease). It is now considered a preferred second-line regimen for patients who received upfront osimertinib plus chemotherapy, or as a subsequent line of therapy following other standard regimens [search-sourced] [onclive.com](https://www.onclive.com/view/nccn-releases-nsclc-guideline-update-dato-dxd-designated-as-a-preferred-second-line-regimen-in-egfr-mutated-disease) [healio.com](https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20251114/updated-nccn-guidelines-on-nsclc-put-spotlight-on-biomarker-testing).

**5. Individually Tailored Targeted Therapy**
Identifying specific resistance mechanisms can qualify patients for targeted approaches or clinical trials:
*   **MET Amplification:** This is the most common alternative pathway for resistance, occurring in approximately 15% of patients progressing on osimertinib [search-sourced] [medscape.com](https://decisionpoint.medscape.com/oncology/viewarticle/910568) [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/choosing-treatment-after-osimertinib-patients-egfr--mutated-nsclc-met-amplification). Dual anti-EGFR/anti-MET inhibition with amivantamab-based regimens is often utilized here [search-sourced] [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/choosing-treatment-after-osimertinib-patients-egfr--mutated-nsclc-met-amplification).
*   **Other Targets:** Patients exhibiting specific on-target mutations (like EGFR C797S) or off-target histologic transformations may be candidates for specialized small molecule TKIs or specific clinical trials [search-sourced] [medscape.com](https://decisionpoint.medscape.com/oncology/viewarticle/910568) [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ND_Cy-uEa8).
