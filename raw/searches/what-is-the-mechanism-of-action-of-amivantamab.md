# What is the mechanism of action of amivantamab?

Amivantamab is a fully human, low-fucose IgG1 bispecific monoclonal antibody [search-sourced] [jnjmedicalconnect.com](https://www.jnjmedicalconnect.com/products/rybrevant/medical-content/rybrevant-mechanism-of-action). It is engineered to target and bind to the extracellular domains of two key receptors on tumor cells: the Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor (EGFR) and the Mesenchymal-epithelial Transition Factor (MET) [search-sourced] [oncodaily.com](https://oncodaily.com/drugs/amivantamab-2). 

It exerts its anti-tumor effects through three distinct mechanisms of action [search-sourced] [nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36481319/):

1. **Ligand Blocking:** By binding directly to the extracellular domains of both EGFR and MET, amivantamab physically prevents natural ligands from attaching to these receptors [search-sourced] [oncodaily.com](https://oncodaily.com/drugs/amivantamab-2) [cancernetwork.com](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/journal-expert-commentary-on-the-product-profile-of-amivantamab-vmjw). This disrupts the intracellular signaling pathways that promote tumor cell survival and proliferation [search-sourced] [cancernetwork.com](https://www.cancernetwork.com/view/journal-expert-commentary-on-the-product-profile-of-amivantamab-vmjw).
2. **Receptor Degradation:** The binding of the antibody induces the internalization, down-regulation, and degradation of the targeted EGFR and MET receptors [search-sourced] [nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36481319/).
3. **Immune Cell-Directing Activity:** Amivantamab is specifically engineered to have low levels of core fucose [search-sourced] [jnjmedicalconnect.com](https://www.jnjmedicalconnect.com/products/rybrevant/medical-content/rybrevant-mechanism-of-action). This characteristic allows its Fc region to bind much more tightly to FcγRIIIa receptors on the surface of immune effector cells, such as Natural Killer (NK) cells [search-sourced]. [jnjmedicalconnect.com](https://www.jnjmedicalconnect.com/products/rybrevant/medical-content/rybrevant-mechanism-of-action) This directs the patient's immune system to destroy the targeted tumor cells through immune responses like antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) and trogocytosis [search-sourced]. [nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36481319/) [jnjmedicalconnect.com](https://www.jnjmedicalconnect.com/products/rybrevant/medical-content/rybrevant-mechanism-of-action) 

Notably, amivantamab's efficacy does not require all three mechanisms to occur simultaneously, which broadens its applicability against tumors [search-sourced]. [nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36481319/)
