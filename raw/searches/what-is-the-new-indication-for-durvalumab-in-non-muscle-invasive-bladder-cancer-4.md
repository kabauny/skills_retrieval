# What is the new indication for durvalumab in non muscle invasive bladder cancer?

On May 28, 2026, the FDA approved durvalumab (Imfinzi) in combination with Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) for the treatment of adult patients with BCG-naïve, high-risk non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC).  [fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-durvalumab-combination-bacillus-calmette-guerin-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder) [astrazeneca-us.com](https://www.astrazeneca-us.com/media/press-releases/2026/imfinzi-durvalumab-approved-in-the-us-in-only-immunotherapy-combination-for-patients-with-bcg-naive-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer.html)

This approval marks the first immunotherapy combination regimen approved for this specific setting, establishing a new therapeutic option for high-risk patients in an early curative-intent stage.  [astrazeneca-us.com](https://www.astrazeneca-us.com/media/press-releases/2026/imfinzi-durvalumab-approved-in-the-us-in-only-immunotherapy-combination-for-patients-with-bcg-naive-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer.html) [webmd.com](https://www.webmd.com/drugs/updates/imfinzi-fda-approves-combination-bcg)The FDA's decision was based on positive results from the Phase III POTOMAC trial.  [astrazeneca-us.com](https://www.astrazeneca-us.com/media/press-releases/2026/imfinzi-durvalumab-approved-in-the-us-in-only-immunotherapy-combination-for-patients-with-bcg-naive-high-risk-non-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer.html) [urologytimes.com](https://www.urologytimes.com/view/fda-approves-durvalumab-plus-bcg-for-bcg-naive-high-risk-nmibc)In the study, the addition of durvalumab to standard BCG induction and maintenance therapy significantly improved disease-free survival compared to BCG alone, effectively reducing the risk of disease recurrence, progression, or death by roughly 32%.
