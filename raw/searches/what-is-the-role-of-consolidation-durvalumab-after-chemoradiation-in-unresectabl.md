# What is the role of consolidation durvalumab after chemoradiation in unresectable stage III NSCLC (PACIFIC)?

Durvalumab (also known as Imfinzi) is an immunotherapy drug and PD-L1 inhibitor [[durvalumab]]. In the landmark Phase III PACIFIC trial, it was evaluated as a consolidation therapy for patients with unresectable stage III non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) whose disease had not progressed following platinum-based concurrent chemoradiotherapy (CRT) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12696894/). 

The PACIFIC trial established consolidation durvalumab, administered for up to 1 year, as the uncontested global standard of care for this patient population [ilcn.org](https://www.ilcn.org/ea5181-confirms-durvalumab-after-crt-is-soc-for-unresectable-stage-iii-nsclc/) [nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38833971/). The trial demonstrated robust, durable, and significant improvements in both co-primary endpoints compared to placebo:
* **Overall Survival (OS):** Five-year survival outcomes showed an estimated OS rate of 42.9% for patients receiving durvalumab versus 33.4% for those receiving placebo [ascopubs.org](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.21.01308). 
* **Progression-Free Survival (PFS):** The 5-year PFS rate was 33.1% for the durvalumab arm compared to 19.0% for the placebo arm [ascopubs.org](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.21.01308).

Subsequent studies, such as the EA5181 and PACIFIC-2 trials, evaluated whether initiating durvalumab concurrently with CRT would provide additional survival benefits over the sequential approach [ilcn.org](https://www.ilcn.org/ea5181-confirms-durvalumab-after-crt-is-soc-for-unresectable-stage-iii-nsclc/) [medscape.com](https://decisionpoint.medscape.com/oncology/viewarticle/927048). These trials showed no superior progression-free or overall survival benefit from overlapping the treatments, reaffirming that giving durvalumab solely as a consolidation therapy after completing CRT remains the definitive standard of care [ilcn.org](https://www.ilcn.org/ea5181-confirms-durvalumab-after-crt-is-soc-for-unresectable-stage-iii-nsclc/) [medscape.com](https://decisionpoint.medscape.com/oncology/viewarticle/927048).
