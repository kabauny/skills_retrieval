# What is the first-line treatment for metastatic MSI-high colorectal cancer?

The first-line standard of care for metastatic microsatellite instability-high (MSI-H) or mismatch repair-deficient (dMMR) colorectal cancer is immunotherapy [search-sourced] [esmo.org](https://www.esmo.org/guidelines/living-guidelines/esmo-living-guideline-metastatic-colorectal-cancer/advanced-and-metastatic-disease-without-potential-conversion/ras-or-braf-mutated-and-or-dmmr-msi-h). Because these specific tumors have a high probability of neoantigen formation, they respond very well to immune checkpoint inhibitors, which have largely replaced traditional chemotherapy as the optimal upfront treatment [search-sourced] [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8416693/). 

The primary first-line treatment options include:

*   **Pembrolizumab (Keytruda):** This anti-PD-1 monotherapy is widely considered a standard-of-care first-line treatment and was FDA-approved for this indication in 2020 [search-sourced] [ascopost.com](https://ascopost.com/news/january-2026/triplet-therapy-demonstrates-superior-progression-free-survival-in-first-line-dmmr-msi-h-metastatic-colorectal-cancer/) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8416693/). Its approval was based on the Phase III KEYNOTE-177 trial, which demonstrated that pembrolizumab significantly improves progression-free survival compared to standard chemotherapy regimens [search-sourced] [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/selecting-treatment-patients-stage-iv-msi-h-colorectal-cancer-should-data-trends-equate) [vjoncology.com](https://www.vjoncology.com/video/qt2v87x7mxy-keynote-177-and-checkmate-8hw-promising-first-line-immunotherapy-for-msi-h-mcrc/).
*   **Nivolumab (Opdivo) plus Ipilimumab (Yervoy):** The combination of these two immunotherapy drugs is another highly effective, FDA-approved first-line option [search-sourced] [facingourrisk.org](https://www.facingourrisk.org/XRAY/treating-unresectable-and-metastatic-CRC) [vjoncology.com](https://www.vjoncology.com/video/qt2v87x7mxy-keynote-177-and-checkmate-8hw-promising-first-line-immunotherapy-for-msi-h-mcrc/). Data from clinical studies like CheckMate 142 and the Phase III CheckMate 8HW trial have underscored that this dual-immunotherapy approach is highly promising, slowing cancer growth and proving superior to chemotherapy for newly diagnosed MSI-H/dMMR metastatic disease [search-sourced] [ascopubs.org](https://dailynews.ascopubs.org/do/selecting-treatment-patients-stage-iv-msi-h-colorectal-cancer-should-data-trends-equate) [vjoncology.com](https://www.vjoncology.com/video/qt2v87x7mxy-keynote-177-and-checkmate-8hw-promising-first-line-immunotherapy-for-msi-h-mcrc/).

**Emerging Strategies:**
While standalone immunotherapy is the current standard, recent data from the Phase III COMMIT trial has shown that a "triple-punch" combination of the immunotherapy drug atezolizumab alongside standard chemotherapy (FOLFOX) and targeted therapy (bevacizumab) yields superior progression-free survival compared to atezolizumab alone, marking it as a highly promising potential new first-line treatment option [search-sourced] [asco.org](https://www.asco.org/about-asco/press-center/news-releases/triple-punch-combo-therapy-slows-progression-some-metastatic-colorectal-cancers) [ascopost.com](https://ascopost.com/news/january-2026/triplet-therapy-demonstrates-superior-progression-free-survival-in-first-line-dmmr-msi-h-metastatic-colorectal-cancer/).
