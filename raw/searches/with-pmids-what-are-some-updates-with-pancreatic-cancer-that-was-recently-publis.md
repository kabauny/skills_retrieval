# With PMID's what are some updates with pancreatic cancer that was recently published at ASCO?

Based on recent American Society of Clinical Oncology (ASCO) meetings and related publications, here are some of the key updates and clinical guidelines concerning pancreatic cancer, including their associated PubMed IDs (PMIDs):

### Recent Clinical Trial Highlights from ASCO (2024–2026)
*   **Targeting KRAS Alterations (PMID: 41701177):** Following presentations at the ASCO Gastrointestinal Cancers Symposium, a recent 2026 publication evaluated MEK inhibitor-based genomically matched combinatorial targeted therapies. The study assessed the efficacy of these targeted combinations in patients with metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma harboring specific *KRAS* alterations (G12R, G12D, and G12V) [search-sourced].
*   **RAS(ON) Inhibitors:** At the ASCO 2026 Annual Meeting, the Phase 3 RASolute 302 trial received high profile attention (and was simultaneously published in the *New England Journal of Medicine*) for the drug daraxonrasib. This multi-selective RAS(ON) inhibitor was shown to nearly double the survival time in patients with pre-treated metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma compared to standard chemotherapy [search-sourced].
*   **Tumor Treating Fields (TTFields):** Highlighted recently around ASCO, the Phase 3 PANOVA-3 study demonstrated a statistically significant overall survival benefit when TTFields (electric fields that disrupt cancer cell division) were added to first-line gemcitabine and nab-paclitaxel for patients with locally advanced pancreatic cancer [search-sourced].
*   **Immunotherapy and Lymphopenia:** At ASCO 2025, data from the QUILT-88 trial showed that using ANKTIVA (an IL-15 superagonist) alongside CAR-NK therapy provided a landmark overall survival benefit for 3rd-to-6th line metastatic pancreatic cancer patients. The therapy successfully reversed treatment-induced lymphopenia, correlating improved lymphocyte counts directly with improved survival [search-sourced].

### Foundational ASCO Guidelines & Updates with PMIDs
*   **Metastatic Pancreatic Cancer (PMID: 32755482):** ASCO updated its guidelines for metastatic pancreatic cancer to integrate routine germline and somatic testing. The update strongly recommends testing for microsatellite instability (MSI-H/dMMR), *BRCA* mutations, and *NTRK* gene fusions to identify candidates for targeted therapies such as pembrolizumab, olaparib, and TRK inhibitors [search-sourced].
*   **Adjuvant Therapy for Curable Disease (PMID: 31180816):** Prompted by the practice-changing PRODIGE 24/CCTG PA.6 trial presented at ASCO, the society updated its guidelines for potentially curable pancreatic adenocarcinoma. ASCO now recommends 6 months of modified FOLFIRINOX (mFOLFIRINOX) as the preferred adjuvant chemotherapy for resected patients who do not have medical contraindications [search-sourced]. 
*   **Genetic Susceptibility Evaluation (PMID: 30457921):** An ASCO Provisional Clinical Opinion established the standard that *all* patients diagnosed with pancreatic adenocarcinoma should undergo comprehensive risk assessment and testing for hereditary cancer syndromes, shifting the paradigm towards universal genetic screening for this disease [search-sourced].
*   **Locally Advanced, Unresectable Disease (PMID: 27247216):** ASCO's guidelines on locally advanced, unresectable pancreatic cancer establish evidence-based standards on the initial assessment, the use of multiphase CT imaging, and the integration of systemic chemotherapy and chemoradiotherapy [search-sourced].
