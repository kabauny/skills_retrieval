# What were the specific median overall survival values, hazard ratios, progression-free survival, and overall response rates observed in the daraxonrasib and standard-of-care arms?

Based on the findings from the RASolute 302 trial, the specific efficacy values for the daraxonrasib and standard-of-care chemotherapy arms are as follows:

*   **Median Overall Survival (OS):** Median OS reached 13.2 months for patients treated with daraxonrasib, effectively doubling the 6.6–6.7 months observed in patients receiving standard-of-care chemotherapy [[tell-me-about-the-publication-associated-with-the-rasolute-302-trial]].
*   **Hazard Ratios (HR):** The hazard ratio for overall survival was 0.40, representing a 60% reduction in the risk of death [[tell-me-about-the-publication-associated-with-the-rasolute-302-trial]]. For progression-free survival, the hazard ratio was 0.45 in the RAS G12 mutation group and 0.49 in the overall trial population [search-sourced].
 [onclive.com](https://www.onclive.com/view/daraxonrasib-meets-all-primary-key-secondary-efficacy-end-points-in-pretreated-pdac)*   **Progression-Free Survival (PFS):** In the targeted patient population, median PFS more than doubled, reaching 7.3 months for the daraxonrasib arm compared to 3.5 months for the standard chemotherapy arm [[tell-me-about-the-publication-associated-with-the-rasolute-302-trial]]. In the overall study population, median PFS was 7.2 months with daraxonrasib versus 3.6 months with chemotherapy [search-sourced].
 [onclive.com](https://www.onclive.com/view/daraxonrasib-meets-all-primary-key-secondary-efficacy-end-points-in-pretreated-pdac)*   **Overall Response Rates (ORR):** For patients with a RAS G12 variant, the objective response rate was 33.2% with daraxonrasib versus 11.8% with chemotherapy [search-sourced].  [asco.org](https://www.asco.org/about-asco/press-center/multi-selective-ras-inhibitor-nearly-doubles-survival-pancreatic-cancer)For the overall trial population, the ORR was 31.6% in the daraxonrasib arm compared to 11.2% in the chemotherapy arm [search-sourced].
