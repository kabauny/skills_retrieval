# What is the role of adjuvant osimertinib in resected EGFR-mutant NSCLC (ADAURA trial)?

Based on both clinical context and recent trial data, the role of adjuvant osimertinib in resected EGFR-mutant non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) is highly significant. 

While osimertinib—a third-generation EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI) with strong central nervous system (CNS) penetration—is firmly established as a preferred first-line treatment for *metastatic* EGFR-mutated NSCLC [[what-is-the-first-line-treatment-for-egfr-exon-19-deletion-metastatic-nsclc]], the phase 3 **ADAURA trial** established its critical role in the **early-stage (adjuvant)** setting [search-sourced]. 

The ADAURA trial evaluated adjuvant osimertinib administered for up to 3 years in patients who had undergone complete tumor resection for stage IB to IIIA NSCLC with specific EGFR mutations (exon 19 deletions or L858R) [search-sourced]. 

Key findings defining its role include:
*   **Disease-Free Survival (DFS):** Osimertinib demonstrated an unprecedented, clinically significant improvement in DFS compared to placebo, drastically reducing the risk of disease recurrence [search-sourced]. 
*   **Overall Survival (OS):** Updated results proved a highly statistically significant and clinically meaningful overall survival benefit [search-sourced]. In the overall population (stage IB–IIIA), the 5-year OS rate was 88% with osimertinib compared to 78% with placebo [search-sourced].
*   **CNS Benefit:** Consistent with its known strong CNS penetration [[what-is-the-first-line-treatment-for-egfr-exon-19-deletion-metastatic-nsclc]], osimertinib provided a substantial improvement in CNS disease-free survival, reducing the risk of brain metastases and recurrence [search-sourced].

**Conclusion:**
Adjuvant osimertinib is now established as the standard of care targeted treatment option for patients with resected, EGFR-mutated stage IB–IIIA NSCLC, offering significant prolonged survival and reduced recurrence risk regardless of whether patients received prior adjuvant chemotherapy [search-sourced].
