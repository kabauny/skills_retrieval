# What is the biomarker testing to guide therapy for metastatic urothelial carcinoma?

For patients with metastatic urothelial carcinoma (mUC), biomarker testing is essential to guide the selection of precision therapies and immunotherapies. Clinical guidelines, such as those from the National Comprehensive Cancer Network (NCCN), recommend molecular profiling and biomarker testing at the time of diagnosis of advanced or metastatic disease [jnjprecisionmedicine.com](https://www.jnjprecisionmedicine.com/pdfs/Bladder-Cancer-Sample-Requirements-Testing-Methods-and-Opportunities-Digital-Chapter.pdf) [ascopubs.org](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2025.43.16_suppl.4570).

The primary biomarker tests used to guide therapy in mUC include:

*   **FGFR2 and FGFR3 Genetic Alterations**: Testing for mutations and fusions in the Fibroblast Growth Factor Receptor (FGFR) genes—particularly FGFR3—is a well-established guideline recommendation. Id [nih.gov](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK616221/) [tandfonline.com](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14737140.2025.2543454)entifying these susceptible alterations determines a patient's eligibility for the targeted pan-FGFR inhibitor erdafitinib, which is FDA-approved for patients whose cancer has progressed on or after prior systemic therapy.
*  [nih.gov](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK616221/) [frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/oncology/articles/10.3389/fonc.2025.1670574/full)  **PD-L1 Expression**: Assessing Programmed Death-Ligand 1 (PD-L1) expression on tumor or immune cells has historically been used to guide the use of immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) such as pembrolizumab, atezolizumab, and avelumab. Wh [bmj.com](https://jitc.bmj.com/content/5/1/94) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11399036/)ile the current preferred first-line therapy (the combination of enfortumab vedotin and pembrolizumab) is approved regardless of PD-L1 status, PD-L1 testing still plays a role when considering ICI monotherapy for certain platinum-ineligible patients.
*  [keytrudahcp.com](https://www.keytrudahcp.com/efficacy/urothelial-cancer-first-line-combination-therapy/) [bcbsms.com](https://www.bcbsms.com/policy-search/medical/policy-detail/somatic-biomarker-testing-for-immune-checkpoint-inhibitor-therapy-braf-msimmr-pdl1-tmb)  **MSI-H / dMMR and TMB-H**: Testing for Microsatellite Instability-High (MSI-H), deficient Mismatch Repair (dMMR), or High Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB-H) is recommended. Th [bcbsms.com](https://www.bcbsms.com/policy-search/medical/policy-detail/somatic-biomarker-testing-for-immune-checkpoint-inhibitor-therapy-braf-msimmr-pdl1-tmb) [tandfonline.com](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14737140.2025.2543454)ese tissue-agnostic biomarkers can qualify patients for immune checkpoint inhibitors like pembrolizumab upon disease progression.
