# What is the first-line treatment for EGFR exon 19 deletion metastatic NSCLC?

The first-line treatment landscape for metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) with an EGFR exon 19 deletion has evolved significantly, with several preferred options now supported by clinical guidelines (e.g., NCCN, ASCO) and recent FDA approvals [search-sourced]:

1. **Osimertinib Monotherapy**: Osimertinib, a third-generation EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI), has been established as a standard preferred first-line treatment option [search-sourced]. It demonstrated significant improvements in overall survival and progression-free survival compared to older-generation TKIs (erlotinib or gefitinib) and features strong central nervous system (CNS) penetration [search-sourced].
2. **Osimertinib plus Platinum-Based Chemotherapy**: Based on the phase 3 FLAURA2 trial, combining osimertinib with platinum doublet chemotherapy (such as pemetrexed and carboplatin/cisplatin) is an approved first-line strategy to delay resistance and enhance initial disease control [search-sourced].
3. **Amivantamab plus Lazertinib**: Based on the phase 3 MARIPOSA trial, this chemotherapy-free combination was recently approved as a first-line treatment for TKI-naïve patients with EGFR exon 19 deletions or L858R mutations [search-sourced]. 

**Mechanism of Action for Amivantamab**
Amivantamab is a fully human, low-fucose IgG1 bispecific monoclonal antibody [[what-is-the-mechanism-of-action-of-amivantamab]]. It is specifically engineered to target the extracellular domains of the Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor (EGFR) and the Mesenchymal-epithelial Transition Factor (MET) [[what-is-the-mechanism-of-action-of-amivantamab]]. It exerts anti-tumor effects through three concurrent but independent pathways [[what-is-the-mechanism-of-action-of-amivantamab]]:
* **Ligand Blocking**: It physically prevents natural ligands from attaching to EGFR and MET receptors, disrupting intracellular signaling pathways that promote tumor proliferation [[what-is-the-mechanism-of-action-of-amivantamab]].
* **Receptor Degradation**: The antibody binding induces the internalization, down-regulation, and degradation of the targeted receptors [[what-is-the-mechanism-of-action-of-amivantamab]].
* **Immune Cell-Directing Activity**: By being engineered with low levels of core fucose, amivantamab's Fc region binds more tightly to FcγRIIIa receptors on immune cells (such as Natural Killer cells) [[what-is-the-mechanism-of-action-of-amivantamab]]. This directs the patient's immune system to destroy tumor cells via processes like antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) and trogocytosis [[what-is-the-mechanism-of-action-of-amivantamab]].
