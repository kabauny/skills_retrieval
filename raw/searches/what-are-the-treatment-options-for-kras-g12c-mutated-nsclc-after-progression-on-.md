# What are the treatment options for KRAS G12C-mutated NSCLC after progression on first-line therapy?

For patients with KRAS G12C-mutated non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who experience disease progression on first-line therapy (which typically involves immunotherapy with or without platinum-based chemotherapy), there are targeted, conventional, and investigational options available [search-sourced] [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9012672/). 

**FDA-Approved KRAS G12C Inhibitors**
The historic conception of KRAS as an "undruggable" target changed with the discovery of targeted inhibitors that bind to the mutant protein [search-sourced] [ilcn.org](https://www.ilcn.org/breakthroughs-in-targeting-kras-g12c-mutations-in-nsclc/). For patients who have progressed on first-line therapies, there are currently two FDA-approved targeted agents:
*   **Sotorasib (Lumakras):** Sotorasib was the first targeted therapy to receive FDA accelerated approval for the treatment of adult patients with KRAS G12C-mutated locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC who have received at least one prior systemic therapy [search-sourced] [lungcancerresearchfoundation.org](https://www.lungcancerresearchfoundation.org/research/why-research/treatment-advances/). 
*   **Adagrasib (Krazati):** Adagrasib is a covalent KRAS G12C inhibitor that received FDA accelerated approval in December 2022 for the second-line treatment of advanced NSCLC harboring the mutation [search-sourced] [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11963745/). In the phase 3 KRYSTAL-12 trial, adagrasib significantly improved progression-free survival (PFS) compared to docetaxel in patients whose disease progressed on chemoimmunotherapy regimens [search-sourced] [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11963745/).

**Chemotherapy Options**
*   **Docetaxel +/- Ramucirumab:** Prior to the approval of targeted KRAS G12C inhibitors, the standard second-line treatments were docetaxel monotherapy or docetaxel administered in combination with the anti-angiogenic agent ramucirumab [search-sourced] [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9012672/). These traditional regimens remain viable options for patients, especially if targeted therapies are not tolerated or if disease progresses further [search-sourced] [aacrjournals.org](https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/28/8/1482/694149/FDA-Approval-Summary-Sotorasib-for-KRAS-G12C).

**Investigational and Next-Generation Therapies**
Several next-generation KRAS G12C inhibitors are currently in development to address resistance to first-generation drugs, combat brain metastases, and improve response duration [search-sourced] [ascopost.com](https://ascopost.com/news/april-2026/elisrasib-demonstrates-high-dcr-in-kras-g12c-nsclc/). 
*   **Elisrasib** has received FDA Fast Track and Breakthrough Therapy designations for use in the second-line setting for patients who are naive to KRAS G12C inhibitors [search-sourced] [aacr.org](https://www.aacr.org/about-the-aacr/newsroom/news-releases/next-generation-kras-g12c-inhibitor-elisrasib-elicited-promising-response-rates-in-patients-with-advanced-lung-cancer/).
*   Other covalently binding inhibitors such as **garsorasib**, **olomorasib**, and **divarasib** have shown promising efficacy and disease control rates in phase II clinical trials for patients who progressed on anti-PD-(L)1 therapy and platinum-based chemotherapy [search-sourced] [ilcn.org](https://www.ilcn.org/breakthroughs-in-targeting-kras-g12c-mutations-in-nsclc/). 
*   **Fulzerasib** is another novel drug in this targeted class that recently achieved regulatory approval in China for similar indications [search-sourced] [ilcn.org](https://www.ilcn.org/breakthroughs-in-targeting-kras-g12c-mutations-in-nsclc/).
