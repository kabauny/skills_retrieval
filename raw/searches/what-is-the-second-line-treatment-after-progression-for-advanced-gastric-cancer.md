# What is the second-line treatment after progression for advanced gastric cancer?

For patients with advanced or metastatic gastric cancer who experience disease progression after first-line therapy, the choice of second-line treatment is highly personalized and depends on factors such as the tumor's biomarker status (e.g., HER2, MSI-H/dMMR), prior therapies received, and the patient's overall performance status [search-sourced] [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2579675/) [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MN4NtHgJslE). 

According to major clinical guidelines (including NCCN and ESMO), standard second-line treatment options include:

*   **Preferred Combination Therapy:** The combination of ramucirumab (an anti-VEGFR2 monoclonal antibody) plus weekly paclitaxel is widely established as the primary, standard-of-care preferred regimen for most patients [search-sourced] [nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28260227/) [ascopubs.org](https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.2023.41.16_suppl.e16018) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11739642/).
*   **Alternative Chemotherapy:** The NCCN guidelines also list the FOLFIRI regimen (fluorouracil plus irinotecan) as a preferred second-line systemic treatment option [search-sourced] [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11739642/).
*   **Monotherapies:** For patients who cannot tolerate combination regimens, single-agent options are recommended. These include monotherapy with ramucirumab, paclitaxel, docetaxel, or irinotecan [search-sourced] [nccn.org](https://education.nccn.org/node/74936) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11739642/).
*   **HER2-Positive Disease:** For patients with HER2-positive advanced gastric cancer, recent clinical data (such as the DESTINY-Gastric04 trial) has established the targeted antibody-drug conjugate trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) as a standard second-line option, showing improved outcomes compared to ramucirumab plus paclitaxel [search-sourced] [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MN4NtHgJslE) [oncologynewscentral.com](https://www.oncologynewscentral.com/gastric-cancer/triplet-therapy-clears-early-hurdle-in-advanced-gastric-cancer).
*   **MSI-H/dMMR Tumors:** Patients with microsatellite instability-high (MSI-H) or mismatch repair-deficient (dMMR) tumors may be considered for treatment with immune checkpoint inhibitors if they have not already received immunotherapy during their first-line treatment [search-sourced] [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yAoyvPEXdj0) [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MN4NtHgJslE).
