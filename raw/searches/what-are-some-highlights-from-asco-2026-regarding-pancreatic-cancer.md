# What are some highlights from ASCO 2026 regarding pancreatic cancer? 

The 2026 American Society of Clinical Oncology (ASCO) Annual Meeting featured several major advancements in the treatment of pancreatic cancer, most notably a landmark trial that promises to change the standard of care for previously treated patients [curetoday.com](https://www.curetoday.com/view/top-5-takeaways-from-asco-2026-that-patients-should-know) [ecancer.org](https://ecancer.org/en/news/28376-asco-2026-daraxonrasib-doubles-median-overall-survival-in-metastatic-pancreatic-cancer). Here are the key highlights:

**1. Daraxonrasib: A "Grand Slam" Breakthrough (RASolute 302 Trial)**
* Daraxonrasib, a first-in-class, oral, multi-selective pan-RAS(ON) inhibitor, was the centerpiece of ASCO 2026's pancreatic cancer presentations, drawing a standing ovation during the plenary session [curetoday.com](https://www.curetoday.com/view/top-5-takeaways-from-asco-2026-that-patients-should-know) [diaceutics.com](https://www.diaceutics.com/articles/asco-2026-day-3-highlights-a-transformative-advance-in-pancreatic-cancer). 
* In the Phase 3 RASolute 302 trial, the drug was evaluated against standard chemotherapy as a second-line treatment for patients with metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) [curetoday.com](https://www.curetoday.com/view/top-5-takeaways-from-asco-2026-that-patients-should-know) [ecancer.org](https://ecancer.org/en/news/28376-asco-2026-daraxonrasib-doubles-median-overall-survival-in-metastatic-pancreatic-cancer).
* The drug reduced the risk of death by 60% and nearly doubled median overall survival (13.2 months with daraxonrasib vs. 6.7 months with standard chemotherapy) [curetoday.com](https://www.curetoday.com/view/top-5-takeaways-from-asco-2026-that-patients-should-know) [bioworld.com](https://www.bioworld.com/articles/731516-asco-2026-a-revolution-in-pancreatic-cancer?v=preview). 
* Progression-free survival also more than doubled (roughly 7.2–7.3 months vs. 3.5–3.6 months), and objective response rates nearly tripled (31–33% vs. 11–12%).
* Impo [diaceutics.com](https://www.diaceutics.com/articles/asco-2026-day-3-highlights-a-transformative-advance-in-pancreatic-cancer)rtantly, these survival benefits were observed across the overall patient population regardless of the specific RAS mutation they carried. * Bas [curetoday.com](https://www.curetoday.com/view/top-5-takeaways-from-asco-2026-that-patients-should-know) [diaceutics.com](https://www.diaceutics.com/articles/asco-2026-day-3-highlights-a-transformative-advance-in-pancreatic-cancer)ed on these results, researchers championed daraxonrasib as the new standard of care for second-line metastatic PDAC, and the FDA has already authorized an expanded access program for the drug as of May 1, 2026.
