# What is the second-line treatment after progression for advanced renal cell carcinoma?

The selection of a second-line treatment for advanced clear cell renal cell carcinoma (RCC) is heavily guided by the specific therapies a patient received in the first-line setting [search-sourced] [cloudfront.net](https://d56bochluxqnz.cloudfront.net/documents/pocket-guidelines/EAU-Pocket-on-Renal-Cell-Carcinoma-2025.pdf). Modern first-line treatments typically feature an immune checkpoint inhibitor—such as the PD-1 inhibitor [[pembrolizumab]]—in combination with a tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI), or a dual immunotherapy regimen [search-sourced].

* [cloudfront.net](https://d56bochluxqnz.cloudfront.net/documents/pocket-guidelines/EAU-Pocket-on-Renal-Cell-Carcinoma-2025.pdf)*For patients who have progressed after prior immunotherapy:**
*   **VEGF Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors (TKIs):** [[cabozantinib]] is a preferred standard-of-care monotherapy with robust data supporting its efficacy following progression on first-line PD-1–based combinations [search-sourced]. Othe [cloudfront.net](https://d56bochluxqnz.cloudfront.net/documents/pocket-guidelines/EAU-Pocket-on-Renal-Cell-Carcinoma-2025.pdf)r NCCN and ESMO guideline-recommended targeted options include axitinib, tivozanib, or the combination regimen of lenvatinib plus everolimus [search-sourced]. 
*   [nccn.org](https://www.nccn.org/patients/guidelines/content/PDF/kidney-patient.pdf) [esmo.org](https://www.esmo.org/guidelines/living-guidelines/esmo-living-guideline-renal-cell-carcinoma/advanced-and-metastatic-disease/systemic-treatment-for-advanced-and-metastatic-ccrcc/management-of-advanced-and-metastatic-disease-second-line-treatment-for-clear-cell-rcc) **HIF-2α Inhibitors:** [[belzutifan]] is a targeted HIF-2α inhibitor FDA-approved for advanced renal cell carcinoma [[belzutifan]]. It is specifically indicated and recommended in the second-line setting and beyond for patients whose disease has progressed following one to three lines of prior systemic therapies, including immune checkpoint inhibition and VEGF-targeted treatments (based on progression-free survival benefits seen in the LITESPARK-005 trial) [search-sourced].

**For [cancer.gov](https://www.cancer.gov/types/kidney/hp/kidney-treatment-pdq) patients who did NOT receive prior immunotherapy (IO-naïve):**
*   Guidelines strongly recommend introducing immunotherapy in the second line. Options include the PD-1 inhibitor nivolumab (which shares the same core mechanism of releasing T-cell exhaustion as [[pembrolizumab]]) as a single agent, or in combination with ipilimumab [search-sourced]. 
*   Ta [nccn.org](https://www.nccn.org/patients/guidelines/content/PDF/kidney-patient.pdf)rgeted regimens like [[cabozantinib]] monotherapy, lenvatinib plus [[pembrolizumab]], or axitinib plus [[pembrolizumab]] are also standard options for this population [search-sourced].
