# What is the biomarker testing to guide therapy for Hodgkin lymphoma?

Biomarker testing in Hodgkin lymphoma (HL) is crucial for classifying the specific subtype of the disease and guiding the use of precision cancer medicines, such as targeted therapies and immunotherapies [yourcancercare.com](https://www.yourcancercare.com/types-of-cancer/hodgkin-lymphoma/diagnosis-tests-for-hodgkin-lymphoma). These biomarkers are typically identified using immunohistochemistry (IHC) or genomic testing on an excisional lymph node biopsy [yourcancercare.com](https://www.yourcancercare.com/types-of-cancer/hodgkin-lymphoma/diagnosis-tests-for-hodgkin-lymphoma) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6310079/). 

The primary biomarkers tested to guide therapy in Hodgkin lymphoma include:

*   **CD30:** Almost all cases of classic Hodgkin lymphoma (cHL) are characterized by Hodgkin and Reed-Sternberg cells that strongly express the CD30 protein [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6310079/) [mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2673-6357/6/3/20). Testing positive for CD30 guides the use of CD30-directed therapies, most notably the antibody-drug conjugate brentuximab vedotin [yourcancercare.com](https://www.yourcancercare.com/types-of-cancer/hodgkin-lymphoma/diagnosis-tests-for-hodgkin-lymphoma) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6310079/) [ashpublications.org](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/131/15/1654/36819/Biology-of-classical-Hodgkin-lymphoma-implications).
*   **PD-L1 and PD-L2:** Cells in cHL often overexpress the programmed death ligands PD-L1 and PD-L2, which is frequently driven by an alteration/amplification in the 9p24.1 chromosome [thisislivingwithcancer.com](https://www.thisislivingwithcancer.com/content/biomarker-testing-in-cancer-treatment) [superpower.com](https://superpower.com/guides/non-hodgkin-lymphoma). Identifying the overexpression of these biomarkers informs the use of immune checkpoint inhibitors (PD-1 inhibitors) such as nivolumab and pembrolizumab, which block this pathway and help the immune system attack the cancer cells [superpower.com](https://superpower.com/guides/non-hodgkin-lymphoma) [ashpublications.org](https://ashpublications.org/blood/article/131/15/1654/36819/Biology-of-classical-Hodgkin-lymphoma-implications). 
*   **CD20:** While classical Hodgkin lymphoma is typically negative for CD20, this pan-B-cell marker is highly expressed in a rarer subtype known as nodular lymphocyte-predominant Hodgkin lymphoma (NLPHL) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6310079/) [nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9057666/). If biomarker testing reveals CD20 positivity, physicians can target the cancer using CD20-directed monoclonal antibodies like rituximab [yourcancercare.com](https://www.yourcancercare.com/types-of-cancer/hodgkin-lymphoma/diagnosis-tests-for-hodgkin-lymphoma) [superpower.com](https://superpower.com/guides/non-hodgkin-lymphoma).
